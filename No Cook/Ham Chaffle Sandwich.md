## Ingredients

- 2 pieces Keto Cheese Chaffle Bread
- Deli ham
- Mayo or butter (optional)

## Instructions

1. Lay deli ham between two pieces of chaffle bread.
2. Add mayo or butter if you have it (not required).

## Notes

- The fastest lunch of the keto week — pure assembly on the chaffle bread.
- From KetoFocus extreme-budget $40 keto week.
