## Ingredients

- 1 bell pepper (green is lowest in carbs; orange/red/yellow are sweeter)
- 4 oz cream cheese
- Your favorite sandwich fixings (deli meat, cheese, etc.)

## Instructions

1. Slice the bell pepper in half and scrape out the seeds and insides.
2. Spread cream cheese inside.
3. Add your favorite sandwich fixings.
4. Cut in half — now it's a portable, grain-free sandwich.

## Notes

- Keto / low-carb — the bell pepper replaces bread and adds a great crunch.
- Green bell pepper is the lowest-carb choice.
