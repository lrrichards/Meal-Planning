## Ingredients

- 6 eggs
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- (Standard deviled-egg add-ins as you like: mustard, salt, pepper, paprika)

## Instructions

1. Hard-boil and peel the eggs, then halve them and scoop the yolks into a bowl.
2. Mash the yolks with mayonnaise (and any mustard/seasoning you like) until smooth.
3. Pipe or spoon the filling back into the egg white halves.

## Notes

- Keto / low-carb. Easy enough to make a batch for dinner and keep leftovers (in the piping bag) for lunch the next day.
- Very cheap (just eggs and mayo), so tagged extreme-budget.
