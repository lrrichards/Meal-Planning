## Ingredients

- 1 bag pork rinds (or any low-carb chip)
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 2 cans tuna
- Optional: vegetables, bacon bits, seasonings

## Instructions

1. Mix the tuna with the mayonnaise (and any add-ins) in a bowl.
2. Use the pork rinds to scoop up the tuna dip, like chips and salsa.

## Notes

- Keto / low-carb snack or quick lunch — around $3 and 50 g of protein.
- Tuna pouches tear open, so no can opener needed on the go.
- Uses pork rinds (a keto convenience item), so not tagged extreme-budget.
