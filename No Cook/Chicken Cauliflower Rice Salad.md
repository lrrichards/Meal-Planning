## Ingredients

- 2 cups lettuce
- 1 packet shelf-stable cauliflower rice (pre-cooked)
- Cooked chicken (deli or pre-cooked)
- Tomatoes
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Sugar-free dressing

## Instructions

1. Add the lettuce to a large bowl.
2. Add the cauliflower rice (it's pre-cooked — use straight from the package, or heat it).
3. Add the cooked chicken and tomatoes.
4. Drizzle in 2 tbsp olive oil and your sugar-free dressing.
5. Mix together. Makes a large, filling salad for only a few net carbs.

## Notes

- Keto / low-carb — cauliflower rice adds bulk that fills you up without the carbs.
- **Contains tomatoes — not Evan-safe** (leave them out for him).
- Distinct from Keto Chicken Salad (that's a mayo-based chicken salad; this is a tossed green salad with cauliflower rice).
