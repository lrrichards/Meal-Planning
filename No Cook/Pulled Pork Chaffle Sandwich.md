## Ingredients

- 2 pieces Keto Cheese Chaffle Bread
- 2 cups cooked shredded pork
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese

## Instructions

1. Warm the shredded pork.
2. Pile it onto a piece of chaffle bread.
3. Optional: lay cheese over the pork and broil briefly to melt it.
4. Top with the second piece of chaffle bread.

## Notes

- If you have sugar-free barbecue sauce, mix some into the pork.
- From KetoFocus extreme-budget $40 keto week.
