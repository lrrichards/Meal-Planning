## Ingredients

- 2 cups cauliflower
- Roasted bell pepper and onion
- Chicken Schnitzel, sliced (reheated and re-crisped)
- 1 avocado
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- Optional: guacamole or salsa

## Instructions

1. Re-crisp the leftover chicken schnitzel in the air fryer or oven (about 10–12 minutes), then slice.
2. Portion Spanish cauliflower rice into a bowl as the base.
3. Top with the roasted bell pepper and onion and the sliced schnitzel.
4. Add avocado, sour cream, and a sprinkle of cheese (guacamole or salsa optional).

## Notes

- An assembly bowl built from meal-prepped components (cauliflower rice, roasted veg, chicken schnitzel). Keto / low-carb.
- Uses keto components, so not tagged extreme-budget.
