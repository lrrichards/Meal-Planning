## Ingredients

- 2 cups cooked shredded chicken
- 3 stalks celery
- 3 green onions
- Salt and pepper
- 2 tbsp mayonnaise
- 2 cups lettuce
- 1 tbsp lemon juice

## Instructions

1. Mix the shredded chicken with chopped celery and green onion. Season with salt and pepper.
2. Add a spoon of mayo if you have it (not required).
3. Serve two ways: spread on Keto Cheese Chaffle Bread for a chicken salad sandwich, or pile over chopped romaine for a salad.
4. For the salad, squeeze a little lemon juice on top to brighten it up.

## Notes

- Uses the pre-cooked shredded chicken base, so it comes together in minutes.
- From KetoFocus extreme-budget $40 keto week.
