## Ingredients

- 2 lb chicken breast
- 1/2 cup almond flour
- 2 eggs
- 1/4 cup grated parmesan cheese
- 1/2 cup pork panko
- 1 cup marinara sauce
- 1 cup mozzarella cheese
- 2 tbsp olive oil

## Instructions

1. Set up a keto breading using a combination of almond flour, egg, grated parmesan, and pork panko in place of breadcrumbs.
2. Coat the chicken in the breading.
3. Fry the chicken until the breading turns into a thick, crunchy crust and the chicken is cooked through.
4. Top with low-carb marinara sauce and melted mozzarella cheese.

## Notes

- Keto / low-carb chicken parm — pork panko and almond flour replace breadcrumbs, and there's no pasta.
- **Contains marinara (tomatoes) — contains tomatoes.** Use a low-carb marinara.
- Distinct from the other two chicken parms (the penne casserole and the breaded-cutlet-with-spaghetti) — this is the grain-free fried keto version.
- Uses almond flour and pork panko (pricier keto staples), so not tagged extreme-budget.
