## Ingredients

- 2 zero-carb tortillas
- Pepperoni slices
- Shredded cheese (mozzarella, cheddar, or a blend)

## Instructions

1. Heat a dry skillet over medium heat.
2. Lay one tortilla in the pan. Sprinkle an even layer of shredded cheese over half (or the whole tortilla if folding).
3. Add a layer of pepperoni over the cheese, then top with a little more cheese to help it hold together.
4. Fold the tortilla over (or top with a second tortilla).
5. Cook 2–3 minutes until the bottom is golden, then flip and cook the other side until the cheese is fully melted and both sides are crisp.
6. Slide onto a plate, let it set a moment, then cut into wedges.

## Notes

- Keto / low-carb thanks to the zero-carb tortillas — a fast meal or snack to have on hand.
- Add-ins if you want: a smear of pizza/marinara sauce inside (note: marinara makes it contains tomatoes), or sliced jalapeños for heat.
