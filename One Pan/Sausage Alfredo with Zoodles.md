## Ingredients

- 1 lb sausage
- 1/2 (4 oz) package spinach
- 1/2 block parmesan cheese, grated
- 1/2 block (4 oz) cream cheese
- 1 1/2 cups heavy cream (divided)
- 1 1/2 zucchini, made into noodles (zoodles)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- Italian seasoning, salt, pepper

## Instructions

1. Brown the sausage in a large skillet over medium heat. Season with about 1 tsp garlic powder.
2. Add the spinach and cook until wilted. Add a little Italian seasoning.
3. Stir in the cream cheese and let it melt into a creamy sauce.
4. Add 1 cup heavy cream and half the grated parmesan. Taste and adjust salt and pepper.
5. Add another 1/2 cup heavy cream and stir.
6. Add the zucchini noodles and mix. Cook 5–10 minutes until bubbly.
7. Serve with a sprinkle of parmesan and red pepper flakes if you like heat.

## Notes

- Use a spiralizer for the zoodles, or just cut the zucchini into noodle strips.
- Cream- and cheese-forward, so not tagged extreme-budget despite the cheap base.
- From a $70/week keto-on-a-budget week (2 people, 7 days).
