## Ingredients

- 4 keto tortillas
- 2 bratwurst
- 1 sweet onion (Vidalia), cut into long pieces
- 2 tbsp dijon mustard
- Olive oil
- Salt, pepper

## Instructions

1. Caramelize the onions: cook the long onion pieces in olive oil with salt and pepper, stirring occasionally, until soft and sweet. Set aside.
2. In the same pan, brown the brats on all sides, then cover and cook through (add a splash of water if needed).
3. Optional finish: air fry the brats at 390°F for 3 minutes to crisp them, then slice in half lengthwise.
4. Brown a keto tortilla in the pan.
5. Lay the brats on the tortilla, top with the caramelized onions, and add dijon mustard.
6. Fold it over and serve (extra brats on the side optional).

## Notes

- A keto tortilla fold-over with cheese-filled brats, sweet caramelized onions, and dijon.
- Uses keto tortillas (specialty), so not tagged extreme-budget.
