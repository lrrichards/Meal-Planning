## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground turkey (or any ground meat)
- 1 can refried beans
- Large burrito-sized flour tortillas
- Shredded cheese (optional)

*Not counted: water, taco seasoning, olive oil*

## Instructions

1. Add ground meat to a pan over medium heat. Break it up and cook through. (Frozen ground meat works — just takes a few extra minutes.)
2. Add a splash of water and taco seasoning. Stir and simmer 2 minutes.
3. Add the entire can of refried beans directly to the pan. Stir to combine with the meat. Cook together a few minutes until heated through.
4. Fill a large flour tortilla with the meat and bean mixture. Add cheese or other toppings if desired. Roll up.
5. Heat a little olive oil in the same pan over medium heat. Crisp the burrito on each side until golden.
6. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Cooking the refried beans with the meat softens the texture and gives it a restaurant-style quality.
- Crisping the tortilla in oil is what elevates this from sad to great — don't skip it.
- Can go from frozen ground meat to dinner in about 20 minutes.
- Only 3 core ingredients.
