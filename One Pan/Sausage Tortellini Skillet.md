## Ingredients

- 1 lb sweet Italian sausage
- 18 oz cheese tortellini (use 9 oz for 3–4 people)
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese (more if you like)
- 1 jar marinara sauce
- 1 can chicken broth (the small can)
- Garlic, salt, pepper, oregano

## Instructions

1. Cook the sausage in a large skillet. Don't drain the grease — it flavors the onions.
2. Add the chopped onion and cook until see-through and fragrant.
3. Season with garlic, salt, pepper, and oregano.
4. Pour in the chicken broth and marinara. Mix well and bring to a simmer.
5. Submerge the tortellini and cook 4–5 minutes until tender.
6. Stir in the spinach and let it cook down.
7. Top with mozzarella, let it melt, and serve.

## Notes

- A true one-skillet meal; 18 oz tortellini feeds a family of six.
- Distinct from the existing meatless tortellini recipes (this one has sausage and a marinara base, cooked in one skillet).
- "Poor man's comfort food" — cheap enough (~$2–3/serving) for the extreme-budget tag.
