## Ingredients

- 1–1½ lbs chicken breast (or tenderloins)
- ⅓ cup chicken broth
- 2 tbsp low sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- ⅓ cup honey

*Not counted: olive oil, salt, pepper, butter (1 tbsp), minced garlic (1 tbsp)*

**To serve:** White rice, green beans, sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Season chicken on both sides with salt and pepper.
2. Cook chicken about 4 minutes per side — it doesn't need to be fully cooked through at this point.
3. While chicken cooks, whisk together chicken broth, soy sauce, rice vinegar, and honey in a small bowl.
4. Add 1 tbsp butter and 1 tbsp minced garlic to the pan. Stir for about 15 seconds until fragrant.
5. Pour sauce into the pan. Stir and simmer for a couple of minutes.
6. Optional: remove chicken, slice into bite-sized pieces, return to pan. Simmer another 2 minutes until sauce slightly thickens and chicken is cooked through.
7. Serve over rice with green beans. Spoon plenty of sauce over everything. Top with sesame seeds if desired.

## Notes

- Cutting the chicken into smaller pieces after the first sear gets more sauce on each piece — the kids love it this way.
- Sauce thickens as it simmers — don't rush past this step.
- Minute rice works great as the side when time is short.
- Sweet-savory sauce is the whole point of this dish — make sure to pour it generously over the rice too.
- Note: different from the Freezer Meal and Slow Cooker versions of honey garlic chicken — this is a 15-minute stovetop recipe.
