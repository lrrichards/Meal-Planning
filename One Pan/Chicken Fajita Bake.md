## Ingredients

- Chicken thighs
- Bell peppers, mixed colors, sliced
- 1 sweet onion, sliced
- Three types of cheese, shredded (your choice)
- Olive oil
- Fajita seasoning (homemade is better — chili powder, cumin, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper)

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 375°F. Slice the peppers and onion.
2. Drizzle olive oil in an oven-safe pan and sizzle the chicken thighs to start a sear. Season the bottoms first, then flip bare-side up and season the tops generously — don't be shy, blandness is the enemy of fajitas.
3. Top the chicken with the sliced peppers and onion.
4. Sprinkle cheese over the top — a generous amount.
5. Bake 35–45 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the cheese is melted and bubbly.

## Notes

- Keto / low-carb — no tortillas, just chicken, peppers, onion, and cheese.
- Evan-safe (no tomatoes).
- Make your own fajita seasoning to control the flavor.
- Trader Joe's "poor man's comfort food." Uses three cheeses, so left off the extreme-budget tag — say the word to add it.
