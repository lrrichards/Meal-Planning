## Ingredients

- 4 keto tortillas
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1/2 can sloppy joe sauce
- 1 tbsp olive oil

## Instructions

1. Brown the ground beef, then stir in the keto Manwich (sloppy joe) sauce.
2. Heat a little avocado oil in a pan and lay in a keto tortilla to brown.
3. Spread half the tortilla with the Manwich beef.
4. Fold the tortilla over the filling.
5. Brown both sides until golden, tucking in any filling that escapes.

## Notes

- One of several keto tortilla fold-overs — a fast keto meal in about 10 minutes.
- **Manwich/sloppy joe sauce is tomato-based — contains tomatoes.**
- Uses keto tortillas (specialty), so not tagged extreme-budget.
