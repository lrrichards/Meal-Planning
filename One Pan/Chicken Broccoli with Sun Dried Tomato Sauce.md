## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs chicken breast, sliced or cubed
- 2–3 cups frozen broccoli florets, thawed
- ¼ cup sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed, chopped
- ½ cup chicken broth
- ¾ cup heavy cream
- 1 cup shredded Colby Jack cheese

*Not counted: olive oil, Italian seasoning, salt, pepper, garlic powder, red pepper flakes*

## Instructions

1. Season chicken with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning.
2. Heat olive oil (or oil from the sun-dried tomato jar) in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Cook chicken until browned and cooked through, about 5–7 minutes. Remove and set aside.
4. Add sun-dried tomatoes to the pan and cook 1 minute to release their oil.
5. Pour in chicken broth and heavy cream. Season with garlic powder and red pepper flakes. Bring to a simmer.
6. Add thawed broccoli to the sauce. Simmer 3–4 minutes until broccoli is heated through.
7. Return chicken to pan. Top with Colby Jack cheese.
8. Cover 2–3 minutes until cheese is melted, or broil briefly if using an oven-safe pan.

## Notes

- Use the oil from the sun-dried tomato jar instead of plain olive oil — it's packed with flavor.
- Thaw broccoli before adding so it heats through quickly without watering down the sauce.
- Sun-dried tomatoes add a concentrated, slightly sweet depth that fresh tomatoes can't replicate.
- This is a close relative of Marry Me Chicken — rich, creamy, and deeply savory.
