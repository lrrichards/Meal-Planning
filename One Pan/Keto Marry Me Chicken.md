## Ingredients

- 2 lb chicken breast
- Onion powder, garlic powder, smoked paprika, oregano, chili powder, salt, pepper
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 cup half and half
- 1 cup broth
- 1/4 cup parmesan cheese
- 1/4 cup sun-dried tomatoes
- Fresh basil
- Roasted cauliflower, to serve (in place of pasta)

## Instructions

1. Season the chicken with onion powder, garlic powder, smoked paprika, oregano, chili powder, salt, and pepper. Coat with a little avocado oil.
2. Sear the chicken in a pan until it has a nice crisp and is cooked through. Set aside.
3. In the same pan, sauté the diced onion and fresh garlic. Add a little tomato paste.
4. Add half and half for creaminess, then the bone broth (adds protein).
5. Stir in parmesan, salt, and pepper. Add the sun-dried tomatoes and basil.
6. Cover and simmer. Return the chicken to the sauce.
7. Serve over roasted cauliflower to keep it low carb.

## Notes

- A keto / low-carb take on marry me chicken — roasted cauliflower stands in for pasta.
- **Contains tomato paste and sun-dried tomatoes — contains tomatoes.**
- Distinct from Marry Me Chicken Over Rice — this is the keto version over cauliflower.
- Uses sun-dried tomatoes (a pricier ingredient), so not tagged extreme-budget.
