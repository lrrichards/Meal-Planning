## Ingredients

- 1.5 lb steak
- 3 bell peppers
- 1 onion
- Large flour tortillas
- 6 tostada shells
- Queso
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- Chili lime seasoning, fajita seasoning, garlic salt, olive oil

## Instructions

1. Season the steak (chili lime + garlic salt, or fajita seasoning). Marinate 30 minutes if you have time; otherwise season and cook right away.
2. Get a pan hot with olive oil. Sear the steak about 2 minutes per side for a nice char — don't overcook. Rest it, then slice/chop.
3. In the same pan with the steak's rendered grease, cook the sliced peppers and onion until snappy with some char (not soggy). Wipe the pan clean afterward.
4. Build each crunch wrap: lay out a flour tortilla, spoon queso in the center, then add steak, fajita vegetables, and shredded cheese.
5. Set a tostada shell on top, then spread on sour cream (and guac if you want).
6. Fold the edges of the tortilla up and over the center to seal into a hexagon.
7. Place open-side-down in the hot pan and griddle, then flip to crisp both sides.

## Notes

- Distinct from Steak Fajita Quesadillas — this is a layered, sealed crunch wrap with queso, a tostada, and sour cream inside.
- Make extra steak for leftovers.
- Not keto (tortilla + tostada).
- Cinco de Mayo "poor man's comfort food."
