## Ingredients

- 1 lb Italian sausage, casing removed
- 1 white onion, diced
- ¾ cup heavy cream
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- 1 lb potato gnocchi (shelf stable or refrigerated)
- 1 cup mozzarella cheese, shredded

*Not counted: olive oil, Italian seasoning, salt, pepper, garlic powder, red pepper flakes*

## Instructions

1. Cook sausage in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up. Drain excess fat.
2. Add diced onion to the same pan. Cook 3–4 minutes until softened.
3. Pour in diced tomatoes. Season with Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes. Simmer 5 minutes.
4. Add heavy cream. Stir to combine.
5. Add gnocchi directly to the skillet. Stir to coat with sauce.
6. Cover and cook on medium-low for 5–7 minutes until gnocchi is tender.
7. Stir in fresh spinach until wilted. Top with mozzarella cheese.
8. Cover briefly to melt cheese, or broil 2–3 minutes if using an oven-safe pan.

## Notes

- Shelf-stable gnocchi works perfectly here — no need to boil it separately first.
- The cream and diced tomato together create a quick rosa sauce.
- Spinach wilts fast — stir it in at the very end so it stays vibrant green.
- Hot Italian sausage makes this dish — mild works for a family-friendly version.
