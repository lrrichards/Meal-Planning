## Ingredients

- 1/2 lb ground beef
- 1/2 onion, diced
- Shredded cheddar (or mozzarella) cheese
- 4 slices bacon
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1 tomato
- 1 cup lettuce
- 2 oz dill pickle
- Onion powder, garlic salt, salt, pepper

## Instructions

1. Sauté the diced onion in olive oil over medium heat until softened.
2. Add the ground beef and brown. Season with 1–2 tsp onion powder, about the same of garlic salt, plus salt and fresh pepper.
3. Build the bowl: shredded lettuce (or just the meat) on the bottom, then the seasoned beef.
4. Top with shredded cheese, a plop of sour cream, diced tomato, bacon, and pickle chips.

## Notes

- Everything-burger-but-the-bun in a bowl. Customize toppings to what's in the fridge.
- From a $70/week keto-on-a-budget week (2 people, 7 days).
