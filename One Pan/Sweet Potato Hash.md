## Ingredients

- 1 sweet potato, peeled and diced
- 2 large russet potatoes, peeled and diced
- 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 cup frozen spinach
- 3/4 cup water
- Shredded cheese for topping (Parmesan, Colby Jack, or cheddar)

*Not counted: olive oil, salt, pepper, onion powder, garlic powder, paprika*

## Instructions

1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add diced onion and season with salt, pepper, onion powder, garlic powder, and paprika. Cook about 5 minutes until softened.
2. Add black beans, sweet potato, and russet potatoes. Stir to combine.
3. Add frozen spinach and 3/4 cup water. Stir well.
4. Cover with a lid (or a sheet pan if no lid available) and simmer about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until potatoes are tender.
5. Serve topped with shredded cheese.

## Notes

- A sheet pan works perfectly as a lid if your pan doesn't have one.
- Nutrient-dense — sweet potato and spinach add significant vitamins and minerals.
- Top with anything you like: cheese, sour cream, hot sauce, green onions.
