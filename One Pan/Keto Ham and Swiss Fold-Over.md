## Ingredients

- 4 keto tortillas
- 4 oz Swiss cheese
- 2 tbsp heavy cream
- A pinch of nutmeg
- 4 oz deli ham
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, pepper

## Instructions

1. Make a quick cheese sauce: pile the shredded swiss into a pot, add a little heavy cream and a pinch of nutmeg. Whisk over heat, adding cream until you get a slightly thin sauce (it tightens as it cooks). Season with salt and pepper.
2. Chop the shaved deli ham into small pieces.
3. Brown a keto tortilla in a little avocado oil.
4. Spread cheese sauce over the tortilla, layer on the ham, and add a little more cheese sauce on top.
5. Fold it over and press down — the cheese glues it together. Brown both sides.

## Notes

- A keto tortilla fold-over with a swiss "fondue" cheese sauce and ham.
- Uses keto tortillas (specialty), so not tagged extreme-budget.
