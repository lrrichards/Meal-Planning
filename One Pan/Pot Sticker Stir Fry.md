## Ingredients

- 1 bag frozen pot stickers
- 1 bag frozen Asian vegetable medley (with its sauce packet)
- Soy sauce
- Garlic powder or minced garlic
- Oil
- Rice, to serve

## Instructions

1. Start the rice (about a 1:1 ratio in a rice cooker).
2. In a bowl, mix the medley's sauce packet with a little extra soy sauce and garlic.
3. Heat oil in a hot skillet. Add the frozen pot stickers. When the bottoms are golden and crispy, add 1/4 cup water and steam 4 minutes.
4. Once the water is gone, set the pot stickers aside.
5. Add more oil and stir fry the vegetables a few minutes. Add a little more garlic.
6. Return the pot stickers to the pan, pour in the sauce, and toss/stir about a minute.
7. Serve over rice.

## Notes

- Swaps pot stickers in for the usual protein in a stir fry.
- Not keto (pot stickers + rice).
- Evan-safe (no tomatoes).
- Distinct from Pot Sticker Chicken Alfredo Carbonara (that's a creamy pasta dish) — this is an Asian stir fry.
- "Poor man's comfort food."
