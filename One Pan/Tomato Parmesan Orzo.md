## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs chicken breast, cubed
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1 cup orzo, uncooked
- 1 cup tomato sauce
- 1½ cups chicken broth
- ½ cup heavy cream
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- ½ cup parmesan cheese, shredded

*Not counted: olive oil, Italian seasoning, salt, pepper, garlic powder, red pepper flakes*

## Instructions

1. Season cubed chicken with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear chicken cubes 3–4 minutes per side until browned. Remove and set aside.
3. Add diced onion to the same pan. Cook 3 minutes until softened.
4. Add orzo, toasting 1–2 minutes in the dry pan.
5. Pour in tomato sauce and chicken broth. Season with garlic powder, red pepper flakes, and more salt and pepper. Stir.
6. Bring to a simmer. Cook uncovered for 8–10 minutes, stirring often, until orzo is tender and liquid is mostly absorbed.
7. Stir in heavy cream and return chicken to pan.
8. Add fresh spinach and stir until wilted. Top with parmesan cheese and serve.

## Notes

- Sear the chicken separately first — it develops a crust and the fond from searing flavors the whole dish.
- Toast the orzo in the dry pan before adding liquid — adds a subtle nuttiness.
- Stir frequently once broth is added — orzo sticks easily as it absorbs.
- The cream added at the end keeps the sauce silky without making it heavy.
