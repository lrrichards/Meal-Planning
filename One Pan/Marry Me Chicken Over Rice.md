## Ingredients

- 2 chicken breasts (sliced thin horizontally — makes 4 thin pieces)
- 1/3 cup sun-dried tomatoes (in oil)
- 3/4 cup chicken broth
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1/3 cup fresh basil, chopped
- 2 cups fresh spinach (optional)
- White rice for serving

*Not counted: butter, garlic, salt, pepper, Italian seasoning*

## Instructions

1. Season chicken generously on both sides with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning.
2. Melt 2 tbsp butter in a pan over medium heat. Cook chicken about 5 minutes per side until cooked through. Remove to a plate.
3. In the same pan, add 1 tbsp minced garlic and sun-dried tomatoes. Cook together 2 minutes.
4. Add chicken broth and heavy cream. Stir and simmer about 5 minutes until the sauce thickens.
5. Add basil and spinach (if using). Stir until spinach wilts.
6. Return chicken and any plate juices back to the pan.
7. Serve over white rice (or egg noodles).

## Notes

- The pan drippings from the chicken add huge flavor to the sauce — don't skip scraping the pan.
- Also works great over egg noodles.
- Reheats well — sauce stays rich.
