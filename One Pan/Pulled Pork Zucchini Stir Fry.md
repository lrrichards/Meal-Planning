## Ingredients

- Cooked shredded pork (see Slow Cooker Pulled Pork)
- 1 zucchini, sliced
- 1/2 yellow onion, sliced
- Celery, chopped
- Leftover spinach
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat a skillet with a little oil over medium-high heat.
2. Add the zucchini, onion, and celery. Stir fry until tender-crisp.
3. Add the shredded pork and any leftover spinach. Stir fry until everything is heated through.
4. Season with salt and pepper.

## Notes

- A clean-out-the-fridge dinner — use whatever leftover keto vegetables you have.
- From KetoFocus extreme-budget $40 keto week.
