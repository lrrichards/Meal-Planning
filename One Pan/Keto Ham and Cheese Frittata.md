## Ingredients

- 12 eggs
- 1 oz cream cheese, softened
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp pepper
- 3 oz shredded cheddar cheese
- 2 green onions, sliced
- Deli ham, diced (about 1/2 cup)

## Instructions

1. Whisk the eggs with the softened cream cheese, salt, pepper, and shredded cheddar.
2. In an oven-safe skillet, cook the green onion and diced ham until heated through and fragrant.
3. Pour the egg mixture over the ham and onion. Stir gently on and off for about 2 minutes so the bottom starts to scramble and set.
4. Let it settle and cook undisturbed 30 seconds.
5. Transfer the skillet to the oven under the broiler for about 3 minutes, until golden brown on top.
6. Rest 5 minutes before slicing.

## Notes

- Meal-prep friendly — makes enough for two breakfasts for a small family.
