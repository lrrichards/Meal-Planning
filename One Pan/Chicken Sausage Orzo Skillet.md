## Ingredients

- 12 oz chicken sausage, sliced into rounds
- 2–3 cups broccoli florets
- 1 cup orzo, uncooked
- 2½ cups chicken broth
- ½ cup parmesan cheese, shredded

*Not counted: olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, red pepper flakes*

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add sliced chicken sausage.
2. Sear sausage rounds undisturbed for 2–3 minutes per side until browned. Remove from pan and set aside.
3. Add broccoli to the same pan. Cook 3–4 minutes until beginning to char slightly. Remove and set aside.
4. Add orzo to pan, toasting it dry for 1–2 minutes until lightly golden.
5. Pour in chicken broth. Season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and red pepper flakes. Bring to a simmer.
6. Cook uncovered for 8–10 minutes, stirring frequently, until orzo is tender and most broth is absorbed.
7. Return sausage and broccoli to the pan. Stir in parmesan cheese. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Searing the sausage first is key — the browned crust adds flavor the whole dish benefits from.
- Toasting the orzo in the dry pan before adding broth gives it a slightly nutty flavor.
- Stir the orzo often once broth is added — it sticks easily as the liquid reduces.
- Add a squeeze of lemon at the end to brighten the whole dish.
