## Ingredients

- Chicken
- 18 eggs
- Mushrooms, chopped
- Onions, chopped
- Cabbage, chopped
- Frozen cauliflower
- Frozen broccoli
- Oil
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Chop the mushrooms, onions, and cabbage. Whisk the 18 eggs.
2. Sear the chicken in oil until no pink remains. Set aside.
3. Scramble the eggs until fluffy. Set aside.
4. Cook the vegetables in two batches: first the mushrooms, onions, and some cabbage; then the rest of the cabbage with the chicken, adding the frozen cauliflower and broccoli.
5. Combine everything in one big bowl and portion into meal-prep containers.
6. Keep a couple in the fridge and freeze the rest.

## Notes

- Keto / low-carb meal prep — makes about 7 bowls at roughly 890 calories each.
- Cheap proteins (eggs, chicken) and vegetables; freezes well.
