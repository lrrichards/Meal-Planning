## Ingredients

- Low-carb noodles (shirataki / Palmini-style, about 1 net carb per serving)
- Butter
- Parsley
- Salt

## Instructions

1. Prepare the low-carb noodles per the package (rinse and heat).
2. Toss with butter, parsley, and salt until coated and warm.

## Notes

- A simple keto / low-carb pasta side — these noodles aren't rubbery and taste good mixed with butter and seasonings.
- A side dish; uses specialty low-carb noodles, so not tagged extreme-budget.
