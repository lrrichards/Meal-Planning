## Ingredients

- 1 bag tortilla chips
- 1 package breakfast sausage
- 6 slices bacon
- 1 cup marble cheese, shredded
- 6 eggs
- Pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to about 375°F. Cook the breakfast sausage in an oven-safe skillet, then remove the meats and wipe the pan clean.
2. Layer the bottom of the skillet with tortilla chips.
3. Sprinkle on half the sausage and bacon, then a layer of shredded cheese.
4. Add another layer of tortilla chips, then more cheese.
5. Make six divots in the top and crack an egg into each well.
6. Bake 20–25 minutes, until the eggs are set to your liking (gooey yolks are great here).
7. Finish with a little pepper.

## Notes

- Breakfast-for-dinner — works for breakfast, lunch, or dinner.
- Not keto (tortilla chips).
- Walmart "poor man's comfort food," cheap proteins (sausage, bacon, eggs).
