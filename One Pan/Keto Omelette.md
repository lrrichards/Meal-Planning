## Ingredients

- 2–3 eggs
- Deli ham, diced
- Shredded cheddar cheese
- Any leftover vegetables (green onion, spinach, etc.)
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Whisk the eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper.
2. Pour into a greased skillet over medium heat and let the bottom set.
3. When mostly set, add diced ham, cheese, and any leftover veg to one half.
4. Fold the omelette over the filling and cook another minute until the cheese melts.

## Notes

- Built to use up leftover eggs, deli ham, and odds-and-ends vegetables from the week.
- From KetoFocus extreme-budget $40 keto week.
