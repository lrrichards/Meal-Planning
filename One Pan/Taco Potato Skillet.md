## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground turkey (or ground chicken or ground beef)
- 1 can diced tomatoes with green chilies (Rotel)
- 1/2 bag frozen diced potatoes (or Potatoes O'Brien with onions and peppers)
- 1/4 cup water
- Shredded cheese and sour cream for topping (optional)

*Not counted: taco seasoning*

## Instructions

1. Brown ground turkey in a large pan over medium heat, breaking it up as it cooks.
2. Add Rotel, water, and taco seasoning. Stir to combine.
3. Add frozen diced potatoes. Stir.
4. Cover and cook on medium for about 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until potatoes are cooked through. Add a splash more water if potatoes start sticking.
5. Serve topped with cheese and sour cream.

## Notes

- Potatoes O'Brien (with onions and peppers) add extra flavor if your store carries them.
- If potatoes stick, add a small splash of water and stir immediately.
- Taco seasoning does all the flavor work — no other spices needed.
- Only 3 core ingredients.
