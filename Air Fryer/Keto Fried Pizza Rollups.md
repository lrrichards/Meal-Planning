## Ingredients

- 4 slices keto bread
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
- Pepperoni, cut into strips
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp parsley
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Marinara sauce, for dipping (optional)

## Instructions

1. Cut the crust off a slice of keto bread. Roll it flat with a rolling pin (put a damp paper towel under the parchment to keep it from sliding).
2. Top the flattened bread with a few tablespoons of shredded mozzarella and a few strips of pepperoni.
3. Roll it up.
4. Melt 2 tbsp butter with 1/4 tsp garlic powder and 1/4 tsp parsley in the microwave (30-second intervals). Brush over each roll.
5. Air fry at 350°F for 5–7 minutes, or pan-fry in a little avocado oil until crispy outside and the cheese is melted.
6. Dip in marinara sauce.
