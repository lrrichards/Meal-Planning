## Ingredients

- 1 whole rotisserie chicken, shredded (about 3 cups)
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 cup salsa
- 1½ cups shredded Colby Jack or Mexican-style cheese (plus more for layers)
- 6 flour tortillas

*Not counted: taco seasoning (1½ tbsp), oil spray, lime juice*

**To serve:** Extra salsa, sour cream, cilantro, lime

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. In a large bowl, combine shredded chicken, black beans, sour cream, salsa, 1½ cups cheese, juice of ½ lime, and 1½ tbsp taco seasoning. Stir well.
3. Spray a sheet pan with oil spray. Lay tortillas across the pan, slightly overlapping, to cover the surface.
4. Sprinkle cheese over the tortillas, then spread the filling evenly on top.
5. Lay a tortilla in the center and fold in the outer tortilla edges over the top.
6. Spray the tops of the tortillas with oil spray.
7. Place a second sheet pan directly on top (this keeps everything pressed flat and crisps the top).
8. Bake at 400°F for about 15–18 minutes.
9. Remove top sheet pan. Switch oven to broil and broil for 2 minutes until the top is golden.
10. Cut with a pizza cutter. Top with cilantro, salsa, sour cream, and lime.

## Notes

- Chilled (day-old) rotisserie chickens at Walmart are around $4 and work perfectly here.
- The second sheet pan on top is the trick — it keeps the quesadilla flat and crisps both sides.
- Pizza cutter makes slicing effortless.
- Swap chicken for ground beef if that's what you have on hand.
- Add corn, Rotel, or jalapeños to the filling to customize.
- Kid-friendly — the whole thing can be adapted to family preferences.
