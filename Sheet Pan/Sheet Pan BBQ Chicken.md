## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs chicken breasts
- 1 lb gold (Yukon gold) potatoes, cubed
- 2 ears corn on the cob, cut into rounds
- ¼ cup barbecue sauce
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tbsp honey

*Not counted: olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika, chili powder, non-stick spray*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Line a large sheet pan with foil and spray with non-stick spray.
2. In a small bowl, whisk together barbecue sauce, lime juice, and honey to make the glaze.
3. Toss cubed potatoes and corn rounds with olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder, and paprika. Spread on sheet pan.
4. Nestle chicken breasts among the vegetables. Season chicken with salt, pepper, and chili powder.
5. Brush chicken generously with the barbecue glaze.
6. Bake at 375°F for 30–35 minutes until chicken reaches 165°F internal temperature and potatoes are tender.
7. Brush chicken with a final coat of glaze in the last 5 minutes.

## Notes

- Lime juice in the glaze brightens up what could be a heavy BBQ flavor.
- Corn on the cob cut into rounds makes it easy to eat — no silverware needed for the corn.
- Gold potatoes cook faster and creamier than russets — a better choice for sheet pan timing.
- Check the chicken at 30 minutes with a thermometer — ovens vary.
