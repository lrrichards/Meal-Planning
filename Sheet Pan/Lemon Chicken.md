## Ingredients

- Chicken (breasts or thighs)
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp paprika
- 1 tsp thyme
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp pepper
- Juice of 1 lemon (plus 1 more lemon, sliced into wedges)
- About 1 cup olive or avocado oil
- 1 onion, sliced
- Optional side: California blend vegetables and carrots

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, mix the salt, paprika, thyme, garlic powder, pepper, the juice of 1 lemon, and about 1 cup oil. Stir well (the oil wants to separate).
2. Dunk the chicken in the marinade and let it sit a few minutes.
3. Heat oven to 375°F. Spray a sheet pan and lay out the chicken.
4. Top with sliced onion and lemon wedges.
5. Bake 35–45 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through.
6. Roast vegetables (California blend, carrots) on a second sheet pan if you like.

## Notes

- Keto / low-carb and very tender.
- Budget keto — simple chicken, lemon, and oil.
