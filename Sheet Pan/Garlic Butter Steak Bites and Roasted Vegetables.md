## Ingredients

- 2 lbs London broil, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 12 oz frozen broccoli florets
- 1 red onion, sliced
- 1½ lbs small gold potatoes

*Not counted: melted butter (2½ tbsp), minced garlic (1 tbsp), olive oil, salt, pepper, paprika, rosemary, oregano, thyme*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Line sheet pan with parchment paper.
2. In a large bowl, toss cut steak with 2½ tbsp melted butter, 1 tbsp minced garlic, salt, pepper, paprika, rosemary, oregano, and thyme.
3. Spread steak onto one side of the sheet pan.
4. In the same bowl, add broccoli, sliced red onion, and gold potatoes. Drizzle with 3–4 tbsp olive oil. Season with salt, pepper, paprika, oregano, thyme, and rosemary. Toss with tongs.
5. Spread vegetables on the other side of the sheet pan.
6. Bake at 400°F for 25 minutes.
7. Switch oven to broil for 2–3 minutes to caramelize and crisp the edges of the steak.
8. Check potatoes for fork tenderness. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- London broil is significantly cheaper per pound than most steak cuts and comes out tender in the oven.
- If you prefer medium-rare, bake the vegetables first for 10–15 minutes, then add the steak and cook for less time.
- Broiling at the end is what gives the steak that caramelized crust — don't skip it.
- Works great as meal prep — packs well in food containers for lunch.
- Use tongs when tossing veggies — much easier than a spoon.
