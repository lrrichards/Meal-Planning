## Ingredients

- 1 preseasoned pork tenderloin (caramelized onion, roasted garlic herb, or applewood smoked bacon flavors all work well)
- 1 bundle asparagus, trimmed
- 1 bag Brussels sprouts, halved
- 1 bag small potatoes (gold or Idaho), halved
- Juice of ½ lemon (or a splash of bottled lemon juice)

*Not counted: olive oil, salt, pepper, and any dry seasonings*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. In a large bowl, combine asparagus, Brussels sprouts, and halved potatoes.
3. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt, pepper, and any seasonings you like. Squeeze lemon juice over top. Toss well to coat everything.
4. Spread vegetables onto the sheet pan.
5. Place pork tenderloin in the center of the sheet pan.
6. Bake at 400°F for 40–45 minutes until pork is cooked through and vegetables are fork tender.
7. Slice pork and serve alongside the vegetables.

## Notes

- Preseasoned pork tenderloins are sold at most grocery stores — caramelized onion and roasted garlic herb are great choices.
- Halved pre-packaged Brussels sprouts save prep time.
- Lemon juice adds brightness — even bottled works fine.
- Leftovers pack well for lunches in food storage containers.
- Swap any of the vegetables for whatever you have on hand.
