## Ingredients

- Chicken tenderloins (remove the tendon)
- Italian-style panko breadcrumbs (or shake-and-bake style coating)
- Marinara sauce
- Mozzarella cheese (string cheese works, or shredded)
- Spaghetti
- Parmesan cheese
- Butter
- Italian seasoning, garlic salt, salt, pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 400°F. Remove the tendon from the chicken tenderloins.
2. Coat the chicken in the Italian panko crumbs (about 1 cup), seasoning generously.
3. Lay the breaded chicken on a baking sheet and bake 18 minutes.
4. Take it out, spoon marinara over each piece, and top with mozzarella.
5. Return to the oven a few more minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
6. Meanwhile, boil the spaghetti (garlic-salt the water) about 8 minutes, then drain.
7. Toss the spaghetti with a little butter and parmesan.
8. Serve the baked chicken parm over the spaghetti.

## Notes

- A baked (not fried) chicken parm — lighter than the classic, still hits the spot.
- **Contains marinara (tomatoes) — not Evan-safe.**
- Not keto (panko + spaghetti).
- Distinct from the dump-and-bake Chicken Parmesan Casserole — this is breaded cutlets on a sheet pan with separate spaghetti.
- Follower (Victoria) recipe, "poor man's comfort food."
