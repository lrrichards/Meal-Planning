## Ingredients

- 1/2 bag pierogis (half of a 4 lb bag feeds a family of six)
- 14 oz kielbasa
- Colorful bell peppers, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- Olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1/2 cup sour cream

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 400°F.
2. Spread the pierogis, sliced kielbasa, peppers, and onion on a sheet pan.
3. Drizzle with olive oil and season with garlic salt and pepper. Toss to coat.
4. Bake 20–25 minutes until everything is tasty and browned.
5. Serve with a dollop of sour cream.

## Notes

- True one-sheet-pan dinner; makes a lot of leftovers.
- Not keto (pierogis).
- "Poor man's comfort food," cheap and super easy.
