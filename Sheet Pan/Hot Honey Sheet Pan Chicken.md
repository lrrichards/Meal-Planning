## Ingredients

### Sheet Pan
- 2 sweet potatoes, peeled and diced
- 1 red onion, cut into pieces
- 1½ lbs chicken breast (or tenderloins)

### Hot Honey Sauce
- Honey (generous amount — enough to drizzle twice)
- A few dashes of hot sauce (Frank's or any brand)
- 1 tbsp butter, melted
- Juice of ½ lemon
- Crushed red pepper flakes to taste

*Not counted: olive oil, salt, pepper, crushed red pepper flakes, butter*

**To serve:** Feta cheese (crumbled), cilantro, guacamole

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Line sheet pan with parchment paper.
2. Add sweet potatoes and red onion to the sheet pan. Drizzle with 2 tbsp olive oil, season with salt and pepper, and toss to coat.
3. Bake veggies for 15 minutes while you prepare the sauce and chicken.
4. Make hot honey sauce: mix honey, hot sauce, melted butter, lemon juice, and crushed red pepper flakes together.
5. Remove pan from oven. Add chicken over the top of the veggies.
6. Pour half the hot honey sauce over everything. Toss to coat.
7. Return to oven. Bake 25–30 more minutes until chicken is cooked through and potatoes are fork tender.
8. Remove from oven. Drizzle remaining sauce over the top.
9. Top with crumbled feta cheese, cilantro, and guacamole if desired.

## Notes

- Starting veggies 15 minutes early prevents the chicken from drying out while potatoes finish cooking.
- You can buy bottled hot honey at the store — that works too instead of making the sauce.
- Feta cheese on top is highly recommended — don't skip it.
- Skip the crushed red pepper flakes if you want it without any heat — it becomes a sweet savory chicken.
- Gold or russet potatoes can replace sweet potatoes.
