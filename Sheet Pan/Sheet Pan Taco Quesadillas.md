## Ingredients

- ½ yellow onion, diced
- 1 lb ground beef
- ½ cup salsa
- ½ cup corn (frozen or canned)
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained
- 6–8 flour tortillas (medium, burrito size)
- 2 cups shredded cheese (cheddar or Mexican blend)

*Not counted: olive oil, taco seasoning, salt, pepper, non-stick spray*

**To serve:** sour cream, salsa, guacamole

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Spray two sheet pans with non-stick spray.
2. Cook ground beef and diced onion in a skillet over medium-high heat. Season with taco seasoning, salt, and pepper. Drain fat.
3. Stir in salsa, corn, and black beans. Cook 2 minutes. Remove from heat.
4. Lay tortillas on prepared sheet pan, overlapping to fill the pan.
5. Spread half the cheese over the tortillas. Spoon taco filling evenly over cheese. Top with remaining cheese.
6. Lay another layer of tortillas over the filling.
7. Place a second sheet pan directly on top of the quesadillas to press them flat and crisp both sides simultaneously.
8. Bake at 425°F for 15–20 minutes until tortillas are golden and crispy.
9. Remove top sheet pan. Slice into pieces and serve with sour cream and salsa.

## Notes

- The second sheet pan pressed on top is the trick — it creates even contact and crisps both sides at once.
- Spray both the bottom pan and the inside of the top pan with non-stick spray.
- The taco filling can be made ahead — assemble and bake when ready to eat.
- Cut into strips for a fun appetizer or game day snack.
