## Ingredients

### Marinade
- 6 chicken thighs
- 3 tbsp plain Greek yogurt (not vanilla)
- Juice of 1 lemon
- ½ cup olive oil
- Salt and pepper
- 2 tsp oregano
- Thyme to taste

### Vegetables
- 2 zucchini, sliced
- 1 red or purple onion, sliced
- 1 orange or red bell pepper, sliced
- Cherry tomatoes (handful)
- Salt, pepper, oregano, and thyme to taste

**To serve:** Feta cheese (crumbled), white rice, or as-is

*Not counted: olive oil, minced garlic, salt, pepper, oregano, thyme*

## Instructions

1. Whisk together lemon juice, Greek yogurt, a little minced garlic, salt, pepper, 2 tsp oregano, and thyme in a large bowl. Whisk in ½ cup olive oil.
2. Add chicken thighs and coat completely. Marinate for about 20 minutes.
3. Preheat oven to 425°F.
4. Spread zucchini, onion, bell pepper, and cherry tomatoes onto the sheet pan. Season with salt, pepper, oregano, and thyme. Spray or drizzle with a little olive oil.
5. Add marinated chicken thighs over the top of the vegetables. Pour any remaining marinade over the veggies.
6. Sprinkle feta cheese over the top.
7. Bake at 425°F for 35–40 minutes until chicken is cooked through and vegetables are roasted.
8. Serve over white rice or as-is.

## Notes

- Greek yogurt in the marinade tenderizes the chicken — don't substitute with regular yogurt.
- Chicken thighs stay much juicier than chicken breast in the oven — recommended here.
- Cherry tomatoes burst during roasting and add juice to the vegetables.
- Excellent for meal prep — portions well into containers for lunches.
- Feta cheese is key to the Greek flavor — don't skip it.
