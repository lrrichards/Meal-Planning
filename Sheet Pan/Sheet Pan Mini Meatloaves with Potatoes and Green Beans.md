## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground pork + 1 lb ground beef (half and half makes a good meatloaf)
- 3/4 cup panko breadcrumbs
- 2 eggs
- 1 lb potato
- 12 oz green bean
- Onion powder, garlic salt, pepper
- Olive oil
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- For the glaze: ketchup, mustard, brown sugar

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 400°F. Halve the mini potatoes, toss with olive oil, garlic salt, and pepper, and spread on one side of a sheet pan (leave room for the meatloaves and beans).
2. In a bowl, mix the ground pork and beef with 3/4 cup panko, 2 eggs, onion powder, garlic salt, and pepper (add cheese if you like). Mix well.
3. Form into 6 mini meatloaves and place on the sheet pan.
4. Make the glaze: mix ketchup, a little mustard, and brown sugar. Brush over the meatloaves.
5. Bake at 400°F for about 15 minutes.
6. Toss the green beans with olive oil and the same seasoning. Add them to the empty space on the pan.
7. Return to the oven and bake until the meatloaves are cooked through and the green beans are tender.

## Notes

- A whole sheet-pan dinner: mini meatloaves, roasted potatoes, and green beans.
- Not keto (panko, potatoes, glaze).
- Aldi "poor man's comfort food."
