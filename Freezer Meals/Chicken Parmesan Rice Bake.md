## Ingredients (9x13 aluminum pan)

- 3–4 cups cooked white rice, cooled
- ~2 cups cooked shredded rotisserie chicken
- ¼ cup parmesan cheese
- 24 oz marinara sauce
- 1 cup mozzarella cheese, plus more for the top

## Instructions

1. Add rice, chicken, parmesan, marinara, and mozzarella to a 9x13 aluminum pan. Stir to combine.
2. Top with more cheese.
3. Cover tightly with foil, label, and freeze. Keep it covered when baking so it steams and the top doesn't burn.
4. To cook (from frozen): bake at 350°F about 60 minutes covered, then 10 minutes uncovered.

## Notes

- Evan-safe: marinara is smooth.
- Rice-based cousin of [[Chicken Parmesan Casserole]] (pasta + breadcrumbs) — different carb base, kept as distinct.
