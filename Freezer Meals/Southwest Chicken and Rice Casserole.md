## Ingredients (9x13 aluminum pan)

- 3 cups cooked white rice, cooled
- 1 cup cooked shredded rotisserie chicken
- 1 can black beans, partially drained
- 1 can (10 oz) red enchilada sauce
- ¼ cup salsa
- 1 cup Monterey Jack or cheddar cheese (for the top)

## Instructions

1. Add rice, chicken, black beans, enchilada sauce, and salsa to a 9x13 aluminum pan. Stir to combine.
2. Top with the cheese.
3. Cover tightly with foil, label, and freeze.
4. To cook (from frozen): bake at 375°F about 40–45 minutes covered, until heated through.

- Enchilada sauce is smooth (-OK); the ¼ cup salsa is the only flag — **use a smooth salsa to keep it
