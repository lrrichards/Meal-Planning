## Ingredients (9x13 aluminum pan)

- 1 lb penne pasta, cooked, cooled
- ~1 lb cooked, cooled ground beef
- 24 oz marinara sauce
- ½ cup parmesan cheese (plus a sprinkle for the top)
- ~1 cup mozzarella cheese
- 1½ tsp Italian seasoning

*Not counted: Italian seasoning*

## Instructions

1. Add the cooked pasta, ground beef, marinara, parmesan, mozzarella, and Italian seasoning to a 9x13 aluminum pan. Stir until the noodles are coated in sauce.
2. Sprinkle extra parmesan over the top.
3. Cover tightly with foil, label with bake instructions, and freeze.
4. To cook (from frozen, no thawing): bake at 375°F about 40 minutes covered, then 10 minutes uncovered until heated through and the cheese is melted. Everything is pre-cooked — you're just heating it through.
