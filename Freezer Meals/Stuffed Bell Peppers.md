## Ingredients (per bag — makes 3 stuffed peppers)

- 1/2 lb ground beef, cooked with diced onion
- 3 bell peppers, tops and centers removed
- 1 can diced tomatoes with green chilies
- 1 can (6 oz) tomato paste
- 1/3 cup brown sugar
- 1/2 cup uncooked white rice
- 1/2 cup chicken broth (or water + chicken broth powder)

*Not counted: salt, pepper, paprika*

## Freezer Prep

1. Cook ground beef and diced onion in a pan until beef is cooked through. Drain excess grease if needed.
2. In a large bowl, combine cooked beef/onion, diced tomatoes, tomato paste, brown sugar, rice, broth, salt, pepper, and paprika. Stir well.
3. Cut tops and stems off bell peppers. Remove seeds. If they won't stand upright, trim a small flat slice off the bottom (don't cut through).
4. Stuff peppers generously with the filling mixture.
5. Stand stuffed peppers upright in the freezer bag. **Freeze standing up** — this keeps the filling inside the peppers.
6. Label the bag before filling: name + "SC Low 6–7 hrs."
7. Lasts up to 6 months (best within 3 months).

## Slow Cooker Instructions

1. Remove peppers from bag (they'll slide out easily).
2. Stand peppers upright in the slow cooker.
3. Cook on low 6–7 hours until peppers are tender and filling is cooked through. Add 30–60 min if cooking straight from rock-solid frozen.
4. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- **Freeze standing up** — the only freezer meal in this vault that requires this. Keeps filling in place.
- Tomato paste trick: open both ends of the can, push through with one lid — gets all the paste out cleanly.
- Rice holds its shape beautifully and doesn't get mushy in the slow cooker.
- Double the recipe easily — same effort, twice the dinners.
