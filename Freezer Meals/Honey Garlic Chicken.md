## Ingredients (per bag)

- 1.5 lb chicken tenderloin
- 3/4 cup broth (chicken, veggie, or beef)
- 1/4 cup low sodium soy sauce
- 3 tbsp honey
- 1/4 cup hoisin sauce (or barbecue sauce)
- 1 tbsp fresh or ground ginger
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar (or regular vinegar)
- 1 onion, diced (or frozen diced onion)

*Not counted: garlic or garlic powder, salt, pepper*

**To serve:** White rice, steamed broccoli, green onions, chili flakes

## Freezer Prep

1. Label bag before filling: name + "SC Low 6–7 hrs."
2. Add all ingredients to bag. Add garlic powder (or 1–2 tbsp minced garlic), salt, and pepper.
3. Seal and shake/squish to mix the sauce around the chicken.
4. Freeze flat (in a blob shape that fits your slow cooker).
5. Lasts up to 6 months (best within 3 months).

## Slow Cooker Instructions

1. Shake the bag before opening — gets the sauce mixed back in.
2. Run sealed bag under warm water briefly if rock solid. Dump into slow cooker.
3. Cook on low 6–7 hours.
4. Shred or serve chicken whole over white rice.
5. Top with green onions and chili flakes.

## Notes

- Shake the frozen bag before dumping — the sauce separates during freezing and needs redistributing.
- Hoisin sauce and barbecue sauce are interchangeable here — both work great.
- Rice vinegar adds brightness — regular vinegar works as a substitute.
- Serve with steamed broccoli on the side for an easy complete meal.
