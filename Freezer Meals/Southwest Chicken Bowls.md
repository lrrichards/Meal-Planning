## Ingredients (per bag)

- 2 chicken breasts (frozen works perfectly)
- 1 can diced tomatoes (plain or with green chilies)
- 1 can black beans or pinto beans, drained
- 1 can corn, drained

*Not counted: taco seasoning*

**To serve:** White rice, sour cream, guacamole, shredded cheese, green onions, chips, or tortillas

## Freezer Prep

1. Label bag before filling: name + "SC Low 6–7 hrs."
2. Add all ingredients to bag. Sprinkle in taco seasoning to taste.
3. Seal, press out air, and freeze flat (freeze in a blob shape that fits your slow cooker).
4. Lasts up to 6 months (best within 3 months).

## Slow Cooker Instructions

1. Run sealed bag under warm water briefly if rock solid. Dump into slow cooker.
2. Cook on low 6–7 hours.
3. Shred or chop chicken and stir back in.
4. Serve over rice as a burrito bowl, or use as filling for burritos, tacos, or enchiladas.

## Notes

- Only 4 ingredients — one of the simplest freezer meals in the vault.
- Freeze in a flat blob shape so it fits easily into the slow cooker.
- Incredibly versatile — this filling works in bowls, burritos, tacos, or enchiladas.
- Use black beans or pinto beans interchangeably — whatever is on hand.
