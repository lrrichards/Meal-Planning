## Ingredients (per bag)

- ~1 lb cooked, cooled ground beef
- 1 can mixed chili beans, undrained
- 1 can light red kidney beans, undrained
- 1 can Rotel (diced tomatoes with green chilies), undrained
- 1 can petite diced tomatoes, undrained
- ½–1½ tsp chili powder
- 1–1½ tsp onion powder
- 1–1½ tsp garlic powder
- 1–1½ tsp paprika

*Not counted: chili powder, onion powder, garlic powder, paprika*

## Instructions

1. Brown and drain the ground beef; cool completely.
2. Label a gallon freezer bag: "Chili — low 5 hr."
3. Add all cans (undrained), seasonings, and beef to the bag. Mix lightly, seal, press flat, and freeze.
4. To cook: dump the bag into the slow cooker and cook on low about 5 hours. The undrained cans provide the liquid — no broth needed.
