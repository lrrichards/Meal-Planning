## Ingredients (per bag)

- 3 chicken breasts (frozen works perfectly)
- 1 cup teriyaki sauce
- 2 cups frozen mixed vegetables (any stir-fry or veggie blend works)

**To serve:** Cooked white rice

## Instructions

1. Label a gallon freezer bag: "Teriyaki Chicken Rice Bowls — low 5 hr, serve over rice."
2. Add teriyaki sauce, frozen vegetables, and chicken to the bag. Seal, press flat, and freeze.
3. To cook: dump the bag into the slow cooker and cook on low about 5 hours, until the chicken shreds easily.
4. Shred the chicken into the sauce and serve over rice.
