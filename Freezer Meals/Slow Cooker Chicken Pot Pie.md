## Ingredients (per bag)

- 2–3 chicken breasts (frozen works perfectly)
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 2 cups frozen mixed vegetables (go heavy on the veggies)
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 can refrigerated biscuits (added at cook time, not in the bag)

*Not counted: onion powder, garlic powder, paprika*

## Instructions

1. Label a gallon freezer bag: "Chicken Pot Pie — low 5 hr, add biscuits over the top for the last hour."
2. Add cream of chicken soup, frozen vegetables, seasonings, and chicken to the bag. Seal, press flat, and freeze.
3. To cook: dump the bag into the slow cooker and cook on low 5 hours.
4. For the last hour, lay biscuits over the top and let them cook through.

## Notes

- Freezer meals lose a little flavor in the freezer — season heavier than feels right.
- **Filled in by Claude (not stated in video):** seasoning amounts (~1 tsp each — the paprika lid fell in on camera, so the video amount was "more than a teaspoon"); biscuit count (1 standard 8-count can assumed).
