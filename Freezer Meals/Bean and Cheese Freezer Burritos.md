## Ingredients (makes 5 burritos)

- 1–2 cans refried beans (~½ cup per burrito), seasoned with a little taco seasoning
- ~2 cups shredded cheese
- 5 burrito-size flour tortillas

*Not counted: taco seasoning*

## Instructions

1. Season the refried beans with a little taco seasoning.
2. Spread beans on each tortilla, top with shredded cheese, and roll up.
3. Wrap each individually in foil, label, and freeze.
4. To reheat: microwave (unwrapped), or air fryer until crispy.

## Notes

- Green onions or anything else can go in — simple base on purpose.
- Distinct from [[Rice and Bean Burritos]] (Spanish rice + bean mix, pan-crisped) and [[Lazy Burritos]] (ground meat + beans).
- **Filled in by Claude (not stated in video):** bean and cheese amounts — video never measured; ~½ cup beans + ~⅓ cup cheese per burrito assumed.
