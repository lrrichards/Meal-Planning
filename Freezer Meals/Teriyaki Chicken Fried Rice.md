## Ingredients (per bag)

- ~2 cups cooked white rice, cooled
- ~2 cups cooked shredded rotisserie chicken
- ~2 cups frozen California vegetable blend (carrots, cauliflower, broccoli)
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- ½ cup teriyaki sauce
- 2 eggs (scrambled in at cook time, not in the bag)

*Not counted: garlic*

## Instructions

1. Label a gallon freezer bag: "Teriyaki Chicken & Rice — simmer with ½ cup water 20 min, scramble in 2 eggs."
2. Add rice, chicken, vegetables, garlic, and teriyaki sauce to the bag. Seal, press flat, and freeze.
3. To cook (from frozen): dump into a pot on the stove with ½ cup water and simmer about 20 minutes. Scramble 2 eggs into it as it cooks.

## Notes

- Different dish from [[Teriyaki Chicken and Rice]] (One Pot — from-scratch soy/honey sauce, raw rice) and [[Teriyaki Chicken Rice Bowls]] (slow-cooker bag). This is the fried-rice-style version — kept on purpose.
