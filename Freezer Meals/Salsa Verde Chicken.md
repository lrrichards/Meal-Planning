## Ingredients (per bag)

- 2–3 chicken breasts (frozen works perfectly)
- 1 cup salsa verde

**To serve:** Tortillas and taco toppings (cheese, sour cream, lettuce)

## Instructions

1. Label a gallon freezer bag: "Salsa Verde Chicken — low 5 hr, serve with tortillas + taco toppings."
2. Add frozen chicken breasts and salsa verde to the bag. Seal, press flat, and freeze.
3. To cook: dump the bag contents into the slow cooker and cook on low about 5 hours, until the chicken shreds easily.
4. Shred the chicken into the sauce and serve in tortillas with taco toppings.

## Notes

- Freeze flat and stack — keeps in the freezer up to 6 months, freshest within 3.
- Evan-safe: salsa verde is tomatillo-based, no tomato.
- Near-variation of [[Salsa Chicken]] (CrockPot) — that one uses red salsa + taco seasoning; this is the green, no-seasoning version. Kept on purpose.
