## Ingredients (per bag)

- 4 tbsp butter
- 2 cups cooked white rice, cooled
- 1½–2 cups cooked shredded rotisserie chicken
- ½ cup shredded cheddar cheese
- 1 tbsp minced garlic (go heavy)
- ½ cup chicken broth (added at cook time, not in the bag)

*Not counted: butter, garlic*

## Instructions

1. Label a gallon freezer bag: "Garlic Butter Chicken & Rice — simmer with ½ cup broth, 10–15 min."
2. Add butter, rice, chicken, cheddar, and garlic to the bag. Shake, seal, press flat, and freeze. (Butter freezes wonderfully.)
3. To cook (from frozen): dump into a pot on the stove with ½ cup chicken broth. Simmer 10–15 minutes until heated through.

## Notes

- Looks like nothing in the bag — turns into magic on the stove.
