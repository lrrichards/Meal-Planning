## Ingredients (per bag)

- ~2 cups cooked shredded rotisserie chicken
- 4–5 cups cooked elbow pasta, cooled
- 1–2 cups frozen broccoli florets
- 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
- 2 cups milk
- Salt, pepper, paprika, onion powder, garlic powder — season well, ~½–1 tsp each

*Not counted: salt, pepper, paprika, onion powder, garlic powder*

## Instructions

1. Label a gallon freezer bag: "Chicken Broccoli Mac & Cheese — simmer 15–20 min."
2. Add chicken, elbows, broccoli, cheddar, milk, and seasonings to the bag. Seal, press flat, and freeze. (Milk freezes fine in one-pot meals like this.)
3. To cook (from frozen): dump into a pot on the stove and simmer 15–20 minutes, stirring, until cheesy and heated through.

## Notes

- Season generously — plain pasta + broccoli + cheese needs it, and freezing mutes flavor.
- From-scratch cousin of [[Cheddar Broccoli Chicken Pasta]] (which uses a boxed pasta mix + cream).
- **Filled in by Claude (not stated in video):** seasoning amounts (~½–1 tsp each — video shook them in without measuring). All food amounts were stated.
