## Ingredients (per bag)

- 1 cup lentils
- 1 onion, diced (or ~1/2 cup frozen diced onion)
- 1/2–1 cup frozen diced carrots
- 2–3 ribs celery, sliced
- 2 cans diced tomatoes (garlic & herb, Italian, or plain)
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 1 tbsp brown sugar

**Added when cooking (DO NOT freeze):**
- 6 cups broth (any variety)

*Not counted: Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt, pepper*

## Freezer Prep

1. Label your bag BEFORE adding food: name + "SC Low 6–8 hrs — ADD 6 CUPS BROTH when cooking."
2. Add all ingredients except broth to the bag.
3. Add Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Seal and freeze.
4. Lasts up to 6 months frozen (best within 3 months).

## Slow Cooker Instructions

1. Dump bag contents into slow cooker.
2. Add 6 cups of broth.
3. Cook on low for 6–8 hours. Longer = softer, creamier lentils.
4. Stir and serve.

## Notes

- Broth is kept OUT of the bag to avoid an overfilled bag — always note it on the label.
- Dairy should never go in the freezer bag — if adding cream or cheese, stir in at the very end of cooking.
- Frozen carrots and diced onion save significant prep time and freeze perfectly.
- Balsamic vinegar and brown sugar add depth — don't skip them.
- Flavors can be slightly dulled after freezing — taste and adjust seasoning after cooking.
