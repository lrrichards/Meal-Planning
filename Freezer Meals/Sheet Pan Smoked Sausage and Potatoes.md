## Ingredients (per bag)

- 1 package (~13–14 oz) smoked sausage, sliced into discs
- 3–4 potatoes, cut into small pieces (small = cooks through in the oven)
- 10 oz frozen pepper and onion blend (half a 20 oz bag)
- Cajun seasoning (Tony's Creole) to coat, ~1 tbsp

*Not counted: Cajun seasoning*

## Instructions

1. Label a gallon freezer bag: "SHEET PAN smoked sausage — 350°F for 35–45 min." (Write "sheet pan" on it so you don't dump it in a slow cooker.)
2. Add cut potatoes, pepper and onion blend, sliced sausage, and Cajun seasoning to the bag. Shake to coat, seal, press flat, and freeze.
3. To cook (from frozen): dump onto a sheet pan and bake at 350°F for 35–45 minutes, until the potatoes are tender.

## Notes

- The pre-sliced frozen pepper/onion bag saves all the knife work — recommended for batch days.
- Any smoked sausage works (apple smoked, etc.).
- **Filled in by Claude (not stated in video):** sausage package size (~13–14 oz standard rope assumed) and Cajun seasoning amount (~1 tbsp — video shook it on, then the bag broke mid-pour).
