## Ingredients (per bag)

- 2–3 chicken breasts (frozen works perfectly)
- 1½–2 tbsp ranch seasoning
- 2 cups chicken broth (added at cook time, not in the bag)
- 8 oz cream cheese (stirred in at the end, not in the bag)

*Not counted: ranch seasoning*

## Instructions

1. Label a gallon freezer bag: "Ranch Chicken — low 5 hr with 2 cups chicken broth, stir in 8 oz cream cheese at the end."
2. Add chicken and ranch seasoning to the bag. Seal, press flat, and freeze.
3. To cook: dump the bag into the slow cooker with 2 cups chicken broth and cook on low about 5 hours.
4. Shred the chicken, stir in the cream cheese until melted and creamy.
5. Serve over rice or noodles, with broccoli on the side.

## Notes

- Cream cheese goes in fresh at cook time — it separates and gets chunky if frozen.
- Evan-safe: no tomato.
- Near-variation of [[Crack Chicken]] (CrockPot) — same ranch + cream cheese base but no bacon or cheddar, cooked in broth. Kept on purpose.
- No filled-in amounts — video stated everything.
