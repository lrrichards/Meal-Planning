## Ingredients (9x13 aluminum pan)

- 2–3 cups cooked shredded rotisserie chicken
- ½ cup shredded cheddar cheese (in the filling) + ~1 cup (for the top)
- 1 can (10 oz) red enchilada sauce (half in the filling, half on top)
- 1 tbsp taco seasoning
- ~8 burrito-size flour tortillas

*Not counted: taco seasoning*

## Instructions

1. Mix chicken, ½ cup cheddar, ~5 oz of the enchilada sauce, and taco seasoning in a bowl.
2. Drizzle a little enchilada sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 aluminum pan — it acts as glue to hold the enchiladas in place.
3. Fill each tortilla with the chicken mixture, roll up (sealed both ends, burrito-style), and place seam-down in the pan.
4. Pour the remaining enchilada sauce over the top, then sprinkle on the remaining cheddar.
5. Cover tightly with foil, label, and freeze.
6. To cook (from frozen): bake at 375°F for 40 minutes.

## Notes

- Serve with refried beans, corn, or beans heated on the side. Makes leftovers.
- Bulk up by adding a can of beans or corn to the filling.
