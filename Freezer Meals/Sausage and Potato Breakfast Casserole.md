## Ingredients (9x13 pan)

- 10 eggs
- ½–1 lb cooked, cooled breakfast sausage
- ~½ cup shredded cheddar cheese, plus more for the top
- ~½ cup shredded mozzarella cheese
- ½ cup milk
- 2 large russet potatoes, diced small

## Instructions

1. Whisk the eggs in a large bowl. Add sausage, cheeses, milk, and diced potatoes; whisk to combine.
2. Pour into a 9x13 pan and sprinkle cheese on top.
3. Either bake fresh at 350°F about 50 minutes, OR cover tightly with foil, label, and freeze.
4. To cook from frozen: bake at 350°F about 1 hour 20 minutes covered with foil, until the egg is set.
