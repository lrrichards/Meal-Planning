## Ingredients (makes 4 big burritos)

- 2–3 cups cooked shredded rotisserie chicken
- ~½ cup pre-cooked bacon bits
- ~1 cup shredded cheese
- ~1½ cups frozen pepper and onion blend
- ~1 cup barbecue sauce
- 4 burrito-size flour tortillas

## Instructions

1. Mix chicken, bacon bits, cheese, pepper/onion blend, and barbecue sauce in a big bowl.
2. Fill the tortillas generously — bulky burritos, not wimpy ones — and roll up.
3. Wrap each individually in foil, label, and freeze.
4. To reheat: microwave (unwrapped), or air fryer until crispy outside and heated through.

## Notes

- Inspired by a Texas brisket burrito — brisket works too if you have it.
- **Filled in by Claude (not stated in video):** pepper/onion amount — video used "the rest of the bag" after the sheet pan meal and taco tray; ~1½ cups assumed.
