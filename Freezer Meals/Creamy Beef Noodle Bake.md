## Ingredients (9x13 pan)

- 1–1½ cups frozen mixed vegetables
- ¾ lb cooked, cooled ground beef
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- ½ cup shredded cheddar cheese
- ½ cup milk
- ~3 cups cooked, cooled elbow pasta
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder

*Not counted: onion powder, paprika, garlic powder*

## Instructions

1. Mix vegetables, ground beef, cream of mushroom, cheddar, milk, and seasonings in a bowl.
2. Stir in the cooked elbows until coated in the sauce.
3. Spread into a 9x13 aluminum pan, cover tightly with foil, label, and freeze.
4. To cook (from frozen): bake at 375°F about 40 minutes covered, then 10 minutes uncovered until the cheese is melted on top.

## Notes

- Veggies are built in — no side needed.
- **Filled in by Claude (not stated in video):** elbow pasta amount — video said "some of my elbows"; ~3 cups cooked (about ½ lb dry) assumed to fill a 9x13 alongside the other ingredients.
