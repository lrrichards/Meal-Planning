## Ingredients (per bag)

- ¾ lb cooked, cooled ground beef
- 1 can Rotel (diced tomatoes with green chilies), undrained
- 1 can black beans, undrained
- 1 can corn, drained
- 1 tbsp taco seasoning
- 2 cups broth (added at cook time, not in the bag)

*Not counted: taco seasoning*

## Instructions

1. Brown and drain the ground beef; cool completely.
2. Label a gallon freezer bag: "Taco Soup — low 5 hr with 2 cups broth."
3. Add beef, Rotel, black beans, corn, and taco seasoning to the bag. Seal, press flat, and freeze.
4. To cook: dump the bag into the slow cooker with 2 cups broth and cook on low about 5 hours.
