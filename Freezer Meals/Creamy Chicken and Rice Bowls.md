## Ingredients (per bag)

- ~2 cups cooked white rice, cooled
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- ½ cup milk
- 2 cups frozen broccoli
- ~1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- 2–3 cups cooked shredded rotisserie chicken
- ~1 tbsp no-salt seasoning blend (or garlic powder + onion powder + paprika)
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp pepper

*Not counted: seasoning blend, salt, pepper*

## Instructions

1. Label a gallon freezer bag: "Creamy Chicken & Rice Bowls — simmer with ½ cup water."
2. Add everything to the bag. Seal, press flat, and freeze.
3. To cook (from frozen): dump into a pot on the stove with ½ cup water. Simmer, stirring, about 10–15 minutes until heated through. Microwave works too, but stovetop is better.

## Notes

- Carb + protein + veggie all in one bag — no sides needed.
- Season generously: the rice, broccoli, and cream-of base are bland without it, and freezing mutes flavor.
- Evan-safe: no tomato.
