## Ingredients (makes 5 large burritos)

- ~2 cups cooked shredded rotisserie chicken
- 1 can black beans, drained
- 1 can diced tomatoes, drained
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- ¼ cup cooked white rice (kept light on purpose — protein over filler)
- 5 burrito-size flour tortillas

## Instructions

1. Mix chicken, black beans, drained tomatoes, cheddar, and rice in a bowl.
2. Fill each tortilla generously, roll up burrito-style.
3. Wrap each burrito individually in foil, label the foil with reheat instructions, and freeze.
4. To reheat: microwave (unwrapped) until heated through, or air fryer until crispy outside and hot inside.
