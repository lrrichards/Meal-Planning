## Ingredients (makes 5 burritos)

- 8 eggs
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- 1/2 cup bacon bits
- 1/2 cup refried bean
- 5 burrito-size flour tortillas

## Instructions

1. Scramble the eggs; let cool.
2. Fill each tortilla with scrambled egg, cheese, and bacon bits. Add a spoonful of refried beans to some for extra protein — label those.
3. Roll up, wrap each individually in foil, label with reheat instructions, and freeze.
4. To reheat: microwave (unwrapped), or air fryer until crispy outside and heated through.

## Notes

- Breakfast-for-dinner, or grab-and-go breakfasts/lunches.
- Diced cooked potato is a good add-in if you want them heartier.
