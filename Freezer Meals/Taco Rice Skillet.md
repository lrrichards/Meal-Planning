## Ingredients (per bag)

- ½–1 lb cooked, cooled ground beef
- 1 can black beans, undrained
- 1 can diced tomatoes, undrained
- ½ cup salsa
- 1 tbsp taco seasoning
- 1½ cups cooked white rice, cooled

*Not counted: taco seasoning*

## Instructions

1. Label a gallon freezer bag: "Taco Rice Skillet — simmer with ½–1 cup water, 10–15 min."
2. Add everything to the bag. Seal, press flat, and freeze.
3. To cook (from frozen): dump into a pot on the stove with ½–1 cup water. Simmer 10–15 minutes until heated through.
