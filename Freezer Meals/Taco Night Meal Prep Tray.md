## Ingredients (9x13 aluminum pan, divided into 3 sections with foil)

- 1 can black beans
- ~3 cups cooked shredded rotisserie chicken
- ~1 cup frozen pepper and onion blend (on top of the chicken, for flavor)
- ~3 cups cooked white rice, cooled

**To serve:** Tortillas and taco toppings

## Instructions

1. Use folded foil strips to divide a 9x13 aluminum pan into 3 sections.
2. Section 1: black beans. Section 2: chicken topped with the pepper/onion blend. Section 3: rice.
3. Label the lid: "Tacos — serve in tortillas, microwave to reheat." Cover and freeze.
4. To serve: scoop onto plates and microwave to reheat (never put the aluminum pan in the microwave). Build tacos/burritos/fajita bowls at the table.

## Notes

- Components stay separated — also great as a take-to-a-friend meal (new baby, etc.).
- **Filled in by Claude (not stated in video):** rice amount ("quite a bit" → ~3 cups) and pepper/onion amount ("some" → ~1 cup). Chicken was stated (~3 cups).
