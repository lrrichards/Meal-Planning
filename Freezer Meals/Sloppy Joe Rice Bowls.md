## Ingredients (per bag)

- ~2 cups cooked white rice, cooled
- ½–1 lb cooked, cooled ground beef
- 1 can Manwich (sloppy joe sauce)

**To serve:** Veggies on the side (none in the bag)

## Instructions

1. Label a gallon freezer bag: "Sloppy Joe Rice Bowls — simmer ~10 min, serve with veggies on the side."
2. Add rice, ground beef, and Manwich to the bag. Seal, press flat, and freeze.
3. To cook (from frozen): dump into a pot on the stove and simmer about 10 minutes until heated through.
