## Ingredients (per bag)

- 1 lb cooked, cooled ground beef
- 4 large russet potatoes, peeled and cut bite-sized (about 1 potato per person)
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup (cream of chicken or any cream-of soup works)
- 1–2 tsp garlic powder
- 1–2 tsp onion powder
- 1–2 tsp paprika
- 3 cups chicken broth (added at cook time, not in the bag)
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese (stirred in at the end, not in the bag)

*Not counted: garlic powder, onion powder, paprika*

## Instructions

1. Brown and drain the ground beef; let it cool completely before bagging (prevents ice crystals).
2. Label a gallon freezer bag: "Cheeseburger Potato Soup — low 5 hr with 3 cups chicken broth, add cheese at the end."
3. Add potatoes, cream of mushroom soup, seasonings, and ground beef to the bag. Seal, press flat, and freeze.
4. To cook: dump the bag into the slow cooker with 3 cups chicken broth and cook on low about 5 hours, until the potatoes are tender.
5. Stir in the cheddar at the end of cooking.
