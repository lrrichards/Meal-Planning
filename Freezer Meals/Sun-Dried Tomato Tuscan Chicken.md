## Ingredients (per bag)

- 3 chicken breasts (frozen works perfectly)
- 2 cups spinach
- 1/3 cup sun-dried tomatoes (herb variety recommended)
- 1 onion, diced (or frozen diced onion)

*Not counted: salt, pepper*

**To serve:** Mashed potatoes, white rice, cauliflower rice, or veggies

## Freezer Prep

1. Label bag before filling: name + "SC Low 6–7 hrs."
2. Season chicken with salt and pepper. Add to bag.
3. Add spinach, sun-dried tomatoes, and onion.
4. Seal, press out air, and freeze flat.
5. Lasts up to 6 months (best within 3 months).

## Slow Cooker Instructions

1. Run sealed bag under warm water briefly if rock solid. Dump into slow cooker.
2. Cook on low 6–7 hours.
3. Shred chicken and stir back into the juices so it absorbs the flavor.
4. Serve over mashed potatoes, rice, or veggies.

## Notes

- Also known as Tuscan Chicken or Marry Me Chicken — same flavor profile.
- Sun-dried tomatoes with herbs are better than plain — worth finding that variety.
- Spinach added in the bag freezes and cooks beautifully — no need to add at the end.
- Shredding the chicken and returning it to the juices is what makes this exceptional.
