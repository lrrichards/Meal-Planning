## Ingredients (per bag)

- 4–5 cups cooked penne pasta, cooled
- 2 cups frozen broccoli
- ~2 cups cooked shredded rotisserie chicken
- 1 jar (16 oz) Alfredo sauce
- Dash of salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Label a gallon freezer bag: "Chicken Broccoli Alfredo — simmer with ⅓ cup water, ~15 min."
2. Add pasta, broccoli, chicken, and Alfredo sauce to the bag (a splash of the water can rinse the jar's last bit of sauce into the bag). Season, seal, press flat, and freeze.
3. To cook (from frozen): dump into a pot on the stove with ⅓ cup water. Simmer 10–15 minutes until heated through.

## Notes

- Jarred Alfredo isn't gourmet, but with the broccoli and pasta it's the easiest dinner there is.
