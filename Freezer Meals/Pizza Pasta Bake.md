## Ingredients (9x13 aluminum pan)

- 1 lb rotini pasta, cooked, cooled
- 24 oz pasta sauce
- ~4 oz pepperoni (half a bag)
- ½ cup mozzarella cheese, plus more for the top
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning

*Not counted: Italian seasoning*

## Instructions

1. Add cooked rotini, pasta sauce, pepperoni, ½ cup mozzarella, and Italian seasoning to a 9x13 aluminum pan. Stir until the noodles are coated.
2. Top with more mozzarella.
3. Cover tightly with foil, label, and freeze.
4. To cook (from frozen): bake at 375°F for 35 minutes covered, then 10 minutes uncovered, until heated through. Nothing raw in it — just heating through.
