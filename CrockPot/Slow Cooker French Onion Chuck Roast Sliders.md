## Ingredients

**Slow Cooker:**
- 2.5 lbs chuck roast
- 1 yellow onion, sliced or diced
- 1.5 cups beef broth
- 1/2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce

*Not counted: garlic, steak seasoning*

**Slider assembly:**
- 8 hamburger buns
- 4 slices Colby Jack cheese
- 1/2 cup crispy French fried onions

## Instructions

1. Add chuck roast to slow cooker. Add onion, beef broth, Worcestershire sauce, 1 tbsp minced garlic, and 1/2 tbsp steak seasoning.
2. Cook on low for 8 hours until meat is pull-apart tender.
3. Shred the meat. Reserve the slow cooker juices for dipping.
4. Preheat oven to 400°F. Place open hamburger buns on a sheet pan.
5. Pile shredded meat onto buns. Top with Colby Jack cheese slices and French fried onions.
6. Bake at 400°F for about 5 minutes until cheese is melted.
7. Serve with a small bowl of the slow cooker juices on the side for dipping.

## Notes

- The slow cooker juices make an excellent dipping sauce — don't discard them.
- Also excellent served over mashed potatoes or white rice instead of as sliders.
- French fried onions on top are optional but highly recommended.
- 8 hours on low = perfect for a workday — start it in the morning.
