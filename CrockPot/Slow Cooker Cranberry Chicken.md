## Ingredients

- 3–5 chicken breasts (or boneless, skinless chicken thighs)
- 14 oz whole or jellied cranberry sauce
- 1 cup French dressing

*Not counted: salt, pepper*

**To serve:** White rice, sesame seeds, steamed vegetables

## Instructions

1. Whisk cranberry sauce and French dressing together in a bowl until fully combined.
2. Add chicken breasts to the slow cooker. Season with a dash of salt and pepper.
3. Pour cranberry sauce mixture over the chicken.
4. Cook on low for 4 hours or high for 2 hours.
5. Shred chicken and stir into the sauce.
6. Serve over white rice with sesame seeds.

## Notes

- It sounds strange but doesn't taste like cranberry or French dressing — comes out more like teriyaki chicken.
- Whole or jellied cranberry sauce both work.
- Only 3 main ingredients — one of the simplest crockpot recipes in the vault.
- Add steamed broccoli or veggies on the side for a complete meal.
