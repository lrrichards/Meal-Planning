## Ingredients

- 3 large chicken breasts
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp thyme
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1 1/2 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 cup diced onion
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 2 cans (4 oz each) diced green chilies
- 2 cups spinach
- 1 block cream cheese
- 1 cup heavy whipping cream
- 1/2 cup parmesan cheese
- 1/2 cup bacon

## Instructions

1. To the crock pot add the chicken breasts, cumin, thyme, salt, pepper, minced garlic, diced onion, chicken broth, and the diced green chilies.
2. Cook on high about 5 hours, then shred the chicken.
3. Add 2 cups spinach and 1 block cream cheese. Stir and cook about 30 more minutes to wilt the spinach.
4. Stir in 1 cup heavy whipping cream, 1/2 cup parmesan, and 1/2 cup bacon.
5. Adjust thickness with more broth or cream to taste.

## Notes

- Keto / low-carb white chili — no beans, no tomatoes.
- Cream-heavy (heavy cream + parmesan + cream cheese), so not tagged extreme-budget.
