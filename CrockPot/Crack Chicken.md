## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lb chicken thighs (or chicken breast)
- 1 oz ranch seasoning mix
- 1 block cream cheese
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- 1 cup bacon bits

## Instructions

1. Place the chicken in the crock pot. Add 1 oz ranch seasoning and lay the block of cream cheese on top.
2. Cook on high for about 4 hours, or low for 6–7 hours, until the chicken is tender and shreds easily.
3. Shred the chicken and stir the cream cheese through it.
4. Stir in 1 cup cheddar and 1 cup bacon bits until the cheese is melted.

## Notes

- Keto / low-carb. Serve over spinach, on low-carb bread/buns/tortillas, or with a salad.
- Budget keto — cheap chicken base.
