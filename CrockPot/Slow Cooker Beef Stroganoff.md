## Ingredients

- 2 lbs chuck roast, cut into 1-inch cubes
- 1½ cups beef broth
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 8 oz brown mushrooms, sliced
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1 cup sour cream
- 12 oz egg noodles, cooked separately

*Not counted: salt, pepper, garlic powder, cornstarch (to thicken if needed)*

## Instructions

1. Cut chuck roast into 1-inch cubes. Season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
2. Add beef, onion, and mushrooms to slow cooker.
3. Pour in beef broth and Worcestershire sauce. Stir to combine.
4. Cook on LOW for 7–8 hours or HIGH for 4–5 hours until beef is fork tender.
5. Stir in sour cream during the last 30 minutes of cooking. Do not add earlier or it may curdle.
6. If sauce needs thickening, mix 1 tbsp cornstarch with 2 tbsp cold water and stir in during last 30 minutes.
7. Cook egg noodles separately according to package. Serve stroganoff over noodles.

## Notes

- Add sour cream only in the last 30 minutes — high heat causes it to break and turn grainy.
- Chuck roast becomes incredibly tender low and slow — stew beef also works.
- For a thicker sauce, use the cornstarch slurry in the last 30 minutes with the lid off on HIGH.
- Also great over mashed potatoes instead of egg noodles.
