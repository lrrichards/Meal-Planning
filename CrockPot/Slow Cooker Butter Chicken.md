## Ingredients

- 4 chicken breasts (or chicken thighs), cubed
- 14 oz tomato sauce
- 6 oz tomato paste
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1 tbsp minced fresh ginger
- ½ cup plain Greek yogurt (added at end)
- ½ cup half and half (added at end)
- Fresh cilantro to taste (added at end)

*Not counted: minced garlic (1 tbsp), curry powder (½ tbsp), garam masala (1 tbsp), chili powder (1 tsp), salt*

**To serve:** White rice, naan bread, lime wedges

## Instructions

1. Add cubed chicken, tomato sauce, tomato paste, diced onion, 1 tbsp minced garlic, 1 tbsp minced ginger, ½ tbsp curry powder, 1 tbsp garam masala, 1 tsp chili powder, and 1 tsp salt to the slow cooker. Stir well.
2. Cook on low for 5 hours.
3. Stir in Greek yogurt, half and half, and fresh cilantro.
4. Serve over white rice with naan bread and lime wedges.

## Notes

- If you can't find garam masala: substitute a mix of cumin, coriander, black pepper, cinnamon, cloves, and nutmeg.
- Tomato paste tip: open both ends of the can and push through with one lid to get all the paste out.
- Greek yogurt and half and half added at the end keep the sauce creamy without curdling.
- Pairs perfectly with Homemade Naan Bread (see Normal/ folder).
- Skip cilantro if you don't like it — the dish is still complete without it.
