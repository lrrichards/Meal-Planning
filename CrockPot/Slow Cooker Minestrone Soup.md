## Ingredients

- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 3 carrots, sliced
- 3 stalks celery, sliced
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 6 cups veggie broth
- ¼ cup sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
- 1 can (15 oz) kidney beans, drained
- 1 can (15 oz) great northern beans, drained
- 2 zucchini, diced
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- 1 cup ditalini pasta, cooked separately

*Not counted: olive oil, Italian seasoning, salt, pepper, garlic powder, parmesan rind (optional)*

## Instructions

1. Add onion, carrots, celery, diced tomatoes, tomato paste, veggie broth, sun-dried tomatoes, kidney beans, and great northern beans to slow cooker. Stir to combine.
2. Season with Italian seasoning, salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Add parmesan rind if using.
3. Cook on LOW for 7–8 hours or HIGH for 4 hours.
4. Stir in zucchini and fresh spinach during the last 30 minutes.
5. Cook pasta separately and add to individual bowls when serving to prevent it from getting mushy.
6. Remove parmesan rind before serving.

## Notes

- Cook pasta separately and add per bowl — pasta left in the slow cooker for hours turns to mush.
- Sun-dried tomatoes add concentrated, rich tomato flavor that regular diced tomatoes can't match.
- A parmesan rind dropped in while cooking is a classic Italian trick — massive flavor with no extra effort.
- This soup is excellent leftover and freezes well without the pasta.
