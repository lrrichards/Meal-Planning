## Ingredients

- 5 russet potatoes, peeled and diced
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 3 large carrots, sliced
- 1 rib celery, sliced
- 16 oz diced ham (pre-diced is easiest)
- 4½ cups chicken broth
- 1½ cups heavy cream
- ¼ cup all-purpose flour
- ½ cup sour cream
- ¾ cup shredded cheddar cheese

*Not counted: salt (1 tsp), pepper (⅛ tsp), paprika (1 tsp), garlic powder (1 tsp), oregano (⅛ tsp)*

## Instructions

1. Add diced potatoes, onion, carrots, celery, ham, and chicken broth to slow cooker.
2. Season with salt, pepper, paprika, garlic powder, and oregano. Stir.
3. Cook on LOW for 7 hours until potatoes and vegetables are very tender.
4. In a small bowl, whisk heavy cream and flour together until smooth.
5. Stir the cream-flour mixture, sour cream, and shredded cheddar cheese into the slow cooker.
6. Cook on HIGH uncovered, stirring occasionally, for 10 more minutes until soup thickens.
7. Serve hot.

## Notes

- The cream-flour mixture is what transforms this from a thin broth into a thick, creamy chowder.
- Whisk the flour into the cream before adding to the soup — this prevents lumps.
- Serve as-is for a dairy version or skip the cream/sour cream/cheese for a lighter broth soup.
- Great for using up leftover holiday ham — this is one of the best ways to use it.
- Pre-diced ham from the store makes prep very fast.
