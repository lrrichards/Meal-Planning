## Ingredients

- 2 chicken breasts
- 4 oz canned diced green chilies
- 10 oz cream of chicken soup
- 10 oz cream of mushroom soup
- ½ cup chicken broth
- 1½ lbs baby potatoes (red or gold), whole
- 1 lb baby carrots

*Not counted: dry ranch seasoning (3 tbsp + extra for chicken), salt, pepper*

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, whisk together chicken broth, green chilies, cream of chicken soup, cream of mushroom soup, and 3 tbsp dry ranch seasoning.
2. Season chicken breasts on both sides with salt, pepper, and a dash of ranch seasoning.
3. Add baby potatoes and baby carrots to the bottom of the slow cooker.
4. Lay seasoned chicken on top of the vegetables.
5. Pour the sauce mixture over everything.
6. Cover and cook on low for 6 hours.
7. Serve as-is — potatoes and carrots absorb the sauce as you cut into them.

## Notes

- The ranch + green chili combination sounds unusual but is genuinely delicious — give it a try before judging.
- Potatoes absorb the sauce and become the best part of the dish.
- Red or gold baby potatoes both work fine.
- Can serve over rice if you want to stretch the meal further.
