## Ingredients

- 2 lbs chuck roast or chuck tender roast
- 1 cup beef broth
- 8 oz tomato sauce
- 2 white onions, sliced
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar

*Not counted: minced garlic (3 tsp), dried thyme, salt, pepper*

**To serve:** Salad, crusty bread, or as-is

## Instructions

1. Add chuck roast to the slow cooker.
2. Add 3 tsp minced garlic, beef broth, tomato sauce, sliced onions, ½ tsp dried thyme, Worcestershire sauce, and red wine vinegar.
3. Cover and cook on low for 7–8 hours until meat is pull-apart tender.
4. Shred meat and stir into the cooking juices.
5. Serve as-is or alongside a salad.

## Notes

- Chuck tender roast is often a better deal than regular chuck roast and comes out less fatty — look for it.
- The onions caramelize as they slow cook and become the flavor base of the whole dish.
- Worcestershire sauce and red wine vinegar are what give this the French onion character — don't skip either.
- Great with a Caesar salad kit on the side.
