## Ingredients

- 2 lbs chicken breasts
- ½ cup chicken broth
- ¼ cup honey
- ¼ cup Dijon mustard

*Not counted: salt, pepper, garlic powder*

## Instructions

1. Season chicken breasts with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
2. Place chicken in slow cooker.
3. In a small bowl, whisk together chicken broth, honey, and Dijon mustard. Pour over chicken.
4. Cook on LOW for 5–6 hours or HIGH for 2.5–3 hours until chicken is cooked through and tender.
5. Shred or slice chicken and toss with the sauce from the slow cooker.
6. Serve over rice, egg noodles, or with roasted vegetables.

## Notes

- Whole chicken breasts hold up better in the slow cooker than thin-sliced.
- The sauce reduces slightly while cooking and concentrates in flavor — don't add more liquid.
- Add a tablespoon of whole grain mustard in addition to Dijon for more texture and heat.
- Great over mashed potatoes or with steamed broccoli as a simple weeknight meal.
