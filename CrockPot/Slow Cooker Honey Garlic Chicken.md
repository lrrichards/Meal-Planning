## Ingredients

- 1.5–2 lbs chicken tenderloins (or chicken breast or thighs)
- 1/2 cup ketchup
- 1/2 cup low sodium soy sauce
- 1/3 cup honey
- 2 cups white rice

*Not counted: garlic*

## Instructions

1. Mix 2 tbsp minced garlic, ketchup, soy sauce, and honey together in a bowl until combined.
2. Add chicken to the slow cooker. Pour the sauce over the top.
3. Cook on low for 6 hours.
4. Shred the chicken and stir back into the sauce.
5. Serve over white rice. Top with sesame seeds if desired.

## Notes

- All pantry staple ingredients — very easy to keep stocked for a quick dump-and-go night.
- Chicken thighs give the most flavor; tenderloins cook up the most tender.
- The sauce over rice is exceptional — don't skip the rice.
- Pairs well with a bagged salad on the side.
