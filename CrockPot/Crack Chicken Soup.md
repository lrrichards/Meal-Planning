## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lb chicken breast
- 1 oz ranch seasoning
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 block cream cheese
- 6 cups chicken broth
- 2 cups spinach
- 1/2 cup bacon bits
- 1 cup shredded cheese

## Instructions

1. To the crock pot add the chicken breast, ranch seasoning, cumin, paprika, garlic powder, cream cheese, and 6 cups chicken broth.
2. Cook on low about 8 hours.
3. Mash/shred the chicken and stir through.
4. Add 2 cups spinach, 1/2 cup bacon bits, and 1 cup shredded cheese. Stir.
5. Cook another 30 minutes until the spinach wilts.

## Notes

- Crack chicken in soup form — ranch and bacon flavor. Keto / low-carb.
- Distinct from Crack Chicken (this is the brothy soup version).
- Budget keto — cheap chicken base; uses cream cheese (not heavy cream).
