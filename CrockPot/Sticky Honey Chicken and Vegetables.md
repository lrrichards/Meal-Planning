## Ingredients

### Vegetables
- 1½ lbs red potatoes, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 2–3 carrots, sliced
- 12 oz green beans, trimmed

### Chicken & Sauce
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- ⅓ cup ketchup
- ½ cup low sodium soy sauce
- ½ cup honey

*Not counted: minced garlic (1 tbsp), oregano, crushed red pepper flakes*

## Instructions

1. Add potatoes, carrots, and green beans to the slow cooker.
2. Make the sauce: whisk together ketchup, soy sauce, honey, 1 tbsp minced garlic, a dash of oregano, and crushed red pepper flakes.
3. Place chicken thighs over the vegetables. Pour half the sauce over everything. Stir to coat veggies.
4. Bring chicken to the top. Pour remaining sauce over the chicken.
5. Cook on low for 5 hours or high for 3 hours until chicken is cooked through.
6. Serve as-is or over white rice.

## Notes

- This sauce works on sheet pan meals and casseroles too — make a double batch and save some.
- Chicken thighs stay much juicier than breast through 5 hours in the slow cooker.
- Meal preps well — leftovers hold up in the fridge for several days.
- Swap veggies for whatever you have on hand.
