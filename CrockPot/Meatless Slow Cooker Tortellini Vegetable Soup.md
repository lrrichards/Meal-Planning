## Ingredients

- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 5 carrots, sliced
- 4 ribs celery, sliced
- 6½ cups broth (any variety)
- 3 tbsp tomato paste
- 4 cups fresh spinach
- 1 lb cheese tortellini (fresh or frozen — not dry)
- ½ cup heavy cream (optional)
- ⅓ cup Parmesan cheese (optional)

*Not counted: minced garlic (1 tbsp), salt, pepper, thyme, basil, paprika, oregano*

## Instructions

1. Add diced onion, carrots, celery, 1 tbsp minced garlic, broth, tomato paste, and seasonings (salt, pepper, thyme, basil, paprika, oregano) to the slow cooker. Stir well.
2. Cook on low for 6 hours or high for 3 hours.
3. Stir in heavy cream and Parmesan if using.
4. Add fresh spinach and tortellini. Stir.
5. Cook for an additional 15 minutes until tortellini is tender. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Use fresh or frozen tortellini — dry tortellini takes too long and gets mushy.
- Don't add tortellini more than 15 minutes early — it over-cooks quickly.
- Heavy cream and Parmesan are both optional but add richness.
- Add cooked sausage if you want a meatier version.
- Pairs well with crusty bread.
