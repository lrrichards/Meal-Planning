## Ingredients

- 24 oz pasta sauce
- 8 oz cream cheese, cubed
- 1–2 lbs chicken breast
- 12 oz bow tie pasta (or any pasta)
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese (optional)
- 1/3 cup water

*Not counted: salt, pepper, Italian seasoning*

## Instructions

1. Add pasta sauce, cubed cream cheese, water, 1/2 tsp salt, pepper, and 1 tsp Italian seasoning to the slow cooker. Stir together.
2. Add chicken and coat it in the sauce. Cook on low for 5 hours.
3. Remove chicken and give the sauce a good whisk to smooth out the cream cheese.
4. Chop or shred the chicken and return to the slow cooker. Stir into the sauce.
5. Cook bow tie pasta in a pot of boiling salted water per package directions. Drain.
6. Stir cooked pasta into the slow cooker. Add mozzarella if using and let it melt.
7. Serve immediately. Top with fresh basil if desired.

## Notes

- Adding cream cheese at the end (after whisking the sauce) gives a smoother result — but adding it at the start works fine if you'll forget later.
- Any pasta shape works. Rice is also a good substitute.
- Comfort food staple — rich, warm, and filling.
