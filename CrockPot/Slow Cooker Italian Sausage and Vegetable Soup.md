## Ingredients

- 1 lb Italian sausage
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 3 ribs celery, sliced
- 1 zucchini, sliced
- 2 large carrots, sliced
- 4 cups chicken broth (or veggie broth)
- 1 can (15 oz) cannellini beans
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes (petite diced preferred)
- ½ cup parmesan cheese, shredded

*Not counted: garlic (1 tbsp minced), salt (½ tsp), pepper (⅛ tsp), Italian seasoning (1 tsp)*

## Instructions

1. Cook Italian sausage and diced onion in a skillet over medium-high heat, breaking up the sausage. Cook through. Remove excess grease.
2. Add browned sausage to slow cooker.
3. Add chicken broth, minced garlic, cannellini beans, diced tomatoes, celery, zucchini, and carrots.
4. Season with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning. Stir.
5. Cook on LOW for 6–7 hours.
6. Stir in parmesan cheese during the last few minutes of cooking. Let it melt down before serving.

## Notes

- Brown the sausage before adding to the slow cooker — it's 10 minutes but adds significant depth.
- Cannellini beans (white kidney beans) have a creamy texture that works especially well in Italian-style soups.
- Petite diced tomatoes are smaller and easier to eat in a soup context.
- This soup freezes and reheats wonderfully — make a full batch and freeze half.
- Similar to Slow Cooker Minestrone Soup but sausage-based, no pasta, and no sun-dried tomatoes — a heartier, simpler version.
