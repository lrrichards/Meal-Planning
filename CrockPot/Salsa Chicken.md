## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 to 2 lbs chicken breast
- 1 oz taco seasoning
- 1 cup salsa
- To serve: low-carb tortillas, sour cream, shredded cheese

## Instructions

1. Place the chicken breast in the bottom of the crock pot.
2. Add 1 oz taco seasoning on top, then 1 cup salsa.
3. Cook on low about 6 hours (or high 3–4 hours).
4. Mash/shred the chicken and stir it through.
5. Serve on low-carb tortillas with sour cream and cheese.

## Notes

- Keto / low-carb and about as easy as it gets — 3 ingredients.
- **Contains salsa (tomato-based) — contains tomatoes.**
- Budget keto — very inexpensive.
