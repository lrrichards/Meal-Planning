## Ingredients

**Slow Cooker (dump and go):**
- 2+ lbs pork tenderloin
- 1.5 cups chicken broth
- Juice of 1 orange
- Juice of 1 lime

*Not counted: salt, pepper*

**Sandwich assembly:**
- 6 hoagie rolls
- 2 tbsp yellow mustard
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 4 oz dill pickle
- 6 oz deli ham
- 4 oz Swiss cheese

## Instructions

1. Add pork tenderloin to slow cooker. Pour in chicken broth, orange juice, and lime juice. Season with 1/2 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper.
2. Cook on low for 6 hours until pork is pull-apart tender.
3. Shred pork directly in the slow cooker juices so it absorbs the flavor.
4. Preheat oven to 400°F. Slice hoagie rolls and spread mustard on one side, mayonnaise on the other.
5. Layer pickles, ham, and shredded pork on the rolls. Add Swiss cheese on top.
6. Place on a sheet pan and bake at 400°F for about 5 minutes until cheese is melted and everything is warmed through.
7. Serve immediately — pairs well with a Caesar salad.

## Notes

- Shred the pork in the juices — this is what keeps it flavorful and moist.
- Colosseum or Claussen pickles recommended for best flavor.
- Serve with some of the slow cooker juices on the side for dipping.
