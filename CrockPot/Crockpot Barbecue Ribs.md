## Ingredients

- 2–3 lbs pork ribs (baby back or St. Louis style)
- 1 cup barbecue sauce (plus more for finishing)
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar

*Not counted: salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika, onion powder, brown sugar (dry rub)*

## Instructions

1. Remove membrane from the back of ribs if present. Cut rack into sections to fit slow cooker.
2. Mix dry rub: salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika, onion powder, and a little brown sugar. Rub all over ribs.
3. Stand rib sections upright along the inside walls of the slow cooker (bone side out, meat side against the wall).
4. In a small bowl, mix barbecue sauce and apple cider vinegar. Pour over ribs.
5. Cook on LOW for 7–8 hours until ribs are tender and nearly falling off the bone.
6. Optional: transfer ribs to a foil-lined baking sheet, brush with additional barbecue sauce, and broil 3–5 minutes to caramelize the sauce.

## Notes

- Standing the ribs upright is the trick — they fit in even a standard slow cooker this way.
- Apple cider vinegar cuts through the sweetness of the barbecue sauce and tenderizes the meat.
- The broil step at the end is optional but highly recommended for a sticky, caramelized finish.
- Use your favorite barbecue sauce — sweet, smoky, or spicy all work.
