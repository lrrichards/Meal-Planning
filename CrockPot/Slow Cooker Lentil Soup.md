## Ingredients

- 4 carrots, sliced
- 3 ribs celery, sliced
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 6 cups vegetable broth
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes (petite diced preferred)
- 1 can (14 oz) Italian style diced tomatoes
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1½ cups brown or green lentils, rinsed
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar

*Not counted: garlic (1 tbsp minced), brown sugar (1 tbsp), salt, pepper*

## Instructions

1. Add sliced carrots, celery, diced onion, and minced garlic to slow cooker.
2. Add brown sugar — this adds subtle depth and background flavor.
3. Pour in vegetable broth, both cans of diced tomatoes, and tomato paste.
4. Rinse lentils in a mesh strainer to remove any debris and excess starch. Add to slow cooker.
5. Add balsamic vinegar. Stir everything together.
6. Cook on LOW for about 7 hours.
7. Stir and serve.

## Notes

- Rinse the lentils before adding — removes dirt, debris, and starch that causes foaming.
- Use brown or green lentils — red lentils dissolve into the soup rather than holding their shape.
- Balsamic vinegar adds a subtle tang and complexity that makes this soup feel restaurant-quality.
- Two types of diced tomatoes (plain + Italian style) layer the tomato flavor.
- Lentils are high in protein and fiber, very affordable, and make this an incredibly filling meatless meal.
- Excellent leftover and freezes well for future meals.
