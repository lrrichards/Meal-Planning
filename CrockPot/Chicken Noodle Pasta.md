## Ingredients

- 2–3 chicken breasts
- 2 cans (10 oz each) cream of chicken soup
- 3¾ cups chicken broth
- 9 oz uncooked egg noodles

*Not counted: salt, pepper, Italian seasoning, paprika*

**Optional add-ins:** 2–3 sliced carrots, 2 ribs sliced celery (add at the start with the chicken)

## Instructions

1. Add chicken breasts to the slow cooker.
2. Pour in cream of chicken soup and chicken broth.
3. Season with salt, pepper, Italian seasoning, and paprika.
4. Cover and cook on low for 6 hours.
5. Remove chicken, shred, and return to the slow cooker.
6. Add uncooked egg noodles. Cook on high for about 35 minutes, or until noodles are tender. Do not overcook — check at 25 minutes.
7. Stir and serve.

## Notes

- Don't let the noodles cook past tender — they go mushy fast in the slow cooker.
- Add sliced carrots and celery at the start for a more classic chicken noodle soup feel.
- Kid-friendly and family-approved — one of those go-to cozy weeknight dinners.
- Stores well in the fridge for leftovers.
