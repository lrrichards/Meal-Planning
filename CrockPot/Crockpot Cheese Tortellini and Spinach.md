## Ingredients

- 18 oz refrigerated cheese tortellini
- 1 cup fresh spinach
- 14 oz canned diced tomatoes
- 1.5 cups chicken broth
- 8 oz cream cheese, cubed
- 1 cup shredded cheese

*Not counted: salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, Italian seasoning*

## Instructions

1. Add tortellini, spinach, diced tomatoes, chicken broth, cream cheese, and shredded cheese to the slow cooker.
2. Season with 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder, and 1 tsp Italian seasoning.
3. Gently stir — refrigerated tortellini is fragile and breaks easily.
4. Cook on high for 1 hour or low for 2–2.5 hours.
5. Stir gently and serve.

## Notes

- Use refrigerated tortellini only — dried tortellini won't work the same way and needs much longer.
- Stir gently throughout — tortellini breaks easily, especially toward the end of cooking.
- One of the fastest slow cooker meals in the vault at only 1–2 hours.
- Quick meatless dump-and-go for busy days.
