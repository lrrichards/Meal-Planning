## Ingredients

- 1 pork shoulder (about 3–4 lb)
- Salt
- Pepper

## Instructions

1. Place the pork shoulder in the slow cooker. Cover generously with salt and pepper.
2. Do NOT add any liquid — the pork releases plenty of its own.
3. Cover and cook on low 6–8 hours (or high 4–6 hours) until cooked through and shreds easily.
4. Shred the pork. Pour the released cooking juices back over it to keep it moist.

## Notes

- A cheap protein base for the whole keto week — feeds many meals: pulled pork sandwiches, zucchini stir fry, pork and green beans, and pulled pork pizza.
- If you have sugar-free barbecue sauce, mix some in for flavor.
- From KetoFocus extreme-budget $40 keto week.
