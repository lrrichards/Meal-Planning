## Ingredients

- 1½ lbs chicken breast
- ½ cup sun-dried tomatoes (oil-packed with seasonings recommended)
- 16 oz Alfredo sauce
- ¼ cup water

*Not counted: salt, pepper*

**To serve:** Pasta, rice, veggies, or your choice

## Instructions

1. Add chicken breasts to the slow cooker. Season with salt and pepper.
2. Add sun-dried tomatoes and pour Alfredo sauce over top.
3. Add ¼ cup water (rinse the Alfredo jar to get the last of the sauce).
4. Cover and cook on low for 5–6 hours.
5. Shred chicken directly in the slow cooker and stir into the sauce.
6. Serve over pasta, rice, or vegetables.

## Notes

- Oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes with added seasonings give more flavor than plain dry ones.
- Rinse the Alfredo jar with the ¼ cup water to get all the sauce out.
- The shredded chicken absorbs the tomato-Alfredo sauce beautifully — stir well after shredding.
- Serve over egg noodles or penne for a classic feel.
