## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef (or ground turkey, sausage, or meat substitute)
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 1 head green cabbage, chopped
- 15 oz tomato sauce
- 28 oz crushed tomatoes
- 3½ cups beef broth
- ½ cup uncooked white rice

*Not counted: Worcestershire sauce (1 tbsp), minced garlic (1 tbsp), brown sugar (3 tbsp), paprika (1 tsp), bay leaf, dried thyme (½ tsp), salt, pepper*

## Instructions

1. In a pan over medium heat, brown ground beef and diced onion together. Drain excess grease. Transfer to slow cooker.
2. Add Worcestershire sauce, 1 tbsp minced garlic, tomato sauce, crushed tomatoes, beef broth, chopped cabbage, carrots, 3 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tsp paprika, 1 bay leaf, ½ tsp dried thyme, 1½ tsp salt, and ½ tsp pepper.
3. Stir to combine.
4. **Best method:** Cook on low for 7 hours, then add rice and cook 1 more hour until rice holds its shape.
5. **If you'll be gone all day:** Add rice at the beginning with everything else. It still works — the rice just won't hold its shape as well.
6. Remove bay leaf. Stir and serve.

## Notes

- Brown sugar is what makes this — don't skip it.
- Adding rice in the last hour gives better texture, but adding it at the start still works fine.
- Makes a huge batch — great for multiple nights of leftovers.
- Ground turkey or chicken are lighter alternatives to ground beef.
