## Ingredients

- ½ yellow onion, diced
- 14 oz canned diced tomatoes (fire roasted adds more flavor)
- 4 oz canned diced green chilies
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1 large beef rump roast (or flank steak)

*Not counted: cayenne pepper, salt, pepper, onion powder, garlic powder, oil*

**To serve:** Corn or flour tortillas, shredded cheese, lime wedges, guacamole, avocado

## Instructions

1. Add diced onion, diced tomatoes, green chilies, and lime juice to the bottom of the slow cooker. Stir to combine.
2. Place beef roast on top (can be slightly frozen — it will still cook fine).
3. Season meat with cayenne pepper, ½ tsp salt, pepper, onion powder, and garlic powder.
4. Cover and cook on low for 7 hours.
5. Remove meat and shred with two forks or a meat masher.
6. Return shredded meat to the slow cooker and stir into the juices.
7. Optional: Crisp tortillas in a hot oiled pan for 2 minutes per side before serving.
8. Serve in tortillas with cheese, lime, and desired toppings.

## Notes

- Fire roasted diced tomatoes add extra flavor — use them if available.
- Flank steak works if you can't find or don't want rump roast.
- Meat can go in still slightly frozen — the 7-hour cook time handles it.
- Crisping tortillas in a pan beats the microwave by a lot — worth the extra few minutes.
- Leftovers freeze well — make a big batch and store extras.
