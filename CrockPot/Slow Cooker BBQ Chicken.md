## Ingredients

- 2 lbs chicken breasts
- ½ cup Italian dressing
- ½ cup barbecue sauce

*Not counted: salt, pepper*

## Instructions

1. Season chicken breasts with salt and pepper.
2. Place in slow cooker.
3. Pour Italian dressing and barbecue sauce over chicken. Toss to coat.
4. Cook on LOW for 5–6 hours or HIGH for 2.5–3 hours until chicken shreds easily.
5. Shred chicken directly in the slow cooker and toss with the sauce.
6. Serve on sandwiches, over rice, on baked potatoes, or in tacos.

## Notes

- Italian dressing is the secret — the acid tenderizes the chicken while the herbs add flavor.
- This is one of the easiest slow cooker recipes possible and tastes better than the effort suggests.
- Serve on toasted buns as BBQ chicken sandwiches — crowd-pleaser for a large group.
- Works with chicken thighs too — even more forgiving in the slow cooker.
