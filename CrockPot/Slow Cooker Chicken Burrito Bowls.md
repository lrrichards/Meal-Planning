## Ingredients

- 1–2 lbs chicken breast
- 1 can corn, drained
- 1 can Rotel (diced tomatoes with green chilies)
- 1 can black beans, drained
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- Juice of 1 lime
- White rice for serving

*Not counted: garlic, taco seasoning*

## Instructions

1. Add chicken breast to slow cooker. Add drained corn, Rotel, drained black beans, diced onion, 1 tbsp minced garlic, 1.5 tbsp taco seasoning, and lime juice. Stir.
2. Cook on low for 6 hours.
3. Shred chicken and stir back into everything.
4. Serve over white rice with sour cream, guacamole, shredded cheese, or any taco toppings.

## Notes

- Makes a huge batch — great for lunch leftovers all week.
- Customize freely: add diced green chilies, jalapeños, bell peppers, or swap black beans for pinto beans.
- For a fajita feel, add sliced bell peppers before cooking.
