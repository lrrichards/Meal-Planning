## Ingredients

- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1 yellow bell pepper, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 2 zucchinis, sliced
- 14 oz canned diced tomatoes
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken thighs

*Not counted: minced garlic (1 tbsp), salt, pepper, dried thyme, paprika, oregano*

**To serve:** White rice

## Instructions

1. Add diced onion, bell peppers, zucchini, 1 tbsp minced garlic, and diced tomatoes to the slow cooker.
2. Place chicken thighs on top. Season with 1½ tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper, ½ tsp dried thyme, ½ tsp paprika, and ½ tsp oregano. Give everything a stir.
3. Cook on low for 5 hours or high for 3 hours.
4. Serve over white rice.

## Notes

- Use any color bell peppers — mix and match with whatever's on hand.
- Zucchini can be substituted with cucumber in a pinch — it cooks similarly.
- Great for meal prep — store in containers for easy weekday lunches or dinners.
- Chicken thighs are recommended over breast for juiciness in the slow cooker.
