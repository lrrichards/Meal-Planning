## Ingredients

- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 4 cups broth (veggie, chicken, or beef)
- 10 oz diced tomatoes with green chilies (Rotel)
- 1 can (6 oz) tomato paste
- 15 oz canned diced tomatoes
- 15 oz canned kidney beans, drained
- 15 oz canned chili beans, undrained
- ½ lb elbow pasta

*Not counted: minced garlic (1 tbsp), taco seasoning (2½ tbsp), salt, pepper*

**To serve:** Shredded cheese, sour cream, guacamole, lime wedges

## Instructions

1. Add diced onion, 1 tbsp minced garlic, broth, diced tomatoes with green chilies, tomato paste, diced tomatoes, kidney beans, chili beans, and 2½ tbsp taco seasoning to the slow cooker. Stir to combine.
2. Cover and cook on low for 6 hours.
3. Add uncooked elbow pasta. Cook for about 15 minutes until pasta is tender — check at 10 minutes to avoid mushiness.
4. Serve topped with cheese, sour cream, guacamole, and lime.

## Notes

- Elbow pasta cooks better in the slow cooker than most other shapes — stick with it.
- Watch the pasta closely after adding — it goes mushy quickly if left too long.
- Keep chili beans undrained — that liquid is part of the sauce.
- Tomato paste tip: open both ends of the can and push one lid through to get all the paste out cleanly.
- Fully meatless but still hearty — beans carry the protein load.
