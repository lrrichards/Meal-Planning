## Ingredients

- 1 lb Italian sausage (hot or mild), browned and crumbled
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 4–5 russet potatoes, thinly sliced or cubed
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 2–3 cups kale, roughly chopped
- 1 cup heavy cream

*Not counted: salt, pepper, red pepper flakes, garlic powder*

## Instructions

1. Brown and crumble sausage in a skillet over medium heat. Drain excess fat.
2. Add browned sausage, diced onion, and potatoes to slow cooker.
3. Pour in chicken broth. Season with salt, pepper, red pepper flakes, and garlic powder.
4. Cook on LOW for 6–7 hours or HIGH for 3–4 hours until potatoes are very tender.
5. Stir in kale and heavy cream during the last 30 minutes of cooking.
6. Taste and adjust seasoning. Serve hot.

## Notes

- Brown the sausage first — it takes 10 minutes and adds significant depth of flavor.
- Kale added at the end stays vibrant and slightly textured instead of turning mushy.
- Hot Italian sausage makes this soup — use mild if cooking for kids.
- Add a parmesan rind to the slow cooker while cooking for an extra layer of flavor.
- See also: Zuppa Toscana Casserole (Casserole/ folder) for a baked oven version.
