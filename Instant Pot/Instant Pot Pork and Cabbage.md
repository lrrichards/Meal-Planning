## Ingredients

- About 2 lb thin-cut boneless pork chops
- 1/2 head cabbage, cored and thinly sliced
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, juice and all
- 1 cup bone broth or water
- Bacon grease, for searing
- Old Bay seasoning
- 2 tsp – 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar

## Instructions

1. Set the Instant Pot to sauté and heat a little bacon grease. Sear the pork chops a couple minutes per side (don't cook through), working in batches. Set aside.
2. Hit cancel to stop the sauté. Return all the pork chops with their juices.
3. Pile the sliced cabbage on top. Season well with Old Bay.
4. Pour in the whole can of diced tomatoes and 1 cup bone broth or water. Add the apple cider vinegar.
5. Lid on, set to slow cook for 6 hours.
6. Stir and serve — the pork falls apart. Spoon some of the juice over each plate.

## Notes

- **Contains tomatoes — not Evan-safe.** Sub the diced tomatoes out if cooking for Evan.
- Old Bay is usually for seafood but tastes great on pork and cabbage.
- No-liquid slow cooker version: do the sear steps in a pan first, then transfer everything to the slow cooker.
- From a $70/week keto-on-a-budget week (2 people, 7 days).
