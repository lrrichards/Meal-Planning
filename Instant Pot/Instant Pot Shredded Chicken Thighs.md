## Ingredients

- About 4 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 1 cup water or chicken broth (broth tastes better)
- 1 tsp salt

## Instructions

1. Add the chicken thighs to the Instant Pot. Pour in 1 cup water or chicken broth and add 1 tsp salt.
2. Pressure cook on high for 10 minutes, then let it release naturally for 10 minutes.
3. Shred the chicken (an electric mixer makes it fast), or cube/leave whole as you like.
4. Save the broth from the pot — use it in the chicken and cauliflower rice soup.

## Instructions (from frozen)

- Cook straight from frozen by adding 5 minutes to the pressure cook time (15 minutes total).

## Notes

- A cheap protein base for the keto week — feeds chicken salad, chicken and cauliflower rice soup, and creamy chicken casserole.
- Frozen thighs in the bag are cheaper than fresh.
- From KetoFocus extreme-budget $40 keto week.
