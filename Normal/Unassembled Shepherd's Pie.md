## Ingredients

### Mashed Potatoes
- 2–3 lbs russet potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks
- ½ cup milk
- 1 cup water (for Instant Pot)

### Shepherd's Pie Filling
- ½ lb ground turkey (or ground beef)
- ½ yellow onion, diced
- 2–3 ribs celery, diced
- Half a 16 oz bag of carrots, diced
- 6 oz (about half a bag) frozen sweet peas
- ½ cup water

*Not counted: olive oil, beef stew seasoning packet, salt, pepper*

## Instructions

### Make the Mashed Potatoes (Instant Pot)
1. Peel and cube russet potatoes. Add to Instant Pot with 1 cup water.
2. Pressure cook for 10 minutes. Release pressure.
3. Do not drain — add ½ cup milk, salt, and pepper.
4. Mash with a hand mixer or potato masher until fluffy and creamy.

### Make the Filling
1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet or pan over medium heat.
2. Add ground turkey, celery, carrots, onion, and frozen peas.
3. Add half a packet of beef stew seasoning mix (about ¾ oz) and ½ cup water.
4. Break up the meat and cook through, stirring every couple of minutes. Cook until meat is done and veggies are tender and slightly caramelized.

### Serve
1. Scoop mashed potatoes into a bowl or onto a plate.
2. Top with a scoop or two of the shepherd's pie filling.
3. Sprinkle with shredded cheese if available.

## Notes

- "Unassembled" = same filling as traditional shepherd's pie, served over mashed potatoes in a bowl instead of baked in a casserole dish — one fewer dish to wash.
- Ground turkey or ground beef both work — use whichever is on hand.
- The beef stew seasoning mix is the flavor driver here — don't skip it.
- Mashed potatoes in the Instant Pot are hands-off and come out perfectly creamy.
- Traditionally called "cottage pie" when made with ground meat instead of sliced lamb.
