## Ingredients

- 1½ cups all-purpose flour (plus more for kneading)
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 2 tsp baking powder
- Pinch of salt

**Optional garlic butter topping:**
- Melted butter
- Fresh parsley, minced
- Minced garlic

*Not counted: salt, butter, minced garlic*

## Instructions

1. Combine flour, Greek yogurt, baking powder, and a pinch of salt in a stand mixer or large bowl. Mix until a dough ball forms.
2. Turn dough out onto a floured surface. Knead a few times — no long kneading needed. This dough does not need to rise (no yeast).
3. Divide dough in half, then divide each half into 3 pieces — 6 pieces total.
4. Roll each piece out as thin as possible without it falling apart.
5. Heat a pan over medium-high to high heat (no oil needed).
6. Cook each piece for 2–3 minutes per side until bubbly and golden brown.
7. Optional: Brush with melted butter mixed with minced garlic and fresh parsley while warm.

## Notes

- No yeast, no rise time — the baking powder does all the work.
- Cook on high heat so the naan gets properly bubbly — medium heat won't give the same result.
- Garlic butter topping makes these restaurant-level good.
- Pairs perfectly with Slow Cooker Butter Chicken (see CrockPot/ folder).
- 3 ingredients, done in under 30 minutes total.
