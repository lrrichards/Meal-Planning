## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs chicken breast (rotisserie works great as a shortcut)
- 6 slices bacon
- 14 oz pasta (any shape)
- 3 cups heavy cream
- 3/4 cup Parmesan cheese
- 1/4 cup chicken broth or water

*Not counted: garlic, Italian seasoning, salt, pepper*

## Instructions

1. **Cook chicken:** Use rotisserie chicken for convenience, or cook frozen/fresh chicken in an Instant Pot on high pressure for 25 minutes with a splash of water, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Chop into small pieces.
2. **Cook bacon:** Fry bacon in a large pan until crispy. Remove to a plate. Leave bacon grease in the pan.
3. **Cook pasta:** Boil pasta in a separate pot per package directions. Drain and set aside.
4. **Make sauce:** In the bacon grease pan over medium heat, add 1 tbsp minced garlic and a sprinkle of Italian seasoning. Stir 30 seconds. Add chopped chicken and stir to coat in the bacon grease.
5. Pour in heavy cream and chicken broth. Add salt and pepper. Bring to a simmer and cook 2–3 minutes until slightly thickened.
6. Add Parmesan cheese and stir until melted.
7. Add drained pasta and cooked bacon. Stir everything together and serve.
8. Pairs well with steamed broccoli on the side.

## Notes

- Rotisserie chicken cuts the cooking time significantly and works perfectly here.
- Cooking chicken in bacon grease is what elevates this from basic Alfredo — don't wipe the pan.
- Also appears in the *Make It Simple* cookbook.
- Uses 3 cooking vessels — not a true one-pot meal despite the source video's category.
