## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 3 packets ramen noodles (seasoning packets NOT used — set aside for another recipe)
- 3/4 cup salsa (any variety)
- 6 oz cream cheese, cubed
- 1 can beans, drained (kidney, black, or pinto)
- 1 cup Mexican-style shredded cheese

*Not counted: water, taco seasoning*

## Instructions

1. Cook ground beef in a large pan over medium heat, breaking it up until cooked through.
2. Meanwhile, boil ramen noodles in a pot of water per package directions (do NOT add seasoning packets). Drain and set aside.
3. Add salsa, 3 tbsp water, 1.5 tbsp taco seasoning, and cubed cream cheese to the cooked beef. Stir on low heat until cream cheese melts completely.
4. Add drained beans and shredded cheese. Stir until cheese melts.
5. Pour the taco mixture over the drained ramen noodles. Toss to combine.
6. Serve topped with extra cheese, sliced green onions, and fresh tomatoes.

## Notes

- The ramen seasoning packets are not used here — save them for ramen soup or stir fry.
- Like a taco mac but with ramen noodles — similar concept, different texture.
- Any canned beans work — kidney, black, or pinto all taste great here.
- Cream cheese is what makes this rich — don't skip it.
