## Ingredients

- 1 can corn, drained
- 2 zucchinis, sliced into small pieces
- Elbow pasta (or any small pasta)
- 6 oz cream cheese, cubed
- 1 cup Parmesan cheese
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- 3/4 cup reserved pasta water

*Not counted: olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder*

## Instructions

1. Bring a pot of salted water to boil. Cook elbow pasta according to package directions. Before draining, scoop out 3/4 cup of pasta water and set aside.
2. While pasta cooks, heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add drained corn and sliced zucchini.
3. Season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder. Cook without stirring too much to let the veggies brown and caramelize — about 5–7 minutes.
4. Once veggies are browned, add the reserved pasta water to the pan. Scrape up any browned bits from the bottom.
5. Add cubed cream cheese and stir until melted and combined into a sauce.
6. Add drained pasta and Parmesan cheese. Stir to coat everything well.
7. Squeeze in the juice of half a lemon. Stir and serve.

## Notes

- The pasta water is the secret — the starch in it thickens the sauce and makes it cling to the pasta. Plain water won't work the same way.
- Browning (caramelizing) the corn and zucchini is essential for flavor — don't rush this step.
- Add cooked chicken to make it a chicken veggie pasta.
- Tastes significantly more expensive than it is.
