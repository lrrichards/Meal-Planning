## Ingredients

- Cooked shredded pork (see Slow Cooker Pulled Pork)
- 1 can green beans
- Shredded cheddar cheese

## Instructions

1. Warm the shredded pork through.
2. Heat the can of green beans and split evenly four ways.
3. Plate the pork with the green beans and sprinkle a few shreds of cheddar on top.

## Notes

- The simplest dinner of the keto week — leans entirely on the pre-cooked pulled pork.
- From KetoFocus extreme-budget $40 keto week.
