## Ingredients

- Keto Cheese Chaffle Bread (used as the pizza crust)
- About 2 oz cream cheese, softened
- Shredded mozzarella or cheddar cheese
- Cooked shredded pork (see Slow Cooker Pulled Pork)
- Leftover vegetables (optional — spinach, zucchini, green onion)

## Instructions

1. Soften the cream cheese in the microwave for a few seconds.
2. Spread the cream cheese over the chaffle bread to form a base.
3. Sprinkle on shredded cheese, then top with the pulled pork. Add any leftover veg.
4. Broil in the oven until the cheese melts and bubbles.

## Notes

- Uses the chaffle bread as a low-carb pizza crust.
- From KetoFocus extreme-budget $40 keto week.
