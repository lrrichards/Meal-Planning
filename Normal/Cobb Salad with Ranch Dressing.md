## Ingredients

**Homemade Ranch Dressing**
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 2 tbsp milk
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 2–3 tbsp ranch seasoning

**Salad**
- 4–5 strips bacon
- 5 chicken tenderloins
- 2 cups lettuce
- 1/2 cup frozen peas, thawed
- Tomatoes, diced
- 1/2 red onion
- 1 avocado, sliced
- Shredded cheese to taste

*Not counted: ranch seasoning, paprika*

## Instructions

1. **Make the dressing:** Combine buttermilk, milk, sour cream, and ranch seasoning in a measuring cup or jar. Stir well. Refrigerate while you prep everything else.
2. **Cook bacon:** Add bacon strips to a pan over medium heat. Cook until crispy. Remove to a paper towel-lined plate to drain.
3. **Cook chicken:** Season tenderloins with ranch seasoning and paprika. Cook in the same pan (using the bacon drippings) a few minutes per side until cooked through. Let rest, then chop into small pieces.
4. **Assemble:** Start with a bed of romaine. Top with peas, tomatoes, red onion, chicken, avocado, crumbled bacon, and cheese.
5. Drizzle ranch dressing over the top and serve.

## Notes

- Cooking the chicken in bacon drippings adds great flavor — don't wipe the pan.
- Kids may prefer everything served separately on a plate rather than assembled as a salad.
- Thawed frozen peas on a salad are underrated — worth trying.
- Trader Joe's ranch seasoning is highly recommended if available.
