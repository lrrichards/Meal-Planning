## Ingredients

- 1 can biscuits
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- 1/3 cup ketchup
- 1/3 cup sour cream
- Parsley, garlic salt

## Instructions

1. Heat oven per the biscuit can (about 375°F). Dice the onion and cook it in a hot oiled pan.
2. Add the ground beef and brown it. Soak up excess grease with a paper towel.
3. Stir in about 1/3 cup ketchup and 1/3 cup sour cream, plus parsley and a little garlic salt. Mix well — it tastes like a sloppy joe.
4. Open the biscuits, lay them out, and flatten each one.
5. Scoop the meat onto each flattened biscuit and add a sprinkle of cheese.
6. Gather and pinch the biscuit closed around the filling, rolling into a ball.
7. Bake about 15 minutes, until golden and cooked through.

## Notes

- Tastes like a sloppy joe in a biscuit. Mr. Da's recipe skips cheese and onion — this version adds them.
- Good with a dipping sauce: garlic butter or ketchup.
- Not keto (biscuits).
- "Poor man's comfort food" follower edition.
