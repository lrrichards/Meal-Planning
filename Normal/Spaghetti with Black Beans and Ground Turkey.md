## Ingredients

- ½ lb ground turkey (or ground beef)
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 16 oz shell pasta (or any pasta)
- 24 oz jar pasta sauce

*Not counted: salt, pepper, oregano, garlic, onion*

## Instructions

1. Cook pasta in a large pot of boiling salted water until tender. Drain.
2. In a separate pan, cook ground turkey over medium heat, breaking it up until cooked through. Add garlic and onion if available.
3. Add half the jar of pasta sauce to a blender along with the drained black beans. Blend until smooth.
4. Pour blended sauce into the pan with ground turkey. Add the remaining pasta sauce and stir to combine.
5. Season with salt, pepper, and oregano to taste.
6. Add cooked pasta to the sauce and toss to combine. Serve.

## Notes

- Blending the black beans into the sauce makes them completely undetectable — no bean texture, just added protein and fiber.
- This stretches ½ lb of meat to feed a crowd — the beans fill in the gap.
- Blending half the sauce with the beans and adding the rest straight gives the right sauce-to-pasta ratio with a full 16 oz of pasta.
- Any pasta works — shells, spaghetti, penne, rotini.
- Any canned bean works: kidney beans, white beans, or pinto.
