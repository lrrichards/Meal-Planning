## Ingredients

For the meatloaf cupcakes:
- 1 lb ground pork + 1 lb ground beef
- 1 onion, diced small
- 4 eggs
- 2 tbsp oregano
- 2 tbsp thyme
- Garlic salt, pepper
- 2 cups panko breadcrumbs
- 1/2 cup ketchup
- Shredded cheese

For the mashed potato topping:
- Potatoes, peeled and diced
- Butter
- Milk
- Sour cream
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 350°F. Mix the ground pork and beef with the diced onion, 4 eggs, oregano, thyme, garlic salt, pepper, 2 cups panko, 1/2 cup ketchup, and cheese. Mix well by hand.
2. Grease a muffin tin and press the meat mixture into each cup.
3. Bake 20–25 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, boil the potatoes until soft. Drain, then mash with a little butter, milk, and sour cream. Don't make them too loose. Season to taste.
5. Pipe or spoon the mashed potatoes on top of each meatloaf cupcake.
6. Broil about 10 minutes until the potato tops are golden.

## Notes

- Individual meatloaf "cupcakes" topped with mashed potatoes — like personal shepherd's pies.
- Distinct from Sheet Pan Mini Meatloaves (that's sheet-pan with roasted potatoes and green beans).
- Not keto (panko + potatoes).
- Aldi "poor man's comfort food."
