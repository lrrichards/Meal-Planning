## Ingredients

- 1 lb Italian sausage (sweet Italian recommended)
- 3/4 lb elbow pasta
- 3 oz cream cheese
- 14 oz petite diced tomatoes (canned)
- 16 oz heavy cream
- 2 cups baby spinach
- 1 cup Parmesan cheese

## Instructions

1. Cook sausage in a large pan over medium heat, breaking it up as it cooks. Cook through completely.
2. Meanwhile, bring a pot of salted water to a boil and cook elbow pasta according to package directions.
3. To the cooked sausage, add cream cheese, diced tomatoes, and heavy cream. Stir until cream cheese melts.
4. Bring sauce to a simmer and let it cook a few minutes to start thickening.
5. Add baby spinach and Parmesan cheese. Stir and simmer a few more minutes.
6. Drain pasta and add it directly into the sauce. Stir and simmer 2–3 more minutes until sauce thickens and coats the pasta.
7. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Petite diced tomatoes work better than regular — smaller pieces blend into the sauce more evenly.
- Great leftover meal — sauce gets even better the next day.
- Sweet Italian sausage gives the best flavor, but hot Italian works for a kick.
