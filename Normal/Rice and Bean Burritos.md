## Ingredients

- 1 bag quick-cooking Spanish style rice mix (Great Value brand works well)
- 1 bag dry refried bean mix
- Flour tortillas (large burrito size)
- Shredded cheese to taste
- Water (per package instructions for both rice and beans)

*Not counted: oil*

## Instructions

1. Cook Spanish rice according to bag directions — typically just add water and simmer a few minutes.
2. Cook refried beans according to bag directions — about 2 cups of water (less water = thicker beans; stay at or under 2 cups).
3. To assemble: lay out a tortilla. Add cheese, rice, and beans down the center.
4. Heat a pan over medium heat with a small amount of oil. Place assembled burrito seam-side down and cook until the tortilla is golden and crispy on each side.
5. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Crisping the tortilla in oil gives it a restaurant-style taste — don't skip this step.
- Dry refried bean mix (not canned) gives a better texture — often found at Walmart.
- Adjust bean consistency by adding less water for thicker beans.
- Easy to scale up for a crowd — just cook more rice and beans.
