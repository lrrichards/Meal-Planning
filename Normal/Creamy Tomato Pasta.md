## Ingredients

- 1 can (15 oz) tomato sauce
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- ¾ cup heavy cream
- 12 oz penne pasta
- ½ cup parmesan cheese, shredded

*Not counted: olive oil, butter, Italian seasoning, red pepper flakes, salt, pepper, garlic powder*

## Instructions

1. Cook penne in a large pot of salted boiling water until al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta water before draining.
2. While pasta cooks, heat olive oil and a pat of butter in a skillet over medium heat.
3. Add tomato paste and cook, stirring, for 2 minutes to deepen the flavor.
4. Pour in tomato sauce. Season with Italian seasoning, red pepper flakes, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Simmer 5–7 minutes.
5. Stir in heavy cream. Simmer 3–4 minutes until sauce is pink and slightly thickened.
6. Toss drained pasta with the sauce, adding pasta water a splash at a time to reach desired consistency.
7. Serve topped with parmesan cheese.

## Notes

- Cooking the tomato paste for a couple minutes before adding sauce removes the raw, sharp edge.
- This is a simplified Italian rosa sauce — tomato plus cream. Quick, elegant, meatless.
- Add shrimp, sausage, or chicken for a protein version.
- Pasta water is key — the starch helps the sauce cling to the pasta.
