## Ingredients

- 1 bag (20 oz) frozen stir fry veggie blend
- 3 packets ramen noodles (use only 1 seasoning packet)
- 1/4 cup low sodium soy sauce
- 4 tbsp water

*Not counted: olive oil*

## Instructions

1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add frozen stir fry veggies.
2. Cook 7–8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until veggies are tender and starting to brown on the outside.
3. Meanwhile, cook ramen noodles in a pot of boiling water until tender. Drain.
4. Add drained ramen to the veggie pan. Add soy sauce, water, and 1 ramen seasoning packet. Toss everything together well.
5. Serve topped with sliced green onions and sesame seeds.

## Notes

- Only 3 ingredients — one of the simplest meals in the vault.
- Browning the veggies before adding the ramen is what gives this dish real flavor — don't rush it.
- Only 1 of the 3 seasoning packets is used — save the others.
- Also appears in the *Make It Simple* cookbook, page 228.
- Add cooked chicken, shrimp, or a fried egg on top for extra protein.
