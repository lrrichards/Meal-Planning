## Ingredients

- 12 eggs
- 12 slices deli ham (cut in half to make it stretch)
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- Cooking spray

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 400°F. Spray a 12-cup muffin tin (or paper liners) with cooking spray.
2. Press a half-slice of deli ham into each muffin cup to form a little cup.
3. Sprinkle a little shredded cheddar into each.
4. Crack one egg on top of each.
5. Bake 12–15 minutes until the eggs are set.

## Notes

- Two cups make a serving. Great for meal prep — keeps for a couple of mornings in the fridge.
- Also works as a snack or a quick lunch, not just breakfast.
