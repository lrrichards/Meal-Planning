## Ingredients

- 8 eggs
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 block (about 4 oz) mozzarella cheese, shredded
- 1/2 (4 oz) package spinach
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, pepper, and any seasoning you like

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 375°F.
2. Sauté the diced onion until cooked. Add the spinach and cook until wilted. Take off the heat.
3. In a bowl, whisk the eggs with the heavy cream and shredded mozzarella. Season with salt, pepper, or your seasoning.
4. Brush a glass pie plate with avocado oil. Pour in the egg mixture, then add the spinach and onion and stir to combine.
5. Bake about 30 minutes, until the top is golden with a slight sheen.
6. Let cool 10 minutes before slicing (it'll deflate a little — normal).

## Notes

- Breakfast-for-dinner — makes enough for leftovers as next-day lunch.
- Want more protein? Add a pound of bacon or ground beef.
- From a $70/week keto-on-a-budget week (2 people, 7 days).
