## Ingredients

- 1 white onion, diced
- 1 cup dry lentils, rinsed
- 2–3 russet potatoes, diced small
- 6–8 flour tortillas (large burrito size)

*Not counted: olive oil, cumin, chili powder, garlic powder, salt, pepper, water*

**Optional fillings:** shredded cheese, sour cream, salsa, hot sauce

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a medium pot over medium heat. Add diced onion and cook 3–4 minutes until softened.
2. Add rinsed lentils and diced potatoes. Season generously with cumin, chili powder, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
3. Add 2½ cups water. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer. Cook covered for 20–25 minutes until lentils are soft and potatoes are tender. Stir occasionally and add more water if needed.
4. Once filling is done, heat a separate dry skillet or griddle over medium-high heat.
5. Spoon filling down the center of a tortilla. Add optional toppings as desired. Fold and roll into a burrito.
6. Place burrito seam-side down in the hot skillet. Cook 2–3 minutes per side until golden and crispy.

## Notes

- Lentils and potatoes together make a surprisingly hearty, satisfying filling — no meat needed.
- Season aggressively — lentils absorb spices and need more than you expect.
- The hot skillet step is worth it — a crispy, toasted burrito is far better than a limp one.
- This is one of the cheapest meals possible: lentils, potatoes, and tortillas cost almost nothing.
