## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 cup beef broth
- Penne pasta (about 12 oz)
- 1 cup heavy whipping cream
- 1/3 block cream cheese, cubed
- 8 oz sour cream
- 2 tbsp minced garlic
- 2 tbsp flour
- A sprinkle of paprika
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Brown and season the ground beef until it gets good color, then set aside.
2. To the pan, add 2 tbsp minced garlic and 2 tbsp flour and mix.
3. Add 1 cup beef broth and 1 cup heavy cream, scraping up the browned bits. Add a sprinkle of paprika and 1 tbsp Worcestershire. Mix and simmer on medium-low.
4. Cube the cream cheese and stir it in until melty (don't let it boil).
5. Stir in the 8 oz sour cream.
6. Meanwhile, cook the penne. Drain.
7. Stir the cooked pasta and the browned beef into the sauce.

## Notes

- A from-scratch creamy beef pasta — about $3 per serving.
- Distinct from the stroganoffs: this uses a scratch cream-cheese-and-heavy-cream sauce (no cream of mushroom soup, no mushrooms) over penne.
- Not keto (pasta).
- Cream-heavy (1 cup heavy cream + cream cheese + sour cream), so not tagged extreme-budget despite the low per-serving cost — say the word to add it.
- Follower (AMF1128) recipe.
