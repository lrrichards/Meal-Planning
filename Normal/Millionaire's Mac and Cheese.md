## Ingredients

- 2 boxes shells and cheese
- 1 bag frozen broccoli
- Real bacon bits
- Crispy fried onions
- Garlic salt
- Pepper

## Instructions

1. Boil the shells in a pot until tender, then drain.
2. Meanwhile, steam the frozen broccoli.
3. Return the drained shells to the pot. Squeeze in the cheese sauce packets and mix well.
4. Season with a little garlic salt.
5. Dump in the steamed broccoli and a generous amount of bacon bits. Mix well.
6. Transfer to an oven-safe dish, top with the crispy fried onions, and broil about 2 minutes until the onions crisp up.
7. Finish with a little pepper.

## Notes

- "Millionaire's mac and cheese" — Dollar Tree comfort food that punches above its price.
- The broccoli makes it (a little) healthier.
- Not keto (pasta).
- Evan-safe (no tomatoes).
- If you have extra cheese, bake it instead of just broiling for a fuller bake.
