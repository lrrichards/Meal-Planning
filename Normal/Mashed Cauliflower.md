## Ingredients

- 1 head cauliflower, cut into florets
- Butter
- Garlic, minced
- Cream cheese
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Steam the cauliflower until very tender.
2. While the cauliflower steams, make a garlic brown butter: cook butter with minced garlic in a pan until the butter browns and smells nutty.
3. Transfer the steamed cauliflower to a food processor along with the garlic brown butter, cream cheese, salt, and pepper.
4. Blend until smooth and creamy, like mashed potatoes.

## Notes

- A healthy, low-carb side that tastes like mashed potatoes — great keto swap.
- A side dish, not a full meal (so not tagged extreme-budget).
- Pairs well with the keto chicken and pork mains.
