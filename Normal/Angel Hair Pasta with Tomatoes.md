## Ingredients

- 8 oz angel hair pasta
- 3 Roma tomatoes, chopped
- 2 green onions, sliced
- 1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese, grated
- 1/4 cup olive oil

*Not counted: olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic*

## Instructions

1. Cook angel hair pasta in a pot of boiling salted water according to package instructions. Drain.
2. While pasta cooks, chop tomatoes, green onions, and basil.
3. Turn off the heat. Add olive oil and Parmesan cheese to the drained pasta. Stir.
4. Add tomatoes, green onions, and basil. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
5. Stir to combine and serve immediately.

## Notes

- Best in summer when tomatoes are fresh and you don't want to heat up the kitchen.
- Great meatless meal — light and fresh tasting.
- Add minced garlic with the veggies for extra flavor.
- Recipe also appears on page 179 of the *Make It Simple* cookbook.
