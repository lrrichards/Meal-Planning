## Ingredients

- 1 batch fat head dough
- 1/4 cup butter
- 3 cloves garlic

## Instructions

1. Roll the fat head dough into little logs or snake shapes.
2. Brush with melted butter and sprinkle with garlic powder or garlic salt.
3. Bake in the oven until golden (around 375°F until set and browned).

## Notes

- Keto / low-carb breadsticks from fat head dough — great alongside the keto lasagna or a salad.
- Uses fat head dough (mozzarella + almond flour), so not tagged extreme-budget.
