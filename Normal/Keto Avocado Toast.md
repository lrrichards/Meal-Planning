## Ingredients

- Keto bread (gluten-free options like Carbonaut or Base Culture if you can find them)
- 1/2 avocado
- Everything bagel seasoning (or salt)
- Smoked salmon
- Capers
- Egg (optional, for extra protein)

## Instructions

1. Toast the keto bread.
2. Scrape out the center of half an avocado and smash (or slice) it onto the toast. Slicing is easier if the avocado is firm.
3. Top with everything bagel seasoning or a little salt.
4. Add smoked salmon and capers on top (and an egg if you want more protein).

## Notes

- Keto / low-carb breakfast in a couple of minutes — healthy fats and protein.
- Evan-safe (no tomatoes).
- Uses avocado and smoked salmon (pricier ingredients), so not tagged extreme-budget.
