## Ingredients

- 8 oz rigatoni
- 2 cups frozen mixed vegetables
- 1/4 cup basil pesto

*Not counted: oil*

## Instructions

1. Bring a pot of salted water to boil. Cook rigatoni according to package directions.
2. In a separate pan, heat a little oil over medium heat. Add frozen mixed veggies. Cook until golden on the outside and heated through.
3. Drain pasta. Add pesto and toss to coat.
4. Add cooked veggies to the pasta. Stir together.
5. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- One of the simplest dinners possible — 3 ingredients, 15 minutes.
- Add a drained can of chicken to bump up the protein while still keeping it budget-friendly.
- The pesto does all the flavor work — a little goes a long way.
- Part of a $25 Walmart budget week.
