## Ingredients

- 2 lb chicken thighs
- 1 cup honey
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1 onion (half for roasting, half for the sauce)
- 12 oz broccoli
- 2 potatoes, cut up
- 3 bell peppers
- 3 cloves garlic
- Olive oil, garlic salt, onion powder, garlic powder, salt, pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 400°F. Cut up the broccoli, potatoes, peppers, and half the onion. Toss with olive oil, garlic salt, and your spices. Roast while you make the chicken.
2. Get a skillet super hot. Season the chicken thighs with salt and pepper and sear both sides (not fully cooked through yet).
3. Add the other half of the onion and minced garlic to the skillet. Remove the chicken once some crispy bits form.
4. Add 1 cup honey and 1/2 cup soy sauce. Turn the heat down and scrape up all the browned bits. Add onion powder and garlic powder and mix.
5. Return the chicken to the pan, coat in the sauce, and cook until no pink remains.
6. Serve the chicken thighs over the roasted vegetables and spoon extra sauce on top.

## Notes

- Distinct from the existing One Pan Honey Garlic Chicken (that's saucy chicken breast with no vegetables) — this is glazed thighs over a full roasted-veg medley.
- Not keto (honey + potatoes).
- "Poor man's comfort food," birthday-eve edition.
