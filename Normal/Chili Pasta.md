## Ingredients

- Small amount of onion, diced
- 1 mini can corn, drained
- 1/2 lb rigatoni pasta
- 1 can tomato paste
- 1 can mixed chili beans (seasoned)
- 3/4 cup water
- Shredded Colby Jack cheese for topping

**Corn Muffin Cookies (side):**
- 1 box Jiffy corn muffin mix
- Water and oil per package (no egg needed)

*Not counted: olive oil, salt, pepper, cumin, oregano, paprika*

## Instructions

1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add onion and drained corn. Cook 2–3 minutes to char the corn slightly — this adds a lot of flavor.
2. Meanwhile, bring a pot of water to boil and cook rigatoni according to package directions.
3. **Make corn muffin side:** Mix corn muffin mix with water and oil (skip the egg). Spoon onto a baking sheet in flat rounds. Bake at 400°F for about 10 minutes until golden.
4. Add tomato paste to the charred corn and onion. Stir in 3/4 cup water and mix well.
5. Add mixed chili beans and season with salt, pepper, cumin, oregano, and paprika. Stir and simmer 5–20 minutes (longer = more flavor).
6. Once pasta is cooked, drain and stir directly into the chili sauce. Toss to coat.
7. Serve topped with shredded cheese and corn muffin cookies on the side.

## Notes

- Charring the corn before adding the tomato paste is the key step — don't rush it.
- Mixed chili beans already have seasoning in them — taste before adding extra salt.
- Corn muffin cookies baked flat like this are a great budget bread substitute.
- No egg needed in the corn muffin mix — works fine without it.
- Part of a $25 Walmart budget week.
