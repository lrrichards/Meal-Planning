## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground sausage (ground beef works too)
- 1 can chili (with or without beans — your call)
- 1 can Rotel (diced tomatoes and green chilies), drained
- 1 box deluxe macaroni and cheese (the microwave kind with the liquid cheese pouch)
- Shredded triple cheddar cheese

## Instructions

1. Brown and crumble the ground sausage in a skillet over medium heat.
2. Microwave the deluxe mac and cheese noodles per the box.
3. Add the chili to the sausage and give it a preliminary stir.
4. Drain the Rotel, then stir it in.
5. Drain the cooked pasta and add it to the skillet along with the liquid cheese. Stir to combine.
6. Top with shredded cheddar. Either let it melt on top, or broil it for a bubbly, browned cheese layer (broiling is the move).

## Notes

- **Contains Rotel/tomatoes — not Evan-safe.** Leave it out or sub for Evan.
- Dollar Tree "poor man's comfort food" — built entirely from cheap canned/boxed ingredients.
- Not keto (pasta-based).
