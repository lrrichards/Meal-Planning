## Ingredients

- 1 package sweet rolls (Hawaiian-style)
- 12 eggs
- Colby Jack cheese, sliced
- 1 package sausage links
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 400°F. Bake the sausage links about 8 minutes until cooked.
2. Crack the eggs into a bowl and scramble them in a hot skillet — the rendered sausage grease is perfect for cooking them. Season with salt and pepper and fluff.
3. Slice the cooked sausage links.
4. Build each sandwich on a sweet roll: scrambled egg, sliced sausage, and a slice of Colby Jack. Closed-face or open-face — closed is best.

## Notes

- About 72 g of protein across the batch of eggs alone.
- Not keto (sweet rolls).
- Evan-safe (no tomatoes).
- Aldi "poor man's comfort food," four items for under $10.
