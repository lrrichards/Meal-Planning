## Ingredients

- 5 russet potatoes, washed
- 2 cans chicken, drained
- 1 bag frozen broccoli cuts, steamed
- 3 green onions, sliced
- Shredded Colby Jack cheese (generous amount)
- 2 tbsp butter

*Not counted: olive oil, garlic salt, salt, pepper, paprika, onion powder*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Poke holes all over each potato with a fork.
2. Drizzle potatoes with a little olive oil and sprinkle generously with garlic salt.
3. Bake directly on the oven rack (or a sheet pan) for 60 minutes.
4. When potatoes are almost done, steam the frozen broccoli in the microwave per bag instructions.
5. Melt 2 tbsp butter in a pan over medium heat. Add drained canned chicken, steamed broccoli, and green onions.
6. Season with salt, pepper, paprika, and onion powder. Cook and stir for about 5 minutes.
7. Add shredded cheese to the pan. Stir until melted. Add a splash of heavy cream if you want it creamier (optional).
8. Split open baked potatoes and top generously with the chicken broccoli cheese mixture.

## Notes

- Garlic salt on the potato skin is the move — gives the skin incredible flavor.
- For large batches (more than 5 potatoes), use a slow cooker instead of the oven.
- Heavy cream makes the topping creamier but is completely optional.
- High protein from the canned chicken — filling and nutritious.
- Part of a $25 Walmart budget week.
