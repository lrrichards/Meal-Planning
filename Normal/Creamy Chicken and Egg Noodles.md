## Ingredients

- 3–4 carrots, sliced
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1.5 lbs chicken breast, cubed
- 3 cups chicken broth
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 12 oz egg noodles

*Not counted: olive oil, butter, salt, pepper, garlic powder, thyme, flour (optional, to thicken)*

## Instructions

1. Heat butter and a drizzle of olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion and carrots. Cook 5–7 minutes until softened.
2. Add cubed chicken. Season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and thyme. Cook until no longer pink, about 7–8 minutes.
3. Pour in chicken broth. Bring to a simmer.
4. Add egg noodles directly to the pot. Cook according to package directions until tender.
5. Stir in heavy cream. Simmer 3–4 minutes until cream is incorporated and sauce thickens slightly.
6. Taste and adjust seasoning. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Cooking the noodles in broth instead of plain water adds flavor — no need for a separate pot.
- Heavy cream added at the end keeps the sauce from breaking — don't add it while heat is too high.
- To thicken: stir a tablespoon of flour into the softened vegetables before adding broth.
- A creamy, one-pot-style take on chicken noodle that's more of a meal than a soup.
