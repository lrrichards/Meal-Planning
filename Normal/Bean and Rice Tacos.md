## Ingredients

- 2 cups cooked Mayocoba beans (or pinto beans)
- 2 cups cooked white rice
- 10 corn tortillas
- Diced tomatoes (leftover from previous meal)

*Not counted: taco seasoning, oil*

## Instructions

1. Mix beans and rice together in a microwave-safe bowl. Add 1 tbsp taco seasoning and stir.
2. Microwave 2–3 minutes until heated through, stirring halfway.
3. Heat a pan over medium heat with a small amount of oil. Warm tortillas one or two at a time, about 30 seconds per side, until lightly golden.
4. Fill warmed tortillas with the bean and rice mixture. Top with diced tomatoes.
5. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Heating the tortillas in oil is what makes these taste restaurant-quality — don't skip this step.
- Mayocoba beans are high in protein and very filling despite being meatless.
- This is a use-up-the-leftovers meal — uses beans and rice from earlier in the week.
- Toppings can include any leftover veggies, cheese, or green onions on hand.
- Part of a $25 Walmart budget week.
