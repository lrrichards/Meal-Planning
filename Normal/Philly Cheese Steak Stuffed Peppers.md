## Ingredients

- 1 lb beef shaved steak
- 4 bell peppers
- 1 onion, sliced
- 8 oz mushroom
- 4 oz provolone cheese
- About 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- Butter, vegetable oil, garlic, salt, pepper, seasoning

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 350°F. Halve the bell peppers and remove the seeds. Line them up on a baking sheet, paint with a little butter, and add your favorite seasoning. Bake 25 minutes to par-bake.
2. Meanwhile, get a skillet hot with a little oil and butter. Add the onions over high heat for about 10 minutes until crispy and caramelized.
3. Add the sliced mushrooms and about 1 tbsp Worcestershire. Stir well. Add garlic, salt, and pepper.
4. Add a little more butter and oil, then add the shaved steak and cook through.
5. Stuff the par-baked peppers with the steak, onion, and mushroom filling.
6. Top each with provolone.
7. Bake 15–20 minutes until done and the cheese is melted.

## Notes

- Keto / low-carb — the creator calls it good for type 1 diabetics and keto eaters.
- "Poor man's comfort food" series, but shaved steak + provolone + mushrooms put it above extreme-budget range, so not tagged extreme-budget.
