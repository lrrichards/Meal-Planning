## Ingredients

- 1 bag frozen pot stickers
- 1 box chicken alfredo dinner (the skillet/microwave kind)
- Shredded mozzarella cheese
- Real bacon bits
- 1 can peas (optional)

## Instructions

1. Get two skillets going at once to save time (or do them one after the other in a single pan if you prefer).
2. In one skillet, add a little oil and the pot stickers. Keep stirring so they don't burn; cook until done and crispy.
3. In the other skillet, prepare the chicken alfredo dinner per the box — the sauce comes together as it cooks.
4. Drain and stir in the peas if using.
5. Add the cooked, crispy pot stickers into the alfredo.
6. Toss in real bacon bits and stir to combine.
7. Add shredded mozzarella to taste, turn off the heat, cover, and let the cheese melt before serving.

## Notes

- "Italian-Asian fusion" — like a carbonara, but with pot stickers standing in for some of the pasta.
- Dollar Tree "poor man's comfort food" — cheap boxed/frozen/canned ingredients.
- Not keto (pasta and pot stickers).
