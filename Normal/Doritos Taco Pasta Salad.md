## Ingredients

- 12 oz rotini pasta
- 1 lb ground beef
- 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
- 3–4 green onions, sliced
- 2 cups iceberg lettuce, shredded
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained
- 1 cup corn (frozen/thawed or canned)
- 2 cups Doritos chips, roughly crushed (Nacho Cheese flavor)
- ½ cup French dressing

*Not counted: taco seasoning, salt, pepper*

## Instructions

1. Cook rotini in salted boiling water until tender. Drain, rinse with cold water, and cool completely.
2. Brown ground beef in a skillet. Season with taco seasoning, salt, and pepper. Drain fat. Let cool.
3. In a large bowl, combine cooled pasta, cooled ground beef, diced tomatoes, green onions, black beans, and corn.
4. Pour French dressing over the top and toss to coat.
5. Add shredded iceberg lettuce and toss again.
6. Refrigerate until ready to serve.
7. Add crushed Doritos right before serving — adding earlier makes them soggy.

## Notes

- Doritos must go in right before serving — even 5 minutes of sitting makes them lose their crunch.
- French dressing is the classic choice for this salad — Catalina dressing works as a substitute.
- Great for potlucks and parties — everything except the Doritos can be prepped hours ahead.
- Rinse and cool the pasta completely before mixing — hot pasta wilts the lettuce.
