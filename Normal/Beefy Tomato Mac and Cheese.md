## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 box mac and cheese (plus butter/milk per box directions)
- 1 can tomato soup

*Not counted: butter, milk, salt, pepper*

## Instructions

1. Brown the ground beef in a skillet over medium heat, breaking it up. Drain.
2. Prepare the boxed mac and cheese according to package directions.
3. Stir the cooked ground beef and the can of tomato soup into the mac and cheese.
4. Mix until combined and heated through. Serve.
