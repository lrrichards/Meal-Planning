## Ingredients

### Bowl
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 yellow onion, diced (optional)
- Frozen French fries (any style)
- Shredded iceberg lettuce
- Shredded cheese

### Special Sauce (optional)
- ¼ cup mayonnaise
- 4 tsp ketchup
- 1 tsp yellow mustard
- 2 tsp dill relish

*Not counted: steak seasoning, oil*

## Instructions

1. Cook French fries in the oven or air fryer according to package directions.
2. Brown ground beef and diced onion in a pan over medium heat, breaking up the meat. Add about 2 tsp steak seasoning once cooked through.
3. Mix special sauce ingredients together in a small bowl.
4. To assemble: add shredded lettuce to a bowl, fries on the side, a scoop of seasoned beef, shredded cheese, and a drizzle of special sauce.
5. Add any other burger toppings you like and serve.

## Notes

- The special sauce is optional but really elevates the bowl — quick to make.
- Add jalapeños, bacon bits, sliced tomatoes, or pickles to make it your own.
- The onion is optional but caramelizes nicely with the beef and adds a lot of flavor.
- A fun twist on regular burger night without needing a grill.
