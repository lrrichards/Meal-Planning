## Ingredients

- 4 packets ramen noodles (any flavor — spicy and beef are a good mix)
- ½ yellow onion, diced
- Remaining frozen mixed vegetables (about half a bag)
- 2 eggs
- ¼ cup low sodium soy sauce

*Not counted: olive oil, ramen seasoning packets (use 2 of the 4), salt, pepper*

**Optional topping:** Chili flakes

## Instructions

1. Cook all 4 ramen packets in a pot of boiling water until tender. Drain and return to the pot.
2. Heat 1–2 tbsp olive oil in a separate pan over medium-high heat. Add diced onion and frozen mixed vegetables.
3. Cook veggies and onion for 5–10 minutes until they get some color and the onion softens.
4. Crack 2 eggs into the pan. Season with salt and pepper. Scramble into the veggie mixture.
5. Add 2 of the ramen seasoning packets (mix flavors if using different varieties). Stir.
6. Once egg is set, add drained ramen noodles to the pan. Pour in soy sauce.
7. Toss everything together and serve. Top with chili flakes if desired.

## Notes

- Only 2 of the 4 seasoning packets are used — saves the others for another recipe.
- Browning the vegetables before adding the noodles is what gives this real flavor — don't rush that step.
- Add cooked chicken, shrimp, or extra eggs for more protein.
- This version differs from Ramen Noodle Stir Fry (also in Normal/) — this one adds eggs as protein and uses mixed vegetables + onion instead of a stir-fry veggie blend.
- Kid-friendly — works well for families.
