## Ingredients

- 4 eggs
- 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
- 2 tbsp almond flour (optional — adds texture so it's not pure egg-and-cheese)

## Instructions

1. Whisk the eggs in a bowl. Stir in the shredded mozzarella and almond flour until combined.
2. Heat a waffle maker. Spoon in enough batter to fill each well (about 1/4 cup per chaffle).
3. Cook 3–4 minutes until golden and set. Repeat with remaining batter — a 4-burner waffle maker speeds this up a lot.
4. Cool slightly. Use as sandwich "bread," pizza crust, or eat plain.

## Notes

- This is the base for the whole keto week — chaffle sandwiches and pulled pork pizza all use it. Make a big batch and store in the fridge.
- Reheat in a toaster or under the oven broiler to crisp them back up.
- No waffle maker? Cook the batter pancake-style in a skillet instead.
- Coconut flour works in place of almond flour (use about half as much — it absorbs more).
