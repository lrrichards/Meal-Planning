## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 3 ribs celery, sliced
- 3 large carrots, sliced
- 2 large russet potatoes, cubed
- 1 can (15 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 1½ cups frozen green beans
- 2 cups V8 juice
- 5 cups beef broth

*Not counted: garlic (1 tbsp minced), salt, pepper*

## Instructions

1. Add ground beef to a large pot over medium-high heat. Break it up and cook for a couple minutes.
2. Add diced onion and cook until ground beef is cooked through. Add minced garlic and cook 30 seconds.
3. Add diced tomatoes, frozen peas, frozen green beans, celery, potatoes, and carrots.
4. Pour in V8 juice and beef broth. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Stir, cover, and simmer for 40–60 minutes until vegetables are very tender.
6. Serve as-is, with bread on the side.

## Notes

- V8 juice is the unexpected secret — it adds a rich, savory tomato-vegetable depth that plain broth misses.
- This is a complete meal on its own — protein, starch, and vegetables all in one pot.
- Great for when someone in the house is under the weather — very nourishing and filling.
- Use whatever frozen or fresh vegetables you have on hand — this recipe is very forgiving.
- Tastes even better the next day after the flavors have had time to meld.
