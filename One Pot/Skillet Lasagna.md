## Ingredients

- 1 lb Italian sausage, casing removed
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 24 oz marinara sauce
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
- 8–10 lasagna noodles, broken into rough 1–2 inch pieces
- 1 cup cottage cheese
- 1½ cups mozzarella cheese, shredded
- ¼ cup parmesan cheese, shredded

*Not counted: salt, pepper, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, red pepper flakes*

## Instructions

1. Cook sausage in a large deep skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat, breaking it up. Drain excess fat.
2. Add marinara sauce, diced tomatoes, and chicken broth. Season with Italian seasoning, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes. Stir.
3. Bring to a simmer. Add broken lasagna noodle pieces, pressing them down into the liquid.
4. Cover and simmer on medium-low for 18–22 minutes, stirring occasionally, until noodles are tender and sauce has thickened.
5. Drop spoonfuls of cottage cheese over the top. Sprinkle mozzarella and parmesan over the cottage cheese.
6. Cover and let cheese melt 3–5 minutes. Serve directly from the skillet.

## Notes

- Break lasagna noodles into rough pieces — no need to be precise, they cook the same.
- The noodles cook directly in the sauce and absorb flavor as they hydrate.
- Cottage cheese mimics the ricotta layer from traditional lasagna — kids usually can't tell the difference.
- Add a handful of fresh spinach before adding cheese for a vegetable boost.
