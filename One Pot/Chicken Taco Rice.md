## Ingredients

- 1 lb chicken breast, cubed small
- 1 can (10 oz) Rotel (diced tomatoes with green chilies)
- 1 can (8 oz) tomato sauce
- 1.5 cups chicken broth
- 1 cup uncooked white rice
- Shredded cheese to taste

*Not counted: oil, salt, pepper, chili powder, cumin, garlic*

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in a large deep pan or pot over medium heat. Add cubed chicken, season with salt and pepper, and cook 7–8 minutes until cooked through.
2. Add 1 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, and 1 tbsp minced garlic. Stir and cook 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add Rotel, tomato sauce, chicken broth, and uncooked rice. Stir well.
4. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook on medium-low for about 20 minutes, stirring occasionally to prevent the rice from sticking to the bottom.
5. Once rice is tender, sprinkle cheese over the top. Cover for 1–2 minutes to let it melt.
6. Serve topped with guacamole, sour cream, lime, or any taco toppings.

## Notes

- Stir every few minutes while the rice simmers — rice sticks easily.
- Great for Taco Tuesday as a twist on the classic.
- Toppings make this dish — don't skip them.
