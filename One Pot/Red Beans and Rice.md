## Ingredients

- 1 box Zatarain's red beans and rice (or similar)
- 14 oz smoked sausage, sliced into rounds
- Water per box instructions

*Not counted: olive oil*

## Instructions

1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add sliced sausage and brown for a few minutes on each side to develop flavor.
2. Add the box of red beans and rice and required amount of water per package directions.
3. Stir to combine. Bring to a simmer and cook covered until rice is tender (per box timing, typically 20–25 minutes).
4. Serve topped with cheese, sour cream, or guacamole if desired.

## Notes

- Browning the sausage first is the key step — gives it much more flavor than just adding it raw.
- Ground beef or ground turkey work as substitutes for smoked sausage.
- Zatarain's brand recommended for best flavor, but any red beans and rice mix works.
- Only 2 ingredients — one of the simplest dinners possible.
