## Ingredients

- 1–2 packets ramen noodles with seasoning packets
- 2 eggs

## Instructions

1. Bring a pot of water to boil. Add ramen noodles and cook until tender.
2. Add the seasoning packet and stir.
3. Crack eggs directly into the pot. Cook until whites are set — leave yolks soft for a runnier egg, cook longer for firmer yolks.
4. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Adds significant protein to an otherwise low-protein meal for almost no cost.
- Soft yolk eggs blend into the broth and add richness.
- Scale up freely — one pot handles multiple servings.
- About as extreme-budget as a meal gets.
