## Ingredients

- 1 package smoked sausage (chicken, turkey, or beef), sliced
- 1 bell pepper, sliced
- 1 cup uncooked orzo
- 12 oz frozen broccoli
- 2 cups chicken broth
- Parmesan cheese to taste

*Not counted: olive oil, salt, pepper, crushed red pepper flakes, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, paprika*

## Instructions

1. Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Add sliced sausage and bell pepper. Cook for about 5 minutes until sausage is browned.
3. Add orzo, frozen broccoli, chicken broth, ½ tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper, a dash of crushed red pepper flakes, 1 tsp Italian seasoning, ½ tsp garlic powder, and ½ tsp paprika. Stir to combine.
4. Bring to a simmer and cook for about 10 minutes until orzo is cooked through.
5. Stir in Parmesan cheese, let melt, and serve.

## Notes

- Use a large pot — the orzo and broccoli expand more than expected.
- Any smoked sausage works: chicken, turkey, beef, or pork.
- Swap broccoli for any frozen veggie on hand.
- Bell pepper is optional but adds color and sweetness.
- Done in 15 minutes start to finish.
