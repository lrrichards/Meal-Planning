## Ingredients

- 1 lb Italian sausage
- 1 onion, diced
- Chicken broth
- 1 can crushed tomatoes
- 3/4 cup heavy cream
- Fresh spinach
- Parmesan cheese
- Cheese tortellini

## Instructions

1. Cook the diced onion, then add the sausage and brown it. Season.
2. Add the chicken broth and crushed tomatoes.
3. Add the spinach and cook until wilted.
4. Stir in a little parmesan, then 3/4 cup heavy cream. Mix well and simmer about 5 minutes.
5. Add the cheese tortellini and cook until they float/are tender.
6. Serve with more parmesan.

## Notes

- A cozy creamy tomato sausage tortellini soup for a cold night.
- **Contains crushed tomatoes — not Evan-safe.**
- Distinct from Sausage Tortellini Skillet (a saucy skillet dish, no broth or cream) and the meatless tortellini soup (this one has sausage and a creamy tomato broth).
- Not keto (tortellini).
- "Poor man's comfort food," rainy day soup edition.
