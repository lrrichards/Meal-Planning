## Ingredients

- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 2 large carrots, sliced
- 2 ribs celery, sliced
- ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
- 7 cups chicken broth
- 2 chicken breasts
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 2 cups egg noodles

*Not counted: butter (3 tbsp), salt (1½ tsp), pepper (½ tsp), Italian seasoning (1½ tsp), oregano (½ tsp)*

## Instructions

1. Melt 3 tbsp butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add diced onion, sliced carrots, and celery. Cook 5–7 minutes until softened.
2. Season with salt, pepper, Italian seasoning, and oregano. Stir in ⅓ cup flour. Cook until flour no longer looks white.
3. Slowly stir in 7 cups chicken broth a little at a time, whisking to keep it smooth and lump-free.
4. Add chicken breasts. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook about 20 minutes until chicken is cooked through.
5. Remove chicken. Shred or chop into pieces. Return to pot.
6. Stir in heavy cream and egg noodles. Let cook until noodles are tender, about 8–10 minutes.
7. Taste and adjust seasoning. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Add broth slowly after the flour — a gradual pour keeps the soup smooth instead of lumpy.
- The flour gives this soup its body and creamy thickness even before the heavy cream goes in.
- Skip the heavy cream for a lighter version — the flour still gives it a nice body.
- Use smaller egg noodles for a kid-friendly texture; larger noodles work too.
