## Ingredients

- 1/4 yellow onion, diced
- 1 green bell pepper, diced
- 2 cups uncooked white rice
- 1/2 cup tomato sauce
- 1/2 cup salsa
- 2.5 cups vegetable broth
- 2 cans (15 oz each) pinto beans, drained and rinsed
- 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese (optional)

*Not counted: olive oil, garlic, salt, pepper, paprika, chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, onion powder*

## Instructions

1. Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add garlic, onion, and bell pepper. Cook about 5 minutes until softened.
2. Add uncooked rice and seasonings: 1/2 tsp salt, 2 tsp paprika, 2 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1/2 tsp pepper, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, 1/2 tsp onion powder. Stir and cook 1 minute.
3. Add tomato sauce, salsa, vegetable broth, and pinto beans. Stir well.
4. Cover and simmer on medium-low for about 20 minutes, stirring occasionally to prevent the rice from sticking to the bottom.
5. Once rice is fluffy, stir in cheddar cheese if using. Let melt and serve.
6. Top with lime, fresh parsley, sour cream, or taco toppings of your choice.

## Notes

- Green bell peppers are the cheapest variety — perfect for budget cooking.
- Stir frequently toward the end — the rice can catch on the bottom.
- Makes a huge batch — halve it for smaller households or double for a crowd.
- Great meatless meal that's high in fiber and protein from the beans.
