## Ingredients

- 1 yellow onion, diced
- ½ cup carrots, chopped
- 2 cups cooked shredded chicken
- 4½ cups chicken broth
- ½ cup heavy cream
- 6 oz cream cheese, cubed
- 1 cup small pasta (any small shape)
- 1 cup fresh spinach
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- ⅓ cup parmesan cheese, shredded

*Not counted: olive oil, garlic (1 tbsp minced), salt, pepper*

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a pot over medium heat. Add minced garlic, diced onion, and chopped carrots. Cook 5–7 minutes until softened.
2. Add shredded chicken, chicken broth, heavy cream, and cubed cream cheese. Stir.
3. Cover and simmer for 5 minutes to soften the cream cheese.
4. Whisk the cream cheese into the soup until fully smooth and incorporated — no lumps.
5. Add small pasta and fresh spinach. Stir.
6. Cover and simmer for about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until pasta is tender.
7. Stir in shredded cheddar and parmesan until melted. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Whisk the cream cheese in rather than stirring — it incorporates more smoothly.
- Add the cheese off heat or very low heat to prevent it from breaking or becoming grainy.
- Rotisserie chicken makes this a very fast prep — just shred and add.
- This is a legitimately filling soup — the cream cheese, heavy cream, and pasta make it a meal.
