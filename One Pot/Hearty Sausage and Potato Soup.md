## Ingredients

- 1 lb Italian sausage
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 2 ribs celery, sliced
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- 6 cups chicken broth (or beef or veggie broth)
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1.5 lbs small red potatoes, cut into small bite-sized pieces
- 2 cups Colby Jack cheese, shredded

*Not counted: salt, pepper, Italian seasoning, garlic (1 tbsp minced)*

## Instructions

1. Add Italian sausage to a large pot over medium-high heat. Break it up and cook for a few minutes — not all the way through yet.
2. Add diced onion, sliced carrots, and celery. Stir. Cover and cook until sausage is cooked through and veggies are beginning to soften, about 5–7 minutes.
3. Stir in 3 tbsp flour until it no longer looks white.
4. Add soy sauce, then pour in 6 cups broth. Scrape up any browned bits from the bottom.
5. Stir in heavy cream, potatoes, minced garlic, salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning.
6. Cover and simmer for 15–20 minutes until potatoes and vegetables are fork tender.
7. Turn off heat. Stir in Colby Jack cheese until melted. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Cut potatoes into small, even pieces so they cook through in the simmer time.
- Soy sauce is the subtle umami secret — adds depth without tasting like soy sauce.
- Cover the pot while the sausage and veggies cook to speed things up and build steam.
- Substitute ground beef, ground turkey, or ground chicken for the sausage.
- Stir in the cheese off heat to prevent it from becoming grainy or breaking.
