## Ingredients

- 2 cups cooked shredded chicken
- 1 head cauliflower, riced
- 3 stalks celery
- 1/2 yellow onion, diced
- 4 cups broth
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. In a pot, add the chicken broth, chopped celery, and diced onion. Simmer until the vegetables soften.
2. Add the riced cauliflower and shredded chicken. Simmer until the cauliflower is tender, about 10 minutes.
3. Season with salt and pepper.

## Notes

- Cauliflower rice stands in for regular rice to keep it keto.
- This recipe is light on fat — stir in shredded cheese, cream cheese, butter, or a drizzle of oil to add richness.
- Make ahead and reheat to serve.
