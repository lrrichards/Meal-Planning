## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground chicken (or ground turkey or ground beef)
- Small amount of onion, diced
- 4 russet potatoes, peeled and diced small
- 1 cup frozen mixed veggies
- 1/4 cup basil pesto
- 1/2 cup water (more if needed)

*Not counted: olive oil, salt, pepper*

## Instructions

1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add diced onion and cook a few minutes until softened.
2. Add ground chicken. Break it up and cook through completely.
3. Add potatoes, frozen mixed veggies, pesto, and water. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Stir to combine. Cover and simmer on medium-low for 20–25 minutes, stirring often to prevent sticking.
5. If the pan looks dry, add another 1/2 cup water and continue simmering until potatoes are tender.
6. Serve once potatoes are fully cooked through.

## Notes

- Ground turkey or ground beef work as direct substitutes for ground chicken.
- Stir frequently — the starch from the potatoes can stick to the bottom.
- Pesto does all the flavor heavy lifting here — don't skip it.
- Part of a $25 Walmart budget week — very affordable per serving.
