## Ingredients

- 1/2 onion
- ½ lb ground turkey (or ground beef)
- 28 oz canned diced tomatoes (Italian style with garlic and oregano preferred)
- 1 can (6 oz) tomato paste
- 2 cans (15 oz each) mixed chili beans
- ⅓ cup frozen corn
- 4 cups water

*Not counted: olive oil, brown sugar (3 tsp), cumin (½ tsp), garlic powder, paprika, cayenne pepper, chili powder (2 tsp), oregano, salt, pepper*

**To serve:** Baked potatoes, shredded cheese, sour cream, diced tomatoes

## Instructions

1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add diced onion and cook 1 minute.
2. Add ground turkey and cook through, breaking it up.
3. Add diced tomatoes, tomato paste, chili beans, frozen corn, and 4 cups water. Stir well.
4. Season with brown sugar, cumin, garlic powder, paprika, a dash of cayenne, chili powder, oregano, salt, and pepper.
5. Bring to a simmer. Cover and cook 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally.
6. Serve in bowls, or use as topping for baked potatoes (see Chili Baked Potatoes).

## Notes

- Italian-style diced tomatoes with garlic and oregano add built-in flavor — worth seeking out.
- Mixed chili beans come pre-seasoned and add a lot of depth without extra spices.
- Brown sugar is the secret — adds a subtle sweetness that balances the heat.
- Reheats extremely well — makes 2–3 dinners from one pot.
- Makes 8+ servings — great for loading into a container and stretching across multiple meals.
