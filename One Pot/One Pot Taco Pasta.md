## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1 cup beef broth
- 1 can (10 oz) diced tomatoes with green chilis (Rotel)
- 1 can (6 oz) tomato paste
- ½ cup milk
- 2 cups elbow pasta, uncooked
- 1½ cups shredded cheese (cheddar or Mexican blend)

*Not counted: taco seasoning, Worcestershire sauce, salt, pepper, garlic powder*

## Instructions

1. Brown ground beef in a large pot over medium-high heat. Drain excess fat. Season with taco seasoning.
2. Add a splash of Worcestershire sauce and stir.
3. Add chicken broth, beef broth, diced tomatoes with green chilis, and tomato paste. Stir to combine.
4. Bring to a boil. Add uncooked elbow pasta. Stir well.
5. Reduce heat to medium-low. Cover and cook 10–12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until pasta is tender and has absorbed most of the liquid.
6. Remove from heat. Stir in milk and shredded cheese until melted and creamy.
7. Taste and adjust seasoning. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- The double broth (chicken + beef) adds depth that single broth alone doesn't match.
- Worcestershire is a subtle but important addition — adds savory, slightly sweet background notes.
- Stir frequently once pasta is added — it can stick to the bottom as it absorbs liquid.
- See also: Taco Mac (One Pot/ folder) — similar concept but different ratios and no Worcestershire or beef broth.
