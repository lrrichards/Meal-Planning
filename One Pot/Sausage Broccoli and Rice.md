## Ingredients

- 1 package beef smoked sausage, sliced into half-moons
- 1 bag frozen broccoli cuts
- 1 box cheddar broccoli rice mix
- 1 3/4 cups water

## Instructions

1. Add sliced sausage to a pan over medium heat. Sear about 3 minutes per side until browned.
2. Add frozen broccoli cuts and the cheddar broccoli rice mix.
3. Pour in 1 3/4 cups water. Stir to combine.
4. Cover and simmer on medium-low for about 12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until rice is tender and fluffy.
5. Serve directly from the pan.

## Notes

- Only 3 ingredients — one of the easiest meals in the vault.
- Searing the sausage first before adding everything else adds significant flavor.
- Feeds a crowd for very little money.
- The boxed cheddar broccoli rice does the seasoning work — no extra spices needed.
