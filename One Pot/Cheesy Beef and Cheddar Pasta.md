## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 2 cups pasta shells
- 2 cups beef broth
- 1 cup milk
- A little butter
- 6 oz cream cheese
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- 1 packet ranch seasoning

## Instructions

1. Brown the ground beef in a large pot or deep skillet. Soak up the excess grease with a paper towel (never pour it down the drain).
2. Add a little butter, 1 cup milk, and 2 cups beef broth. Stir until the butter melts.
3. Stir in the ranch seasoning.
4. Add 2 cups pasta shells, stir, and bring to a boil. Reduce to a simmer, cover, and cook 10–12 minutes, stirring occasionally.
5. When the pasta is getting soft, stir in the cubed cream cheese until creamy.
6. Add the shredded cheddar and stir until melted. Done.

## Notes

- One-pot cheeseburger-helper style dish — the kids love it.
- Not keto (pasta).
- Cubing the cream cheese just helps it stir in faster.
- Pairs well with a side salad.
