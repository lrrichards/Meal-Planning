## Ingredients

**Marinade:**
- 1/4 cup pineapple juice (from the can)
- 1/4 cup low sodium soy sauce
- 1/2 cup coconut milk
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp salt

**Main:**
- 1.5–2 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 1 can (20 oz) pineapple chunks, drained (save all juice)
- 1 cup coconut milk
- 1.5 cups jasmine rice
- Water to bring liquid total to 3 cups

*Not counted: oil, garlic, garlic powder, onion powder, ginger*

## Instructions

1. Whisk together pineapple juice (1/4 cup), soy sauce, coconut milk (1/2 cup), honey, 1 tbsp minced garlic, and salt in a bowl.
2. Add chicken thighs, cover, and marinate in the fridge for 20 minutes up to 24 hours.
3. Heat oil in a large pan over medium-high. Remove chicken from marinade and sear 4 minutes per side until golden brown. Remove to a plate.
4. To the same pan, add remaining coconut milk and pineapple juice. Add enough water to reach 3 cups total liquid. Stir to scrape up the browned bits.
5. Add garlic powder, onion powder, and 1/2 tbsp minced ginger. Stir.
6. Add jasmine rice and drained pineapple chunks. Stir. Nestle seared chicken back on top.
7. Cover and simmer on low for 20–25 minutes until rice is tender.
8. Serve directly from the pan.

## Notes

- Chicken thighs strongly preferred over breasts here — they stay juicy through the full cook time.
- The marinade + the browned bits scraped off the pan make the rice incredibly flavorful.
- Pineapple flavor is mild, not overpowering — the coconut milk mellows everything out.
- Marinating overnight = best results.
