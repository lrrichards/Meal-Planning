## Ingredients

- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1 lb ground beef
- 3½ cups beef broth
- 10 oz Rotel (diced tomatoes with green chilies)
- 12 oz frozen broccoli florets
- 2 cups uncooked white rice, rinsed
- 1 cup shredded Mexican-style cheese

*Not counted: minced garlic, paprika, garlic powder, salt, pepper*

## Instructions

1. Add diced onion and ground beef to a large pot over medium heat.
2. Season with 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tbsp minced garlic, salt, and pepper.
3. Break up beef and cook through. Drain excess grease if needed.
4. Add beef broth, Rotel, frozen broccoli, and rinsed rice. Stir to combine.
5. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook on medium-low for about 20 minutes.
6. If liquid runs low before rice is tender, add water ⅓ cup at a time until fluffy.
7. Stir in shredded cheese, let melt, and serve.

## Notes

- Rotel and plain diced tomatoes with green chilies are interchangeable — off-brand works fine.
- Skip or swap the broccoli for any frozen veggie you have on hand.
- Reheats well in the microwave for leftovers.
- Add water ⅓ cup at a time if rice needs more liquid — every rice cooks slightly differently.
