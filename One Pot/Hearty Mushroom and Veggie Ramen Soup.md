## Ingredients

- 1 package frozen seasoned mushroom medley (variety of mushrooms with garlic and oil)
- 1 cup matchstick carrots
- 1 onion, diced (frozen diced onion works fine)
- 1 cup fresh broccoli florets
- 1 cup fresh kale, chopped
- 5 cups beef broth
- 3 cups water
- 3 tbsp low sodium soy sauce
- 2 packets ramen noodles + 1 seasoning packet

*Not counted: olive oil*

## Instructions

1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add mushroom medley, carrots, and onion. Cook about 5 minutes until mushrooms are tender and starting to brown.
2. Add broccoli florets, kale, beef broth, water, soy sauce, and 1 ramen seasoning packet. Stir.
3. Bring to a simmer. Add ramen noodles (broken up slightly if needed).
4. Simmer until ramen is tender, about 3–4 minutes.
5. Serve topped with green onions, sesame seeds, and a drizzle of chili oil if desired.

## Notes

- Frozen seasoned mushroom medley saves time and money over buying individual fresh mushroom varieties.
- Swap in any veggies you have on hand — this is very flexible.
- Add sliced pork or chicken to make it non-meatless.
- Only 1 of the 2 seasoning packets is used — save the other for another recipe.
