## Ingredients

- 1 box chicken stock (use 2 for a double batch)
- Pastina or other tiny pasta (alphabet pasta works)
- 1 sweet onion, chopped
- Carrots, chopped
- Celery, chopped (about half a bunch)
- Garlic (about 1 tsp, or fresh)
- 4 tbsp butter
- Turmeric (the "magic" ingredient)
- Garlic salt
- Parmesan cheese
- A splash of heavy cream (optional)

## Instructions

1. Add the chicken stock, onion, carrots, celery, and garlic to a pot. Bring to a boil, then simmer 20 minutes.
2. Blend smooth with an immersion blender (or transfer to a blender and back).
3. Stir in 4 tbsp butter, a little turmeric, and garlic salt.
4. Add the tiny pasta and simmer, stirring, 8–10 minutes until tender.
5. Turn off the heat and stir in a splash of heavy cream.
6. Serve topped with shredded parmesan.

## Notes

- A cozy sick-day soup — broth-based with a blended vegetable body, no chicken meat (just the stock).
- Not keto (pasta).
- "Poor man's comfort food," homemade soup edition.
