## Ingredients

- 14 oz smoked sausage
- 6 tbsp butter
- 12 oz pasta (noodles)
- 32 oz chicken broth
- 1/2 cup heavy whipping cream
- 1/4 cup shredded parmesan cheese
- Seasoning of choice

## Instructions

1. Slice the sausage and crisp it in a skillet or pot. Season.
2. Add 6 tbsp butter and let it melt over high heat.
3. Add 12 oz noodles and coat them in the butter.
4. Pour in 32 oz chicken broth and bring to a boil. Cook 8–10 minutes to cook off most of the liquid.
5. Stir in 1/2 cup heavy cream and parmesan cheese to taste.

## Notes

- A true one-pot pasta — the noodles cook right in the broth.
- Distinct from Sausage Alfredo with Zoodles (that's the keto zucchini-noodle version) — this is the regular pasta one-pot version.
- Not keto (pasta).
- Target "poor man's comfort food."
