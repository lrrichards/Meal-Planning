## Ingredients

- 7 boneless skinless chicken thighs (smaller size)
- 1 sweet potato, cut into chunks
- 1 lb golden potatoes, cut into chunks
- 1/2 lb Brussels sprouts, halved
- 3/4 cup chicken broth

*Not counted: olive oil, salt, pepper, Italian seasoning, paprika*

## Instructions

1. Season chicken thighs generously on both sides with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning.
2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pan or pot over medium-high heat. Sear chicken thighs on each side until golden.
3. Add chicken broth and scrape the bottom of the pan to loosen all the browned bits.
4. Add sweet potato, golden potatoes, and Brussels sprouts. Season everything with salt, pepper, and paprika.
5. Stir to combine. Cover and simmer on medium-low for about 30 minutes, or until chicken is cooked through and veggies are tender.
6. Serve straight from the pan.

## Notes

- Make sure your pan is large enough to hold all the veggies.
- Swap in carrots, green beans, or any veggie you have on hand.
- Great for leftovers — holds up well the next day for lunch.
