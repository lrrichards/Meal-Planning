## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 30 oz (about 2 cans) beef broth
- 2 cans (15 oz each) pinto beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 can (10 oz) tomato soup
- 2 cups rotini pasta, uncooked
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour (for queso)
- 2 cups milk (for queso)
- 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese (for queso)

*Not counted: olive oil, butter (2 tbsp, for queso), chili powder (4 tsp), cumin (1 tsp), garlic powder (1 tsp), oregano (½ tsp), salt, pepper*

**Shortcut:** Replace the homemade queso with 1½ cups store-bought queso

**Toppings:** sour cream, cilantro, lime

## Instructions

### Chili
1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add ground beef and diced onion with a dash of salt and pepper. Break up beef and cook through. Remove excess grease.
2. Add beef broth, pinto beans, diced tomatoes, tomato soup, chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, and oregano. Stir.
3. Bring to a simmer. Add rotini pasta. Cook 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until pasta is nearly tender.

### Homemade Queso
4. While chili simmers, melt 2 tbsp butter in a small saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in 2 tbsp flour and a dash of salt until combined.
5. Pour in 2 cups milk. Whisk constantly until smooth and thickened, about 3–4 minutes.
6. Add 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese. Whisk until melted and smooth.

### Combine
7. Pour queso into the chili. Stir to incorporate.
8. Simmer together for 5 more minutes until pasta is fully cooked and flavors are merged.
9. Serve topped with sour cream, cilantro, and a squeeze of lime.

## Notes

- Tomato soup (the condensed kind) might seem odd but adds a rich, slightly sweet tomato depth.
- For a faster version, stir in 1½ cups of store-bought queso instead of making it from scratch.
- Rotini holds up well in chili — other short pastas like elbow or penne also work.
- The queso makes this chili creamy and rich enough that you don't need many toppings.
