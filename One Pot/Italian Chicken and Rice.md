## Ingredients

- 1 lb chicken breast, cubed
- 1 yellow onion, diced (optional but recommended)
- 1 cup uncooked white rice
- 2½ cups chicken broth
- ½ cup heavy cream
- ⅓ cup shredded Parmesan cheese

*Not counted: butter, olive oil, salt, pepper, paprika, Italian seasoning, minced garlic*

## Instructions

1. Heat 1 tbsp butter and 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Add cubed chicken, diced onion, ½ tsp pepper, 1 tsp salt, 2 tsp paprika, 2 tsp Italian seasoning, and 1 tbsp minced garlic. Stir and cook 5–6 minutes.
3. Add uncooked rice and chicken broth. Stir to combine.
4. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook on medium-low for about 20 minutes until rice is tender.
5. Stir in heavy cream and Parmesan. Let cheese melt and stir to combine.
6. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- The heavy cream and Parmesan at the end give it an Alfredo-like quality without making a separate sauce.
- Skip the onion if you don't have one — it still works fine.
- Family-friendly — kids tend to love this one.
- Also in the creator's first cookbook, page 131.
