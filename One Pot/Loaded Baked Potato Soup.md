## Ingredients

- Potatoes
- Bacon (about 4 pieces for the soup, more for garnish)
- 1/2 onion, diced
- Butter
- 2 cups milk
- 1 cup half and half
- 1/3 cup flour
- Chicken broth
- Shredded cheese
- 1 cup sour cream
- Garlic salt, pepper

## Instructions

1. Microwave the potatoes about 13 minutes, then let them cool. Peel and cube them.
2. Crisp up the bacon and cut it into pieces.
3. In a large pot, melt butter and cook the diced onion in it.
4. Combine the 2 cups milk with 1 cup half and half. Slowly whisk 1/3 cup flour into the butter and onion, then slowly whisk in the milk mixture and keep whisking about 2 minutes.
5. Stir in the chicken broth and bring to a simmer. Season with garlic salt and pepper.
6. Stir in the cheese (a couple of handfuls) and 1 cup sour cream, then the bacon.
7. Turn off the heat and stir in the cubed potatoes (cube them small).
8. Serve with garnishes — extra bacon, cheese, green onion.

## Notes

- Comes fully loaded — bacon, cheese, and sour cream.
- Not keto (potatoes).
- "Poor man's comfort food."
