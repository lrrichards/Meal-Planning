## Ingredients

- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 can (10 oz) Rotel (diced tomatoes with green chilis)
- 1 can (15 oz) corn, drained
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1–2 lbs chicken tenderloins (or chicken breast; frozen is fine)
- 4 tbsp cream cheese, cubed

*Not counted: olive oil, butter, garlic (1 tbsp minced), taco seasoning (1 tbsp), salt, pepper*

**Toppings:** tortilla strips, sour cream, shredded cheese, avocado

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil and butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add diced onion and cook 5–7 minutes until softened.
2. Stir in minced garlic. Cook 15 seconds.
3. Add tomato paste, Rotel, drained corn, drained and rinsed black beans, chicken tenderloins, taco seasoning, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine.
4. Cover and simmer over medium heat for about 20 minutes until chicken is cooked through.
5. Remove chicken to a cutting board. Shred or chop into smaller pieces. Return to pot.
6. Add cubed cream cheese. Put lid back on and let cook 1–2 minutes to soften the cream cheese.
7. Whisk the cream cheese into the soup until fully smooth and incorporated.
8. Serve topped with tortilla strips, sour cream, cheese, and avocado.

## Notes

- Frozen chicken works fine here — no need to thaw ahead of time.
- The cream cheese step is optional but adds a rich, creamy depth that makes the soup special.
- Whisk the cream cheese in rather than stirring to prevent lumps.
- Use any beans you have — pinto, kidney, or great northern all work.
