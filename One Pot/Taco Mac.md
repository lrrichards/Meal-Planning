## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef (or any ground meat)
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 cups chicken broth (or 1 cup chicken + 1 cup beef broth)
- 1 cup milk
- 10 oz Rotel (diced tomatoes with green chilies)
- 1/2 lb small pasta (shells or elbow)
- Shredded cheese to taste

*Not counted: butter, garlic, taco seasoning, Worcestershire sauce*

## Instructions

1. Brown ground beef in a large pan over medium heat, breaking it up as it cooks.
2. Once almost cooked through, add 1 tbsp minced garlic and 1 tbsp butter. Stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
3. Add 2 tbsp taco seasoning and 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce. Stir well.
4. Add tomato paste and stir until fully incorporated.
5. Pour in broth, milk, Rotel, and pasta. Stir to combine.
6. Simmer 10–15 minutes until pasta is tender, stirring occasionally. Add more liquid if needed.
7. Stir in shredded cheese and let it melt.
8. Serve topped with sour cream and guacamole if desired.

## Notes

- Any small pasta works — shells, elbows, ditalini.
- Makes a large batch — great leftovers.
- Great for Taco Tuesday as a fun pasta twist.
