## Ingredients

- 4 chicken thighs (about half a standard package)
- 8 oz carrot
- 2–3 ribs celery, chopped
- 1 box Rice-A-Roni (chicken flavor or wild rice flavor), with seasoning packet
- 10 cups water

*Not counted: olive oil, salt, pepper, thyme, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, basil, oregano, paprika*

## Instructions

1. Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium heat.
2. Add chopped carrots and celery. Season with salt, pepper, thyme, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, basil, oregano, and paprika.
3. Sauté veggies for 5–7 minutes until slightly softened.
4. Add chicken thighs, the box of Rice-A-Roni (including the seasoning packet), and 10 cups of water. Stir to combine.
5. Bring to a simmer and cook covered for 25–30 minutes until chicken is pull-apart tender and rice is cooked through.
6. Remove chicken thighs to a bowl or plate and shred using two forks or an electric hand mixer.
7. Return shredded chicken to the pot, stir, and serve.

## Notes

- The Rice-A-Roni seasoning packet acts as free built-in seasoning — don't skip it.
- Any flavor of Rice-A-Roni works: chicken, wild rice, or garlic herb.
- Add onion at the start if you have one — adds extra flavor.
- Makes a huge pot — plan on leftovers for 2+ days.
- Shredding chicken with a hand electric mixer in a bowl is much faster than two forks.
