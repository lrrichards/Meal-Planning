## Ingredients

- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 2 large carrots, shredded
- 1 red bell pepper, chopped
- 1 cup snow peas (or more)
- 3 cups cabbage, sliced
- 1/4 cup hoisin sauce
- 1/4 cup low sodium soy sauce
- 4 cups chicken or veggie broth
- 13 oz lo mein noodles (spaghetti works as a substitute)

*Not counted: olive oil, garlic, ginger*

## Instructions

1. Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Add onion, carrots, bell pepper, and snow peas. Cook a few minutes until starting to soften.
3. Add 1 tbsp minced garlic and 1 tbsp minced ginger. Stir until fragrant.
4. Add cabbage, hoisin sauce, and soy sauce. Stir to combine.
5. Pour in broth and add lo mein noodles.
6. Stir, bring to a simmer, and cook about 10 minutes until noodles are tender.
7. Top with sesame seeds and sliced green onions to serve.

## Notes

- Swap in any veggies you like — frozen veggies work fine.
- Add cooked chicken, steak, or shrimp for a non-meatless version.
- Spaghetti noodles are a reliable substitute if lo mein noodles aren't available.
