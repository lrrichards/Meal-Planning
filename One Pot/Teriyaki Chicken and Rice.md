## Ingredients

- 1 lb chicken breast, cubed
- 1 cup uncooked white or jasmine rice, rinsed
- 1 green bell pepper, diced
- 1 cup shredded carrots
- 1 cup frozen peas
- ⅓ cup low sodium soy sauce
- ¼ cup honey
- 2½ cups chicken broth

*Not counted: olive oil, minced ginger, minced garlic, salt, pepper*

## Instructions

1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add cubed chicken, 1 tsp salt, and 1 tsp pepper. Stir and cook for a few minutes.
2. Add ½ tbsp minced ginger and 1 tbsp minced garlic. Stir until fragrant.
3. Add rinsed rice, diced bell pepper, shredded carrots, frozen peas, soy sauce, honey, and chicken broth. Stir to combine.
4. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook on medium-low for about 20 minutes or until rice is tender.
5. Stir and serve.

## Notes

- Swap or add any veggies you like — snap peas, edamame, diced onion, or sugar peas all work.
- Jar ginger or squeeze-tube ginger keeps in the fridge and saves prep time.
- Honey adds sweetness without going overboard — don't skip it.
- One of those recipes that works with whatever vegetables are on hand.
