## Ingredients

- 2 chicken breasts, cubed
- 1 box (8.3 oz) cheddar broccoli pasta mix (Big Value pack if available)
- 1/2 lb frozen broccoli
- 3 cups chicken broth
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 cup mozzarella cheese (optional, for extra cheesiness)

*Not counted: olive oil, salt, no-salt seasoning blend*

## Instructions

1. Season cubed chicken with salt and your choice of seasoning (no-salt blend, or salt/pepper/Italian seasoning).
2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Cook chicken through, then remove to a plate.
3. In the same pan, add the cheddar broccoli pasta mix, frozen broccoli, chicken broth, and heavy cream. Stir to combine.
4. Simmer until pasta is tender, stirring occasionally.
5. Stir cooked chicken back in. Add mozzarella if using and stir until melted.
6. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- The broth + cream combo is what makes this rich — don't substitute with water.
- Mozzarella is optional but worth it.
- Tastes like it took much longer than it did.
