## Ingredients

- 9 russet potatoes, peeled and cubed
- Remaining yellow onion, diced
- 1 bag (12 oz) frozen sweet corn
- Bacon bits to taste (added at end and as topping)
- 10 cups water

*Not counted: olive oil, ramen seasoning packet (used as broth flavoring), salt, pepper, crushed red pepper, paprika, garlic powder*

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add diced onion and frozen corn. Sauté together for a few minutes until they start to develop some color.
2. Add cubed potatoes and 10 cups water. Scrape any browned bits off the bottom — that's flavor.
3. Add a ramen seasoning packet (chicken flavor works well) for extra depth, plus plenty of salt, pepper, crushed red pepper, paprika, and garlic powder.
4. Bring to a simmer. Cook covered for about 15 minutes until potatoes are fork tender.
5. Stir in a portion of bacon bits. Save the rest for topping individual bowls.
6. Serve hot, topped with additional bacon bits.

## Notes

- Sautéing the onion and corn before adding potatoes and water builds the flavor base — don't skip it.
- A leftover ramen seasoning packet makes a surprisingly good soup base — no waste.
- Add heavy cream to make it a chowder if you have it on hand.
- Shredded cheese over the top takes it up a notch.
- Real bacon or turkey bacon can replace bacon bits.
