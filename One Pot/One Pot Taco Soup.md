## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef (or canned/rotisserie chicken)
- 1 can (15 oz) tomato sauce
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes, undrained
- 1 can (15 oz) corn, undrained
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, undrained
- 15 oz water (fill one empty can)

*Not counted: taco seasoning (2 tbsp), salt, pepper*

**Toppings:** sour cream, shredded cheese, diced onion, cilantro, lime

## Instructions

1. Brown ground beef in a large pot over medium-high heat, breaking it up. Drain fat.
2. Add tomato sauce, diced tomatoes, corn (with liquid), and black beans (with liquid).
3. Fill one of the empty cans with water and add it in.
4. Stir in 2 tbsp taco seasoning, salt, and pepper.
5. Bring to a simmer. Cook covered for about 20 minutes.
6. Serve topped with sour cream, shredded cheese, diced onion, cilantro, and lime.

## Notes

- Use the undrained cans — the liquid from the corn and beans acts as part of the broth.
- This is one of the simplest possible weeknight dinners: 5 ingredients, one pot, 25 minutes.
- Ground chicken, rotisserie chicken, or canned chicken all work as substitutes for the beef.
- Any beans work — pinto, kidney, or great northern instead of black beans.
- See also: Budget Chili (One Pot/ folder) — a richer chili with more spices and tomato paste.
