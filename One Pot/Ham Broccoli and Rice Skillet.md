## Ingredients

- 1 lb ham steak, diced (or pre-diced ham)
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 3/4 cup white or jasmine rice, rinsed
- 1.5 cups chicken broth
- 12 oz frozen broccoli
- 3/4 cup shredded Colby Jack cheese (optional)

*Not counted: olive oil, garlic, salt, pepper, oregano, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder*

## Instructions

1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a Dutch oven or large skillet over medium heat. Add diced onion and cook a few minutes until softened.
2. Add 1 tbsp minced garlic and stir until fragrant.
3. Add rinsed rice, chicken broth, diced ham, and frozen broccoli.
4. Season with 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper, 1 tsp oregano, 1 tsp paprika, and 1/2 tsp each garlic powder and onion powder.
5. Stir, cover, and simmer on medium-low for 15–20 minutes until rice is fluffy. Add a splash more broth if needed.
6. Stir in cheese if using and let melt before serving.

## Notes

- Rinsing the rice removes surface starch and prevents gummy texture — worth the extra minute.
- Add broth 1/3 cup at a time if rice needs more liquid.
- Cheese is optional but highly recommended.
- Ham steaks are affordable and flavorful — worth seeking out over pre-diced ham.
