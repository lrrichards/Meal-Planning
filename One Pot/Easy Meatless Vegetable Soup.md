## Ingredients

- 1 sweet potato, peeled and diced
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 1 zucchini, diced
- 2 cups kale, roughly chopped (stems removed)
- 2 cans (15 oz each) diced tomatoes
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained (or any beans)
- 6 cups vegetable broth
- 1 cup frozen or fresh green beans, chopped

*Not counted: olive oil, salt (2½ tsp), pepper (1 tsp), rosemary (1 tsp), oregano (2 tsp dried), bay leaves (2)*

## Instructions

1. Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion, carrots, and sweet potato. Season with a dash of salt and pepper.
2. Sauté for 5–7 minutes until the onion softens and the vegetables begin to cook down.
3. Add diced tomatoes, chickpeas, zucchini, and kale. Stir.
4. Pour in vegetable broth. Add 2½ tsp salt, 1 tsp pepper, 1 tsp rosemary, 2 tsp dried oregano, and 2 bay leaves.
5. Add green beans. Stir to combine.
6. Cover and simmer for about 25 minutes until all vegetables are tender.
7. Remove bay leaves before serving.

## Notes

- Use 2½ tsp salt — sweet potato and carrots need more salt than you'd expect to balance their sweetness.
- Sautéing the vegetables in oil first builds a flavor base that plain simmered soups lack.
- Swap chickpeas for any canned beans you have on hand.
- This recipe makes a large batch — excellent for meal prep and tastes better the next day.
- Any leafy green works in place of kale: spinach, Swiss chard, or collard greens.
