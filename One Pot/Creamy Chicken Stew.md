## Ingredients

- About 1 lb chicken thighs, diced small
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 pack green onions, green parts (save a few for garnish)
- 1/2 block (4 oz) cream cheese
- About 1/4 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 jar marinara sauce
- 1 1/2 zucchini, cubed
- 1/2 block (4 oz) mozzarella cheese, grated
- Olive oil, all-purpose seasoning (or salt and pepper), smoked paprika

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large oven-safe pan over medium heat. Add the diced bell pepper and green onion and cook until softened.
2. Add the diced chicken, press down, and let it sit. Season with all-purpose seasoning. Cook until nearly cooked through, flipping once.
3. Add the cream cheese to the middle and let it melt. Stir in about 1/4 cup heavy cream to start a cream sauce.
4. Add 1/2 jar marinara and stir into a creamy tomatoey sauce.
5. Stir in the cubed zucchini. Top with grated mozzarella.
6. Bake at 375°F for 20 minutes.
7. Serve in a bowl with plenty of the juice. Garnish with green onion and a dusting of smoked paprika.

## Notes

- Cream- and cheese-forward, so not tagged extreme-budget despite the cheap chicken.
- From a $70/week keto-on-a-budget week (2 people, 7 days).
