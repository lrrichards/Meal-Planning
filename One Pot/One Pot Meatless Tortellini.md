## Ingredients

- 28 oz crushed tomatoes
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 9 oz fresh or frozen cheese tortellini
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese
- 2 cups fresh spinach (optional)
- 1/2 cup water or vegetable broth

*Not counted: oil, garlic, dried oregano, dried basil, salt, pepper*

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in a large pot or skillet over medium heat. Add 1 tbsp minced garlic and cook about 1 minute.
2. Add crushed tomatoes and heavy cream. Stir and bring to a simmer.
3. Add water or veggie broth, a half cup of dried oregano, 1 tsp dried basil, 1 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp pepper.
4. Add spinach if using and stir until wilted.
5. Add tortellini and Parmesan cheese. Stir to combine.
6. Simmer about 8 minutes until tortellini is cooked through.
7. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Fresh or frozen tortellini both work — both cook quickly.
- Spinach is optional but adds nutrition and color.
- Also appears in the *Make It Simple* cookbook.
- Great when short on time — minimal prep, one pot to clean.
