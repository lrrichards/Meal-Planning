## Ingredients

- 1 block cream cheese
- 1 can chili (no beans)
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese

## Instructions

1. Spread the cream cheese in an even layer in the bottom of a microwave-safe dish.
2. Spread the can of chili (no beans) over the cream cheese.
3. Top with shredded cheddar cheese.
4. Microwave 5–6 minutes until hot and the cheese is melted.
5. Serve as a dip.

## Notes

- Keto / low-carb when you use no-bean chili.
- **Contains chili (tomato-based) — contains tomatoes.**
- A party/snack dip, not a full meal (so not tagged extreme-budget).
- Aldi "poor man's comfort food," New Year's Eve edition.
