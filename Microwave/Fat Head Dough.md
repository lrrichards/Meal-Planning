## Ingredients

- 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
- 4 oz cream cheese
- 1/2 cup almond flour
- 2 eggs

## Instructions

1. Melt the mozzarella and cream cheese together in the microwave in short intervals until smooth.
2. Mix in the almond flour and egg until a dough forms (knead a bit).
3. Use as a keto base for pizza crust, breadsticks, and more. Store in the fridge or freezer.

## Notes

- The go-to keto / low-carb dough base — make ahead and keep on hand for pizza or breadsticks.
- The transcript didn't give exact amounts (it's prepped in bulk); this is the standard fat head dough method — adjust ratios to taste (commonly ~1.5 cups mozzarella : 2 tbsp cream cheese : 3/4 cup almond flour : 1 egg).
