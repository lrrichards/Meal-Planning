## Ingredients

- 4–6 russet potatoes
- 2 cups chili
- 2 Roma tomatoes, diced

*Not counted: olive oil, seasoned salt or garlic salt*

## Instructions

1. Scrub and dry potatoes. Poke holes all over each potato with a fork.
2. Rub with a little olive oil and sprinkle with seasoned salt or garlic salt on all sides.
3. Microwave each potato on high for 5–7 minutes until fork tender (time varies by size and microwave — check every 2 minutes).
4. Reheat chili on the stove or microwave until hot.
5. Slice each potato open and lightly mash the inside.
6. Top with a generous scoop of chili and diced tomatoes.
7. Add any other toppings: shredded cheese, sour cream, green onions.

## Notes

- Seasoning the potato skin with garlic salt before microwaving makes the skin worth eating.
- Budget Chili (One Pot/ folder) was made for this — one pot of chili stretches across multiple nights of baked potatoes.
- For kids: serve chili on the side rather than on top if they prefer things separated.
- Also works great with oven-baked potatoes at 400°F for 45–60 minutes if you're not in a rush.
