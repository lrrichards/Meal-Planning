## Ingredients

- 3 lb potatoes, washed and cubed small
- 1 bag mixed vegetables
- 1 can cream soup (cream of chicken or mushroom)
- Sour cream
- 1 sweet onion (half or whole, your choice), chopped
- Shredded cheese
- Pepper, garlic salt

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 400°F. Cube the potatoes small and chop the onion.
2. In a big bowl, combine the potatoes, onion, mixed vegetables, cream soup, and all of the sour cream. Season generously with pepper and lightly with garlic salt. Mix well.
3. Dump into a baking dish and cover with foil.
4. Bake covered at 400°F for 40 minutes.
5. Remove the foil, top with shredded cheese, and bake uncovered about 10 more minutes.
6. Optional: broil 30 seconds for a browned cheese top.

## Notes

- A cheesy, creamy potato bake. The source didn't name the cream soup — cream of chicken or mushroom both work.
- Not keto (potatoes).
- "Poor man's comfort food," about $6.76 with Kroger digital coupons.
