## Ingredients

- 1 bag (32 oz) frozen diced hash browns
- 1 can (10.5 oz) cream of mushroom soup
- 1 cup French onion dip
- 2–3 cups shredded chicken (rotisserie works well)
- 1½ cups shredded cheddar cheese (divided)
- 1 cup French fried onions (crispy topping)

*Not counted: salt, pepper, garlic powder*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease a 9x13 baking dish.
2. In a large bowl, combine frozen hash browns, cream of mushroom soup, French onion dip, shredded chicken, and 1 cup cheddar cheese. Season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Mix well.
3. Pour mixture into prepared baking dish. Spread evenly.
4. Top with remaining ½ cup cheddar cheese.
5. Cover with foil and bake at 375°F for 45 minutes.
6. Remove foil. Top with French fried onions.
7. Bake uncovered for 10 more minutes until onions are crispy and casserole is bubbly.

## Notes

- No need to thaw the hash browns — they cook perfectly from frozen.
- French onion dip is the shortcut that makes this taste like it simmered for hours.
- Add the fried onion topping only in the last 10 minutes to keep them crispy.
- This is a great potluck dish — travels well and holds heat.
