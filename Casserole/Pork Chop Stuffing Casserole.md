## Ingredients

- 4 pork chops
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1 box Stove Top stuffing
- 3 cloves garlic

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to about 375°F. Lay the pork chops in the bottom of a baking dish and season with pepper and garlic salt.
2. In a bowl, mix the cream of mushroom soup with sour cream and a little more salt and pepper.
3. Spread the soup mixture over the pork chops.
4. Bake 20–30 minutes.
5. Meanwhile, make the Stove Top stuffing per the box directions (boil, fluff, rest 5 minutes, fluff again).
6. Spoon the stuffing over the top of the pork chops and return to the oven until toasted on top.

## Notes

- A childhood comfort-food casserole. Eat a bit of pork, stuffing, and the mushroom-sour-cream sauce together in each bite.
- Not keto (stuffing).
- Distinct from the rice (No Peek) and potato pork chop casseroles — this one uses stuffing.
- Food for Less "poor man's comfort food," closing-store haul.
