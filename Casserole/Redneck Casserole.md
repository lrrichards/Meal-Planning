## Ingredients

- 1 big can baked beans
- 1 package hot dogs, sliced
- 1 onion, chopped
- Tater tots (mini tots work, they just take longer to line up)
- 1 cup shredded cheese

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 425°F.
2. Grill the chopped onion and sliced hot dogs in a skillet until the onions are soft and the franks get a little crispy (grilled onion beats raw here).
3. In a baking dish (or an oven-safe skillet), mix in the baked beans with the hot dogs and onion.
4. Line the top with tater tots, flattening them into an even layer.
5. Bake at 425°F for 25 minutes, until the tots are golden and crispy.
6. Optional: sprinkle cheese over the top and let it melt.

## Notes

- "Franks and beans" comfort food — the most American thing on the list.
- **Contains baked beans (tomato-based sauce) — contains tomatoes.**
- Not keto (tater tots and beans).
- Food for Less "poor man's comfort food," cheap closing-store haul.
