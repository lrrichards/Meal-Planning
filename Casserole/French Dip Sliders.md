## Ingredients

- 1 pack Hawaiian rolls (12 count)
- 4 oz provolone cheese
- 1 lb deli roast beef
- 1 cup crispy French fried onions
- 1 packet au jus mix (1 tbsp goes into the butter mixture; the rest is whisked with 1.5 cups water for dipping)

**Butter mixture (brushed on top):**
- 5 tbsp butter, melted
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Mix melted butter, garlic, 1 tbsp of the au jus seasoning packet, sesame seeds, and Worcestershire sauce together until fully combined.
3. Slice Hawaiian rolls in half horizontally. Place the bottom half in a baking dish.
4. Layer provolone cheese on the bottom rolls, then roast beef, then brush half the butter mixture over the meat.
5. Sprinkle French fried onions over the top. Add another layer of provolone.
6. Place the top rolls on. Brush the remaining butter mixture over the tops.
7. Bake at 350°F for 8–10 minutes until cheese is melted and tops are golden.
8. Meanwhile, whisk remaining au jus packet with 1.5 cups water in a small pot. Bring to a simmer until thickened.
9. Serve sliders with au jus on the side for dipping.

## Notes

- Also appears in the *Make It Simple* cookbook, page 244.
- Serve with carrots and ranch for an easy veggie side.
- The butter mixture is what gives these the French onion flavor — don't skip it.
