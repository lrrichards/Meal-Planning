## Ingredients

- 1 cup uncooked white rice
- 10 oz red or green enchilada sauce
- 15 oz canned black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup chicken broth
- ¼ cup salsa
- 6 frozen chicken tenderloins (or thawed chicken breast)
- 1 cup Colby Jack or Mexican-style shredded cheese

*Not counted: butter (2 tbsp melted, stirred in), taco seasoning (1½ tbsp), salt, pepper, non-stick spray*

**To serve:** Avocado, cilantro, diced red onion, sour cream, lime

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Spray a 9×13 baking dish with non-stick spray.
2. Add rice, enchilada sauce, black beans, chicken broth, salsa, melted butter, and taco seasoning to the dish. Whisk together well.
3. Place chicken tenderloins over the top. Flip to coat in the sauce. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Cover tightly with foil. Bake at 350°F for 50 minutes.
5. Remove foil. Sprinkle cheese over the top. Bake uncovered for 15 more minutes.
6. Top with avocado, cilantro, red onion, sour cream, and lime before serving.

## Notes

- Frozen chicken tenderloins go straight in — no need to thaw first.
- Red or green enchilada sauce are interchangeable — use whichever you prefer or have on hand.
- Great for Taco Tuesday as an alternative to traditional tacos.
- Makes ahead well — reheat in the microwave throughout the week.
