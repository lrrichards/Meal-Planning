## Ingredients

- 1 box (6 oz) stuffing mix
- 1½ cups chicken broth (divided)
- 2–3 zucchini, diced
- ½ yellow onion, diced
- 2–3 cups shredded chicken (rotisserie works well)
- 1 cup sour cream
- ½ cup milk

*Not counted: salt, pepper, garlic powder, non-stick spray*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Spray a 9x13 baking dish with non-stick spray.
2. Prepare stuffing mix with ¾ cup chicken broth per package instructions. Set aside.
3. In a large bowl, combine shredded chicken, diced zucchini, diced onion, sour cream, milk, and remaining ¾ cup chicken broth. Season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Mix well.
4. Pour chicken and veggie mixture into prepared baking dish.
5. Spread prepared stuffing evenly over the top.
6. Bake at 350°F for 40–45 minutes until bubbly and stuffing is golden on top.

## Notes

- Rotisserie chicken makes this a true dump-and-go once you shred it.
- The stuffing forms a built-in crispy topping — no breadcrumbs needed.
- Swap zucchini for broccoli, green beans, or any soft vegetable.
- Great for using up zucchini in peak summer when gardens are overflowing.
