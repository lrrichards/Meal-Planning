## Ingredients

- 15 oz canned pinto beans, drained and rinsed
- 10 oz diced tomatoes with green chilies (Rotel or off-brand)
- 10 oz green or red enchilada sauce
- 1¼ cups veggie broth
- 1 cup uncooked quinoa
- Shredded Mexican-style cheese (generous amount for topping)

*Not counted: taco seasoning (1½ tbsp), non-stick spray*

**To serve:** Cilantro, diced red onion, salsa, sour cream, avocado

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Spray a 9×13 baking dish with non-stick spray.
2. Add pinto beans, diced tomatoes with green chilies, enchilada sauce, veggie broth, quinoa, and taco seasoning to the dish. Stir well.
3. Cover tightly with foil. Bake at 375°F for 25 minutes.
4. Remove foil. Stir the quinoa into the mixture.
5. Sprinkle shredded cheese generously over the top.
6. Bake uncovered for an additional 15 minutes until cheese is melted and bubbly.
7. Top with cilantro, red onion, salsa, sour cream, and avocado before serving.

## Notes

- Quinoa is a great meat substitute here — high fiber, fairly affordable, and packed with nutrients.
- Don't skip the stir after removing the foil — it helps the quinoa absorb evenly.
- Go heavy on the cheese topping — it makes the dish.
- Great for meal prep: portions well into containers for weekday lunches.
- If you don't have taco seasoning: use chili powder, cumin, salt, pepper, onion powder, garlic powder, and oregano.
