## Ingredients

- 10 eggs
- ½ lb ground turkey
- A generous amount of frozen mixed vegetables (less than half a standard bag)
- Small amount of yellow onion, diced
- ¼ cup water (or milk if available)

*Not counted: taco seasoning, salt, pepper, non-stick spray*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Spray a baking dish with non-stick spray.
2. Cook ground turkey in a pan over medium heat, seasoning with taco seasoning. Break up and cook through. Remove from heat.
3. In a large bowl, crack 10 eggs. Add salt, pepper, and ¼ cup water (or milk). Whisk well.
4. Stir in cooked ground turkey, frozen mixed vegetables, and diced onion.
5. Pour into prepared baking dish.
6. Bake at 350°F for 30–40 minutes until egg is set in the center.
7. Cut into pieces and serve.

## Notes

- Water instead of milk makes the eggs slightly lighter and fluffier — works perfectly.
- Taco seasoning on the turkey means you barely need to season the egg mixture separately.
- Makes a huge amount — fills a baking dish and feeds a family of 4–5 with leftovers.
- Good for breakfast-for-dinner nights and great as leftover breakfast the next morning.
- Swap frozen mixed vegetables for any vegetables you have on hand.
