## Ingredients

- 1 cup uncooked white rice, cooked (about 2 cups cooked)
- 2.5 cups cooked Mayocoba beans (or canned pinto beans)
- 2–3 green onions, sliced
- 1 can green enchilada sauce
- 10 corn tortillas

## Instructions

1. Cook rice (stovetop or Instant Pot). Mix cooked rice with beans, green onions, and 1/3 cup of the enchilada sauce. This is the filling.
2. Warm corn tortillas: wrap them in a damp paper towel and microwave 1–2 minutes until pliable. This prevents cracking when rolling.
3. Preheat oven to 400°F. Pour a thin layer of enchilada sauce on the bottom of a large baking dish.
4. Fill each tortilla generously with the rice and bean mixture. Roll and place seam-side down in the dish, side by side.
5. Drizzle a little oil over the tops of the enchiladas. Bake uncovered at 400°F for 8 minutes.
6. Remove from oven. Brush remaining enchilada sauce over the tops.
7. Return to oven and bake another 15 minutes.
8. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Do NOT pour all the sauce on before the first bake — this causes soggy enchiladas. Add sauce after the first 8 minutes.
- Mayocoba beans are high in protein, cause less digestive discomfort than pinto beans, and absorb seasoning very well.
- Dry Mayocoba beans: rinse, simmer in 8 cups water with seasonings for 3–3.5 hours until creamy.
- Canned pinto beans are a quick substitute.
- Part of a $25 Walmart budget week.
