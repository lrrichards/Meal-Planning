## Ingredients

- 1 can (10.5 oz) cream of mushroom soup
- ½ cup milk
- 1 box (6 oz) stuffing mix
- ¾ cup chicken broth
- 1.5 lbs chicken tenderloins
- 1½ cups Colby Jack cheese, shredded

*Not counted: salt, pepper, garlic powder, non-stick spray*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Spray a 9x13 baking dish with non-stick spray.
2. In a bowl, mix cream of mushroom soup and milk until smooth. Pour into prepared baking dish.
3. Lay chicken tenderloins on top of soup mixture in a single layer. Season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
4. In a separate bowl, combine stuffing mix and chicken broth. Stir to moisten. Spread over chicken.
5. Cover tightly with foil.
6. Bake covered at 350°F for 45 minutes. Do NOT peek — the steam is what cooks the chicken through.
7. Remove foil, top with Colby Jack cheese, and bake uncovered 10 more minutes until cheese is melted and bubbly.

## Notes

- "No peek" is the rule — lifting the foil lets steam escape and can leave chicken undercooked.
- The soup layer keeps the chicken incredibly moist while the stuffing on top crisps up.
- Use chicken broth instead of the stuffing's water recommendation for extra flavor.
- Works great with cream of chicken soup instead of cream of mushroom.
