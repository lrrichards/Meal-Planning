## Ingredients

- 1 bag egg noodles
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- Canned tuna (with its water)
- 1 can mixed vegetables (or frozen)
- Crackers, for the crunchy topping
- Onion, diced
- Milk (optional, for moisture)
- Garlic salt, pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to about 375°F. Cook the egg noodles al dente — they finish cooking in the oven. Drain and return to the pot.
2. Stir in the cream of chicken soup.
3. Add the tuna along with its can water (this keeps it from being dry). If you want it saucier, add half a can of water and half a can of milk.
4. Stir in the mixed vegetables, diced onion, pepper, and a little garlic salt. Stir well.
5. Transfer to a baking dish.
6. Crumble the crackers over the top for crunch.
7. Bake about 15 minutes, until heated through and the top is crispy.

## Notes

- Use the tuna's can water for moisture — nobody likes a dry casserole.
- Not keto (egg noodles).
- Evan-safe (no tomatoes).
- Dollar Tree "poor man's comfort food," a family dinner under $10.
