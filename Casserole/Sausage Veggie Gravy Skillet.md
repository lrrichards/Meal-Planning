## Ingredients

- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1 lb golden potatoes, halved or diced
- 2 cups baby carrots (or sliced regular carrots)
- 6 chicken and apple sausages (or any sausage)
- 1 packet au jus mix
- 1.5 cups water

*Not counted: olive oil, salt*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. To a large baking dish, add 1 tbsp olive oil. Add onion, potatoes, carrots, and sausages. Season with 1/2 tsp salt and another drizzle of olive oil. Toss to coat.
3. In a small bowl, whisk the au jus packet into 1.5 cups water.
4. Pour the au jus mixture over the top of the sausage and veggies.
5. Cover tightly with foil.
6. Bake at 400°F for about 45 minutes until veggies are tender and sausages are cooked through.
7. Serve straight from the dish.

## Notes

- Brown gravy packet works as a substitute for au jus.
- Any sausage works — Italian, smoked, bratwurst.
- Great cold-weather comfort meal — minimal prep, oven does the work.
