## Ingredients

- 12 oz penne pasta, uncooked
- 24 oz (1 jar) pasta sauce
- 1.5 lbs chicken breast, cubed
- 1½ cups mozzarella cheese, shredded
- ½ cup parmesan cheese, shredded
- ½ cup panko breadcrumbs

*Not counted: olive oil, Italian seasoning, salt, pepper, garlic powder, butter (for breadcrumbs)*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Grease a 9x13 baking dish.
2. Add uncooked penne pasta to baking dish. Pour pasta sauce over pasta.
3. Add cubed chicken breast on top. Season everything with Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
4. Add 1½ cups water to thin out the sauce so the pasta cooks. Stir to distribute.
5. Cover tightly with foil and bake at 400°F for 35 minutes.
6. Remove foil. Top with mozzarella, parmesan, and panko breadcrumbs (toss breadcrumbs in a little melted butter first).
7. Bake uncovered 12–15 minutes until cheese is melted and breadcrumbs are golden.

## Notes

- The pasta cooks directly in the sauce — no boiling needed.
- Adding water to the sauce is key — the pasta absorbs liquid as it cooks, so it needs extra.
- Panko gives the classic Chicken Parm crunch without frying anything.
- Check pasta is cooked through before removing foil — if not, give it another 5 minutes covered.
