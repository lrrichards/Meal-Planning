## Ingredients

- 4 chicken thighs (about half a standard package)
- 1 box Rice-A-Roni (chicken and garlic flavor recommended), with seasoning packet
- 12 oz frozen broccoli florets
- 2½ cups water

*Not counted: non-stick spray, salt, pepper, basil, paprika, garlic powder*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Spray a 9×13 baking dish with non-stick spray.
2. Add frozen broccoli to the dish, then pour in the Rice-A-Roni (including the seasoning packet).
3. Add 2½ cups water and stir so the rice is spread across the bottom of the dish.
4. Lay chicken thighs on top of the rice and broccoli.
5. Season chicken with salt, pepper, basil, paprika, and garlic powder.
6. Cover tightly with foil.
7. Bake at 350°F for 50 minutes, or until rice is tender and chicken is cooked through.
8. Serve immediately — chicken, rice, and broccoli all together.

## Notes

- Chicken and garlic Rice-A-Roni works great here, but any box flavor will do.
- Any frozen veggie can replace broccoli — whatever the family likes.
- Chicken thighs stay juicier than breast in the oven — worth using here.
- 3 ingredients, one baking dish, nearly zero prep.
