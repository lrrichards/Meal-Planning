## Ingredients

- 4 zucchinis
- 1 lb ground turkey (or Italian sausage)
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 24 oz pasta sauce
- ½ cup + extra mozzarella cheese

*Not counted: salt, pepper, Italian seasoning, non-stick spray*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Spray a large baking dish with non-stick spray.
2. Slice zucchinis in half lengthwise. Scoop out the inside with a spoon to form boats. Reserve the scooped zucchini for another use (e.g., add to spaghetti sauce).
3. In a pan over medium heat, cook ground turkey and diced onion with salt, pepper, and ½ tsp Italian seasoning. Break up and cook through.
4. Add pasta sauce to the pan. Stir and simmer for 2 minutes.
5. Stir in ½ cup mozzarella and let melt.
6. Place zucchini boats in the baking dish. Fill each one with the turkey pasta mixture using a spoon or cookie scoop.
7. Sprinkle additional mozzarella over the top.
8. Cover with foil. Bake at 350°F for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake uncovered for 10 more minutes.
9. Serve with a side salad.

## Notes

- Scooping out the zucchini is easier than it looks — a regular spoon works fine.
- Italian sausage instead of turkey makes this even more flavorful.
- Save the scooped zucchini pulp in the fridge — stir it into spaghetti sauce to stretch the sauce further.
- Cookie scoop is the easiest way to fill the boats neatly.
