## Ingredients

- 2 lb chicken thighs
- 8 oz ham (about half a package)
- 4 oz Swiss cheese
- 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
- 1/4 cup butter
- Salt, pepper, and seasoning of choice

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven (about 375°F). Cut the chicken thighs into bite-sized pieces and spread them in a baking dish.
2. Season the chicken well — it needs it.
3. Layer the ham over the chicken, then cover completely with Swiss cheese.
4. In a small bowl, slowly mix the melted butter into the panko breadcrumbs until just buttery — don't overdo it.
5. Spread the buttered crumbs over the top.
6. Bake until the chicken is cooked through and the topping is golden and crispy.

## Notes

- A Facebook/AI cordon bleu casserole — chicken, ham, and Swiss with a crispy panko top.
- **Low-carb, not strict keto** — only the panko topping adds carbs. Swap the panko for crushed pork rinds and it becomes keto.
- Aldi "poor man's comfort food," about a $15 dinner — above the extreme-budget range, so not tagged extreme-budget.
