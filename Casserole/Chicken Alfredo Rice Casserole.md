## Ingredients

- 1 cup uncooked white rice (yields ~2½ cups cooked)
- 2 cups shredded chicken (rotisserie works great)
- 1½ cups frozen peas
- ½ cup milk
- ¼ cup sun-dried tomatoes
- 16 oz Alfredo sauce
- ½ cup panko breadcrumbs (Italian-style recommended)
- ¼ cup melted butter (for topping)

*Not counted: salt, pepper, onion powder, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, butter, olive oil*

## Instructions

1. Cook rice: Instant Pot — 1 cup rice + 1 cup cold water, dash of salt, pepper, and olive oil, press Rice button. Alternatively cook on stovetop or use pre-cooked rice.
2. Preheat oven to 350°F. Spray a 9×13 baking dish with non-stick spray.
3. In a large bowl, combine cooked rice, shredded chicken, frozen peas, milk, sun-dried tomatoes, Alfredo sauce, 1 tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper, 1 tsp onion powder, 1 tsp garlic powder, and 2 tsp Italian seasoning. Stir well.
4. Pour into prepared baking dish. Cover tightly with foil.
5. Bake at 350°F for 20 minutes.
6. Remove foil. Sprinkle panko breadcrumbs evenly over the top, then drizzle with melted butter.
7. Bake uncovered for another 15 minutes until topping is golden.
8. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Store-bought Alfredo mixed with all the other ingredients tastes much better than eating it straight — don't be put off by jarred sauce.
- Italian-style panko adds more flavor than plain — worth finding if available.
- Skip sun-dried tomatoes if not a fan — the casserole still works without them.
- Skip peas or swap for another frozen veggie.
- Chilled rotisserie chicken from the store (day-old, heavily discounted) is a great budget option for the shredded chicken.
