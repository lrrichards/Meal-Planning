## Ingredients

- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup (with roasted garlic if available)
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 1.5 cups uncooked jasmine or white rice
- 1 packet Lipton onion soup mix
- 4 boneless pork chops

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Spray a 9x13 baking dish with non-stick spray.
2. Add cream of chicken soup, cream of mushroom soup, chicken broth, and rice to the dish. Whisk together well.
3. Sprinkle half the onion soup mix into the dish and stir again.
4. Place pork chops on top in a single layer — do not overlap.
5. Sprinkle the remaining half of the soup mix over the pork chops.
6. Cover tightly with foil.
7. Bake at 350°F for 75 minutes until rice is fluffy and pork chops are tender.
8. Serve with steamed carrots or your preferred veggie side.

## Notes

- Do NOT peek while it bakes — lifting the foil lets steam escape and the rice won't cook properly.
- Cream of mushroom with roasted garlic adds extra flavor — use it if your store carries it.
- Pork chops come out very tender, not dry — this is not your average pork chop dinner.
