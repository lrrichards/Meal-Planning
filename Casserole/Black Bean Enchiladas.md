## Ingredients

- 1 lb dried black beans (or 2 cans black beans, drained)
- Enchilada sauce (about 1.5 cups total)
- 6 corn tortillas (for 3 layers, 2 per layer — adjust for your dish)
- 4 oz shredded sharp cheddar cheese
- Optional toppings: diced tomatoes, cilantro

**For cooking dried beans (Instant Pot method):**
- 6 cups water
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tbsp salt
- Seasonings: 1 tsp oregano, 1/2 tbsp chili powder

## Instructions

**If cooking dried beans from scratch:**
1. Sort and rinse black beans. Add to Instant Pot with 6 cups water, olive oil, bay leaves, salt, oregano, and chili powder.
2. Set to Bean mode for 30 minutes. Natural release for 10 minutes. Remove bay leaves. Drain beans.
   *(Stovetop alternative: simmer covered 1.5–2 hours until tender. Slow cooker: cook on low 6–8 hours.)*

**Assemble enchiladas:**
3. Preheat oven to 375°F.
4. Pour a thin layer of enchilada sauce on the bottom of a 9x13 baking dish.
5. Layer 2 corn tortillas over the sauce. Add a layer of drained black beans. Repeat twice more for 3 total layers.
6. Pour remaining enchilada sauce over the top. Sprinkle with sharp cheddar cheese.
7. Bake at 375°F for 25–30 minutes until cheese is melted and edges are bubbly.
8. Top with diced tomatoes and cilantro if desired.

## Notes

- This is a layered casserole style, not rolled enchiladas.
- Draining the beans before layering is important — excess liquid makes soggy enchiladas.
- Canned black beans (2 cans, drained) work as a quick substitute for dried.
- High in fiber, potassium, and vitamin B6 — very filling despite being meatless.
