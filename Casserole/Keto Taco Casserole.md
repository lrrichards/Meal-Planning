## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- A little water
- 1 can Rotel (mild or spicy)
- Shredded cheese (a generous amount)

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 375°F. Brown the ground beef with the diced onion.
2. Add the taco seasoning and a little water. Stir until it's really good taco meat.
3. Spread the taco meat in a casserole dish.
4. Top with a can of Rotel, then a layer of cheese, then more cheese.
5. Bake until hot and the cheese is melted and bubbly.

## Notes

- Keto / low-carb taco comfort food — no beans, rice, or tortillas.
- **Contains Rotel (tomatoes and green chilies) — not Evan-safe.**
- Distinct from the taco pasta, taco rice, and taco soup recipes (this one is grain-free).
- Cheap ground-beef base.
