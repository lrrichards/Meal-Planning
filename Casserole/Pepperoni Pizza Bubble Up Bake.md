## Ingredients

- 1 jumbo can of biscuits
- 1 small can tomato sauce (use about 1/2 cup)
- Shredded mozzarella cheese
- Pepperoni slices

*Not counted: oil or non-stick spray, salt, pepper, garlic powder, Italian seasoning*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a baking dish with non-stick spray or oil.
2. Cut each biscuit into 8 smaller pieces. Add all pieces to the baking dish.
3. Make the pizza sauce: combine 1/2 cup tomato sauce with a pinch of salt, pepper, garlic powder, and Italian seasoning. Stir.
4. Pour sauce over the biscuit pieces.
5. Top with shredded mozzarella, then layer pepperoni over the cheese.
6. Sprinkle Italian seasoning over the top.
7. Bake at 350°F for about 25 minutes until biscuits are cooked through and cheese is bubbly.
8. Serve alongside carrots and ranch.

## Notes

- Homemade pizza sauce from plain tomato sauce saves significant money over store-bought pizza sauce.
- Use any toppings you like — this is just a base recipe.
- Great budget alternative to takeout pizza night.
- Only 4 ingredients.
