## Ingredients

- ½ large yellow onion, diced
- 2 ribs celery, diced
- 2 large carrots, diced
- 1½ cups uncooked wild rice
- ½ cup uncooked orzo
- 3¼ cups chicken broth (or veggie broth)
- 6 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- Sliced butter to place over chicken (optional)

*Not counted: minced garlic (1 tbsp), salt, pepper, paprika, thyme, Italian seasoning, rosemary, non-stick spray*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Spray a 9×13 baking dish with non-stick spray.
2. Add diced onion, celery, carrots, and 1 tbsp minced garlic to the dish.
3. Add wild rice and orzo. Season with 1 tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper, ½ tsp paprika, ½ tsp thyme, ½ tsp Italian seasoning, and ½ tsp rosemary. Pour in chicken broth.
4. Stir everything together well.
5. Lay chicken thighs on top. Season with salt, pepper, paprika, Italian seasoning, and thyme. Place sliced butter over chicken if using.
6. Cover tightly with foil. Bake at 400°F for 65 minutes.
7. Remove foil. Stir the rice and orzo mixture. Bake uncovered for an additional 20 minutes until orzo and wild rice are tender.
8. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Wild rice is relatively high in protein and low in fat — worth seeking out over regular white rice here.
- Orzo adds a pasta-like texture and helps the dish come together without getting too dense.
- Chicken thighs stay juicier than breast after 85 minutes in the oven — use them here.
- Stores and reheats very well — great for making ahead and portioning for lunches.
