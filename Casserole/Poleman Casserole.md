## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 8 hot dogs
- 1/2 cup bbq sauce
- 1 can biscuits
- Seasoning of choice

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 350°F. Brown the ground beef in a skillet, seasoning to taste.
2. Chop the hot dogs and add them to the beef — the rendered grease crisps them up. Remove excess grease if it gets out of hand.
3. Stir in enough BBQ sauce to coat the beef and hot dogs.
4. Spread the mixture in the bottom of a baking dish.
5. Stretch out the biscuits and lay them over the top.
6. Bake at 350°F until the biscuits are cooked through.

## Notes

- A follower (Tinka) recipe — "Poleman casserole," a dinner for under $10.
- Distinct from Redneck Casserole (that one is franks + baked beans + tater tots).
- Not keto (biscuits + BBQ sauce).
