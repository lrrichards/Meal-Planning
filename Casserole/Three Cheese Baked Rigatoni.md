## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 lb ground Italian sausage
- Rigatoni
- 1 jar marinara sauce
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- Ricotta cheese
- Shredded mozzarella cheese
- Parmesan cheese
- 1 egg
- Garlic salt, Italian seasoning, onion powder, garlic powder, parsley

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 375°F. Brown the ground beef and Italian sausage together (the 50/50). Season with garlic salt, Italian seasoning, and onion powder.
2. Once crispy, stir in the marinara sauce and diced tomatoes.
3. Microwave (or boil) the rigatoni to al dente, then drain.
4. In a bowl, mix the ricotta with 1 egg, parsley, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, and a little parmesan.
5. Stir the drained pasta into the meat sauce.
6. Layer in a baking dish: half the pasta, dollops of the ricotta mixture, then the rest of the pasta. Top with mozzarella.
7. Cover loosely with foil and bake 20 minutes, then uncover and bake about 20 more (around 30–40 minutes total) until bubbly and golden.

## Notes

- Baked-ziti style with three cheeses (ricotta, mozzarella, parmesan) and a beef-sausage sauce.
- **Contains marinara and diced tomatoes — not Evan-safe.**
- Not keto (pasta).
- "Poor man's comfort food."
