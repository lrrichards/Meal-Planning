## Ingredients

- 3 cups shredded chicken (rotisserie works great)
- 8 oz cream cheese, softened
- 1 can (10.5 oz) cream of chicken soup
- 1 cup cottage cheese
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1½ cups Ritz crackers, crushed

*Not counted: salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, butter (for topping)*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x13 baking dish.
2. In a large bowl, combine shredded chicken, softened cream cheese, cream of chicken soup, cottage cheese, and sour cream. Season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder. Mix until fully combined.
3. Spread mixture evenly into prepared baking dish.
4. Crush Ritz crackers and sprinkle evenly over top. Drizzle with a little melted butter.
5. Bake at 350°F for 30–35 minutes until bubbly around the edges and topping is golden.

## Notes

- Cottage cheese melts into the dish and disappears — kids won't know it's there.
- The Ritz topping is what makes this feel rich and special — use the full amount.
- Rotisserie chicken makes this a 15-minute prep.
- This is a crowd-pleaser and works well for potlucks — double the recipe for a large group.
