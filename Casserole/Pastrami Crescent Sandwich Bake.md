## Ingredients

- 2 cans (8 oz each) crescent roll sheets (seamless, not the perforated triangles)
- 4–6 slices Swiss cheese
- ½ lb pastrami, thin sliced
- ¼ cup Thousand Island dressing
- ½ cup sauerkraut, drained and squeezed dry

*Not counted: non-stick spray, egg (optional, for egg wash)*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Unroll one crescent sheet and place on baking sheet.
3. Spread Thousand Island dressing over the dough, leaving a 1-inch border around the edges.
4. Layer Swiss cheese slices over the dressing.
5. Pile pastrami evenly over the cheese.
6. Top pastrami with sauerkraut — make sure it's well drained so the dough doesn't get soggy.
7. Unroll second crescent sheet and lay over the top. Pinch and seal all edges tightly.
8. Score the top lightly with a knife (3–4 slashes) for steam to escape. Brush with egg wash if desired.
9. Bake at 375°F for 22–25 minutes until deep golden brown.
10. Let rest 5 minutes, then slice and serve.

## Notes

- Use crescent sheets (seamless) not triangle rolls — the seamless sheet holds the filling in.
- Squeeze sauerkraut extremely dry — excess moisture will make the bottom soggy.
- This is essentially a Reuben sandwich in a baked format.
- Leftovers reheat well in an air fryer at 350°F for 5 minutes to re-crisp the crust.
