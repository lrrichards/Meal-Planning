## Ingredients

- 1 can coconut milk
- 3 tbsp red curry paste
- 2 tbsp low sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey (or brown sugar)
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 1.5 cups fresh spinach, chopped
- 3 packets ramen noodles (seasoning packets not used — set aside for another recipe)
- 1 cup water

*Not counted: garlic, ginger*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Spray a 9x13 baking dish with non-stick spray.
2. Add coconut milk, 1 tbsp minced garlic, 1 tbsp minced ginger, red curry paste, soy sauce, honey, and lime juice to the baking dish. Whisk together until combined and a coral-orange color.
3. Add chopped spinach and break ramen noodles into the dish (break them up slightly if needed to fit). Add 1 cup water.
4. Cover tightly with foil.
5. Bake at 350°F for 35–45 minutes until ramen is cooked through.
6. Remove foil. If any ramen on top isn't fully cooked, stir it in — the hot sauce will finish it.
7. Top with chili oil flakes, sliced green onions, sesame seeds, and a squeeze of lime.

## Notes

- Do NOT use the ramen seasoning packets — the coconut curry sauce provides all the flavor.
- Cubed chicken can be added before baking for extra protein.
- Chili oil flakes on top are optional but add a great kick.
- Stir well after baking — the sauce is rich and needs to be distributed evenly.
