## Ingredients

- About 2 1/2 lbs block cheese, shredded by hand (do not use pre-shredded) — the viral version uses a blend of sharp cheddar, mozzarella, and Colby Jack
- 1 can evaporated milk
- 1 lb cavatappi (swirly) pasta
- 3 tbsp butter
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- Seasonings: garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, pepper (the source listed these on-screen — adjust to taste)

## Instructions

1. Shred all the cheese by hand and mix the varieties together in a bowl. Mix the seasonings together separately.
2. Cook the pasta al dente. Drain and set aside.
3. Heat oven to 350°F.
4. In a skillet, melt 3 tbsp butter. Stir in half the seasoning mix.
5. Whisk in 3 tbsp flour to make a roux.
6. Whisk in the whole can of evaporated milk until thick and bubbly. Add the rest of the seasoning.
7. Keep whisking until thick, then stir in half of the cheese to make the sauce.
8. Add the cooked pasta to the cheese sauce and stir until creamy.
9. Layer in a baking dish: half the pasta, a sprinkle of cheese, the rest of the pasta, then the rest of the cheese on top.
10. Bake 25 minutes until bubbly and golden.

## Notes

- Shredding from a block is the key step — pre-shredded cheese has anti-caking agents that keep the sauce from going smooth.
