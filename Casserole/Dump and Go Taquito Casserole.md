## Ingredients

- 1 can (16 oz) refried beans
- 20–24 frozen taquitos (beef, chicken, or mixed)
- 1 can (10 oz) red enchilada sauce
- 1½ cups shredded Mexican cheese blend

*Not counted: non-stick spray*

**Optional toppings:** sour cream, salsa, guacamole, diced tomatoes, sliced jalapeños

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Spray a 9x13 baking dish with non-stick spray.
2. Spread refried beans across the bottom of the baking dish in an even layer.
3. Arrange frozen taquitos in rows on top of the beans, tightly packed.
4. Pour enchilada sauce evenly over the taquitos.
5. Top with shredded Mexican cheese.
6. Bake at 400°F for 20–25 minutes until taquitos are crispy and cheese is melted and bubbly.
7. Serve topped with sour cream, salsa, or guacamole.

## Notes

- No thawing required — goes straight from freezer to oven.
- The refried bean layer keeps the bottom from drying out and adds extra flavor and protein.
- Beef taquitos are classic but chicken works just as well.
- A true 5-minute prep — great for busy weeknights.
