## Ingredients

For the egg wraps (or use store-bought egg wraps / low-carb tortillas):
- 4 cups egg whites (about 32 eggs' worth; liquid egg whites are easiest)
- 1 tsp xanthan gum (or 1/2 cup protein powder)

For the enchiladas:
- 3 lbs ground beef
- Salt
- 1 can enchilada sauce
- Cheddar cheese, shredded by hand from a block

## Instructions

1. Make the egg wraps: blend the egg whites with the xanthan gum until smooth and frothy. Pour just enough into an oiled non-stick skillet over medium-low to cover the bottom. Cook until you can slide a spatula under, flip, and cook a few minutes more. Repeat to make about 16 wraps.
2. Brown the 3 lbs ground beef in a large skillet over medium-high, seasoning with salt.
3. Stir about 1/2 cup enchilada sauce into the beef.
4. Heat oven to 375°F. Pour a splash of sauce in the bottom of a baking dish.
5. Fill each egg wrap with beef and shredded cheese, roll, and place seam-down. Make 8 per dish.
6. Pour more sauce over the top and add more cheese. Fill a second dish with the rest.
7. Bake at 375°F about 15 minutes, until the cheese is melted and bubbly.

## Notes

- Keto / low-carb — homemade egg-white wraps replace tortillas. Makes 2 trays (16 servings) at about $1.48 each.
- Store-bought egg wraps are easier (and less "eggy") but cost more; low-carb tortillas work too but have gluten.
- **Contains enchilada sauce (tomato-based) — contains tomatoes.**
- Distinct from the bean enchiladas (those use tortillas and beans and aren't keto).
- Shred cheese from a block — cheaper and melts better.
