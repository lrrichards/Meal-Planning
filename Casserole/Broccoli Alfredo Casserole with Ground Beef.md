## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef (or ground turkey or chicken)
- 32 oz frozen broccoli florets
- 22 oz Alfredo sauce
- ⅓ cup + ⅓ cup Parmesan cheese (divided)
- ½ cup + ½ cup shredded cheddar cheese (divided)

*Not counted: minced garlic (1 tbsp), olive oil, salt, pepper, Italian seasoning, non-stick spray*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Cook ground beef in a pan over medium heat, breaking it up. Add 1 tbsp minced garlic, ½ tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper, and 1 tsp Italian seasoning. Cook through and remove from heat.
3. Boil broccoli in a large pot of water for just a few minutes — you want it no longer frozen but not fully cooked. Drain well.
4. Add cooked ground beef and drained broccoli to a 9×13 baking dish. Stir to distribute.
5. Pour Alfredo sauce over the top. Season with additional salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning. Stir together.
6. Stir in ⅓ cup Parmesan and ½ cup cheddar. Sprinkle remaining ⅓ cup Parmesan and ½ cup cheddar over the top.
7. Bake at 350°F for 25 minutes until bubbly and golden on top.
8. Serve over rice, cauliflower rice, or mashed potatoes.

## Notes

- Don't fully cook the broccoli before baking — it will finish in the oven and stay slightly firm.
- Lower-carb option: serve over cauliflower rice instead of regular rice or potatoes.
- Ground turkey or chicken can replace ground beef for a leaner version.
- Drain broccoli thoroughly before adding — excess water dilutes the Alfredo sauce.
