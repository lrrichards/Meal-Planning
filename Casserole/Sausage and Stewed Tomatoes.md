## Ingredients

- 2 cans stewed tomatoes
- 14 oz smoked sausage
- 1 small onion, sliced
- 8 oz pasta
- 1 cup shredded cheese

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 375°F. Slice the onion and put it in the bottom of a baking dish (season it; grilling the onion first is even better).
2. Add the sausages. Once browning/crispy, chop them up.
3. Pour the 2 cans of stewed tomatoes right on top.
4. Bake at 375°F for at least 15 minutes.
5. Cook the pasta (the microwave works) and drain.
6. Serve the sausage and tomatoes over the pasta. Sprinkle cheese on top, or stir cheese into the sauce to thicken it if it's watery.

## Notes

- A simple dad recipe — sausage baked in stewed tomatoes, served over pasta or rice.
- **Contains stewed tomatoes — contains tomatoes.**
- Distinct from Creamy Tomato Sausage Pasta and Sausage and Tomato Gnocchi (this is a baked, non-creamy stewed-tomato version).
- Not keto (pasta).
- "Poor man's comfort food," father's recipe edition.
