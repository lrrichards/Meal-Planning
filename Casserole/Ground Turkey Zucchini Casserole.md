## Ingredients

- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1½ lb ground turkey (or ground beef or chicken)
- 3 zucchini, sliced into rounds
- 28 oz canned diced tomatoes, drained (or 2× 14 oz cans)
- ½ cup + ½ cup mozzarella cheese (divided)
- ½ cup + ½ cup Parmesan cheese (divided)
- ¾ cup Italian-style panko breadcrumbs
- 3 tbsp olive oil (for breadcrumbs)

*Not counted: minced garlic (1 tbsp), olive oil, salt, pepper, Italian seasoning, paprika, oregano, non-stick spray*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. In a large pan over medium heat, cook diced onion, 1½ tbsp minced garlic, and ground turkey together. Season with 2¼ tsp salt, 1½ tsp pepper, 1½ tsp Italian seasoning, 1½ tsp paprika, and oregano. Break up the turkey and cook through.
3. Add drained diced tomatoes and sliced zucchini. Stir and cook for about 5 minutes until zucchini begins to soften. Remove from heat.
4. Transfer mixture to a sprayed 9×13 baking dish.
5. Stir in ½ cup mozzarella and ½ cup Parmesan. Sprinkle another ½ cup each of both cheeses over the top.
6. Mix panko breadcrumbs with 3 tbsp olive oil. Sprinkle evenly over the top.
7. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes until bubbly and breadcrumbs are golden.

## Notes

- Season aggressively — turkey and zucchini are both mild on their own.
- Italian-style panko adds much more flavor than plain — if using plain breadcrumbs, mix in Italian seasoning, oregano, salt, and pepper.
- Good lower-carb option — no rice or pasta.
- Ground beef works equally well if turkey isn't on hand.
