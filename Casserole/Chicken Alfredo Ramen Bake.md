## Ingredients

- 6 packets ramen noodles (use only 1 seasoning packet — discard or save the rest)
- 1 rotisserie chicken, shredded (or any cooked shredded chicken, or canned chicken)
- Alfredo sauce — powdered packet mix (2 packets + milk per package), jarred, or homemade
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 3/4 cup Parmesan cheese
- 1 cup water

*Not counted: salt, pepper, Italian seasoning, non-stick spray*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Spray a 9x13 baking dish with non-stick spray.
2. Prepare Alfredo sauce per package or jar instructions.
3. Lay ramen noodles flat across the bottom of the baking dish. Sprinkle 1 ramen seasoning packet evenly over the noodles.
4. Layer shredded chicken over the ramen.
5. Pour Alfredo sauce and heavy cream over everything. Add 1 cup water. Season with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning.
6. Sprinkle Parmesan cheese over the top.
7. Cover tightly with foil. Bake at 350°F for about 45 minutes until ramen is tender.
8. Remove from oven and stir well — ramen clumps during baking and needs to be distributed through the sauce.
9. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Stir thoroughly after baking — this is the key step that makes it saucy instead of clumped.
- Only 1 of the 6 seasoning packets is used — save the others for soups or stir fries.
- Rotisserie chicken is the most convenient protein here. Canned chicken works as a budget substitute.
- Honest rating: good but not as good as traditional fettuccine Alfredo — a fun experiment.
