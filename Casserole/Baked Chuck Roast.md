## Ingredients

- 3–4 lb chuck roast
- 1 cup beef broth

*Not counted: olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, rosemary*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 325°F.
2. Pat chuck roast dry. Season generously on all sides with salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and paprika.
3. Heat a Dutch oven or oven-safe pot over high heat with a drizzle of olive oil. Sear roast 3–4 minutes per side until deep brown crust forms.
4. Add beef broth to the pot. Cover tightly with lid or foil.
5. Transfer to oven. Cook at 325°F for 3.5–4 hours until fork tender and falling apart.
6. Rest 10 minutes before slicing or shredding. Serve with pan juices.

## Notes

- The sear is what builds the flavor — don't skip it, even for a slow braise.
- Low and slow is the key: rushing this above 350°F makes the meat tough rather than tender.
- Pan juices make a natural gravy — pour them over the meat when serving.
- Leftovers work great in tacos, sandwiches, or over mashed potatoes the next night.
- Add carrots, potatoes, and onions to the pot for a complete one-pot meal.
