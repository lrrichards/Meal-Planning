## Ingredients

- Half a 5 lb bag russet potatoes, sliced thin
- ½ yellow onion, diced
- Half a bag (about 6 oz) frozen sweet peas
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup (cream of chicken or celery also works)
- 1 pork tenderloin, sliced into pork chop-thickness pieces
- Half a can of water

*Not counted: non-stick spray, salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika, Italian seasoning, beef stew seasoning packet*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Spray a 9×13 baking dish with non-stick spray.
2. Add sliced potatoes and diced onion to the dish. Toss together.
3. Add frozen peas, cream of mushroom soup, the remaining half of the beef stew seasoning packet, and half a can of water. Stir until the soup and seasoning are distributed over the potatoes.
4. Lay sliced pork tenderloin pieces over the top of the potato mixture in a single layer.
5. Season the pork with salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika, and Italian seasoning.
6. Cover tightly with foil.
7. Bake at 400°F for 50 minutes, or until potatoes are tender and pork is cooked through.
8. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Buy a whole pork tenderloin and slice it yourself — significantly cheaper per pound than pre-cut pork chops. Freeze leftover slices for another meal.
- Slice potatoes as thin as possible for fastest, most even cooking. A mandolin works if you have one.
- The beef stew seasoning packet adds a bold savory flavor to the potatoes — worth having.
- Cream of mushroom, cream of chicken, or cream of celery are all interchangeable here.
- The potato flavor caramelizes under the pork drippings — the bottom layer is the best bite.
