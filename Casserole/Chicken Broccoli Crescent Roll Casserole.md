## Ingredients

- 2–3 cups shredded chicken (rotisserie works great)
- 2 cups broccoli florets, chopped (fresh or frozen and thawed)
- 1½ cups shredded cheddar cheese (divided)
- ½ cup sour cream
- 2 cans (8 oz each) crescent roll dough
- 1 can (10.5 oz) cream of chicken soup
- ¼ cup milk

*Not counted: salt, pepper, garlic powder, non-stick spray*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Spray a 9x13 baking dish with non-stick spray.
2. In a large bowl, combine shredded chicken, broccoli, 1 cup cheddar cheese, sour cream, cream of chicken soup, and milk. Season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Mix well.
3. Unroll one can of crescent dough and press into the bottom of the baking dish, sealing any seams.
4. Spread the chicken and broccoli mixture evenly over the dough.
5. Unroll second can of crescent dough and lay over the top of the filling, stretching to cover.
6. Top with remaining ½ cup cheddar cheese.
7. Bake at 375°F for 25–30 minutes until crescent dough is golden brown and filling is bubbling.

## Notes

- Press crescent seams firmly — open seams allow filling to leak through.
- Frozen broccoli works fine — thaw and pat dry before mixing in.
- Let it rest 5 minutes before cutting so the filling sets up and slices cleanly.
- This is essentially a chicken broccoli pot pie in casserole form.
