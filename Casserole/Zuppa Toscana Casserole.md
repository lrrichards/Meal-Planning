## Ingredients

- 4 strips bacon, chopped
- 1 lb Italian sausage
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 3–4 russet potatoes, thinly sliced or cubed
- 2–3 cups kale, chopped
- ½ cup parmesan cheese, shredded

*Not counted: olive oil, salt, pepper, red pepper flakes, garlic powder*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease a 9x13 baking dish.
2. Cook bacon in a skillet over medium heat until crispy. Remove and set aside.
3. Brown sausage in the same skillet, breaking it up. Add diced onion and cook until softened.
4. Sprinkle flour over sausage and stir to coat. Pour in chicken broth and heavy cream. Stir until smooth and beginning to thicken.
5. Add potatoes, kale, and bacon. Season with salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes. Stir to combine.
6. Pour into prepared baking dish. Top with parmesan cheese.
7. Bake at 375°F for 35–40 minutes until potatoes are tender and top is golden.

## Notes

- Use hot Italian sausage for more heat, mild for a family-friendly version.
- Thin potato slices cook faster and more evenly — use a mandoline if available.
- Pairs well with crusty bread for dipping into the creamy sauce.
- This is a deconstructed version of the classic Zuppa Toscana soup — same flavors, casserole format.
- See also: Slow Cooker Zuppa Toscana Soup (CrockPot/ folder) for the slow cooker version.
