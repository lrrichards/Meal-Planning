## Ingredients

- 1 rotisserie chicken, pulled
- 8 oz egg noodles
- 2 carrots
- 1 onion, chopped
- 3 stalks celery
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 can chicken broth
- 2 cups heavy whipping cream
- 2 tbsp flour
- 1 small bag corn
- Butter
- For the topping: breadcrumbs, parmesan cheese, olive oil
- Salt, pepper, fresh parsley

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 375°F. In a hot skillet with butter, cook the chopped carrots, onion, celery, and garlic about 2–3 minutes until soft. Season with salt and pepper.
2. Boil the egg noodles for just 5 minutes, then drain.
3. To the vegetables, stir in 2 tbsp flour, then the chicken broth, then 2 cups heavy whipping cream. Bring to a boil and let it reduce a bit.
4. Stir in the pulled chicken, drained noodles, and corn (peas too, if your house likes them).
5. Pour into a baking dish.
6. Mix breadcrumbs, parmesan, and a little olive oil, then sprinkle over the top.
7. Bake 20–25 minutes until toasty. Garnish with chopped parsley.

## Notes

- All the chicken-pot-pie flavor in a baked noodle casserole, with a crunchy parmesan-breadcrumb top.
- Rotisserie chicken keeps it quick.
- Distinct from the stovetop Creamy Chicken and Egg Noodles — this is baked with pot-pie veg (celery, corn) and a crumb topping.
- Not keto (egg noodles).
- Cream-heavy (2 cups), so not tagged extreme-budget.
