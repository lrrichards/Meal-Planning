## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground pork (or ground turkey)
- 1 small onion, diced
- Sloppy joe sauce
- 2 lbs tater tots
- Shredded cheese

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 425°F. Dice the onion and sizzle it in a pan, then remove it.
2. Brown the ground pork, breaking it up. Soak up excess grease with a paper towel.
3. Add the onion back in and stir in the sloppy joe sauce. Mix well.
4. Spread the sloppy joe mixture in a baking dish and line the top with tater tots.
5. Bake at 425°F for 20–30 minutes, until the tots are crispy.
6. Top with shredded cheese (be generous) and return to the oven until melty.

## Notes

- Meat, cheese, potatoes, and sauce — a formula that always works.
- **Contains sloppy joe sauce (tomato-based) — not Evan-safe.**
- Distinct from Redneck Casserole (franks + baked beans) and Sloppy Joe Biscuit Balls (beef in biscuits).
- Not keto (tater tots + sauce).
- Walmart "poor man's comfort food."
