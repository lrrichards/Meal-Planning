## Ingredients

- 1 lb pasta
- 1 rotisserie chicken, pulled (a cheap way to add cooked chicken)
- 2 jars alfredo sauce
- 1/2 bag fresh spinach
- Shredded mozzarella cheese, for topping
- Garlic, salt, pepper, onion powder

## Instructions

1. Boil the pasta. Before draining, save some of the pasta water. Drain the pasta.
2. In the same pot, heat the alfredo sauce until bubbly. Stir in the spinach and let it wilt down.
3. Add the pasta and pulled chicken back in. Add a splash of the saved pasta water, plus garlic, salt, pepper, and onion powder. Stir.
4. Dump into a baking dish and top with shredded mozzarella.
5. Broil a few minutes until the cheese is melted and crispy on top (or bake if you prefer).

## Notes

- Fast, kid-friendly. Rotisserie chicken + jarred sauce keeps it cheap.
- Sneaks in spinach for some nutrition (it cooks way down).
- Not keto (pasta).
- Distinct from the rice and ramen alfredo bakes — this one is a baked pasta version with spinach.
