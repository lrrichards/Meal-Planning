## Ingredients

- 2 cans chili
- 1 box scalloped potatoes
- 1 bag mixed vegetables
- 2 cups water
- A sliver of butter
- 2/3 cup milk

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 450°F. Grease a baking dish.
2. Spread the 2 cans of chili and the mixed vegetables in the dish.
3. Make the scalloped potato sauce: boil 2 cups water, add a sliver of butter and the seasoning packet, then stir in 2/3 cup milk and the dried potato slices.
4. Layer the scalloped potatoes over the chili and vegetables.
5. Bake at 450°F for 25 minutes, until the top is golden brown and the potatoes are tender.
6. Let it rest 5 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- A follower (Opal) recipe — cheap and surprisingly good. Store-brand chili works great in place of Hormel.
- **Contains chili (tomato-based) — contains tomatoes.**
- Not keto (potatoes).
- "Poor man's comfort food" follower edition.
