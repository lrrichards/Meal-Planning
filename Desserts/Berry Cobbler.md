## Ingredients

- 4–5 cups mixed berries (fresh or frozen — blueberries, strawberries, raspberries)
- 1 cup quick oats
- ½ cup brown sugar
- 1 box (15.25 oz) French vanilla cake mix

*Not counted: butter (½ cup, melted), cinnamon*

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x13 baking dish.
2. Spread mixed berries in an even layer in the baking dish.
3. In a bowl, combine French vanilla cake mix, quick oats, and brown sugar. Mix well.
4. Sprinkle the dry cake mix mixture evenly over the berries — do not stir.
5. Drizzle melted butter evenly over the top of the dry mix.
6. Sprinkle with cinnamon.
7. Bake at 350°F for 40–45 minutes until topping is golden brown and berry juices are bubbling around the edges.
8. Serve warm with ice cream or whipped cream.

## Notes

- Do not stir — the layering is what makes a cobbler. Mixing creates a dense cake instead.
- Frozen berries work perfectly — no thawing needed, just add a few extra minutes of baking time.
- French vanilla cake mix gives the topping a rich, buttery flavor that plain yellow cake can't match.
- Serve warm — the contrast of hot cobbler and cold ice cream is the whole point.
