## Ingredients

- 7 tbsp butter (a little less than a stick)
- 1 can sweetened condensed milk
- 1 bag regular marshmallows
- 1 box Cocoa Pebbles
- 1/2 bag mini marshmallows
- 2 tbsp sprinkles

## Instructions

1. Melt the butter in a pot until it's browned and bubbly.
2. Add the sweetened condensed milk.
3. Dump in the full bag of regular marshmallows. Reduce heat to low and stir until nice and melty.
4. Stir in the whole box of Cocoa Pebbles until coated (this takes some muscle).
5. Stir in half a bag of mini marshmallows.
6. Press the mixture into a greased 9x13 baking dish. Top with sprinkles.
7. Let sit about 1 hour for the perfect balance of firm and gooey.

## Notes

- Like a chocolate rice krispie treat, upgraded with browned butter and sweetened condensed milk.
- No-bake — just sets on the counter.
- "Poor man's comfort food," holiday party dessert edition.
