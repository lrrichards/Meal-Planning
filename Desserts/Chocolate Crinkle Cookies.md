## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 cup cocoa powder
- 1 cup vegetable oil
- 1 cup granulated sugar (plus extra for rolling)
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 tbsp real vanilla
- 4 eggs
- Powdered sugar, for rolling

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, whisk together the flour, salt, and baking powder. Set aside.
2. In a separate large bowl, mix the oil, cocoa powder, granulated sugar, and brown sugar. Add the vanilla and beat until well combined.
3. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing after each.
4. Slowly add the dry ingredients to the wet, mixing slowly — don't rush it.
5. Wrap the dough in plastic wrap and refrigerate at least 3 hours, preferably overnight.
6. Heat oven to 350°F. Scoop the dough into balls. Roll each ball first in granulated sugar, then in powdered sugar.
7. Place on cookie sheets and bake 12 minutes.
8. Let cool about 10 minutes, then move to a cooling rack to cool fully.

## Notes

- The powdered-sugar coating cracks as they bake — that's the "crinkle."
- Baking is a science here: measure, don't eyeball.
- "Poor man's comfort food" dessert edition.
