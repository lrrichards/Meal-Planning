## Ingredients

- 1 chocolate pie crust (pre-made)
- 2 packets Dream Whip topping mix (not all 4)
- 1 large box instant chocolate pudding
- 2.75 cups milk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. In a mixing bowl, add the 2 Dream Whip packets, a little vanilla, and 1 cup of milk.
2. Beat on high for 6 minutes, until fluffy.
3. Add the instant chocolate pudding mix and an additional 1 3/4 cups milk. Beat on medium-high for 2 minutes.
4. Spoon the fluffy filling into the chocolate pie crust and smooth the top with a spatula.
5. Refrigerate at least 4 hours before serving.

## Notes

- No-bake — the fridge sets it.
- "Pie day" poor man's comfort food dessert.
