## Ingredients

- 1 box graham crackers
- 1 box chocolate cake mix
- 1 cup chocolate chips
- 3 cups marshmallows
- Butter

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 350°F.
2. Line the bottom of a baking dish with graham crackers and smash them down.
3. Sprinkle the dry chocolate cake mix evenly on top.
4. Sprinkle on the chocolate chips, then the marshmallows, spreading evenly.
5. Slice the butter thin and lay the slices over the top.
6. Bake at 350°F for 25–30 minutes until bubbly and toasted.

## Notes

- Ooey and gooey like a s'more.
- In the source it came out underdone toward the edges — a classic dump cake usually needs the liquid the cake mix calls for (or more butter coverage) to bake through evenly. Add that if your edges aren't setting.
- Dollar Tree "poor man's comfort food," dessert edition.
