## Ingredients

**Crust**
- 20 Golden Oreos, crushed
- 4 tbsp butter, melted

**Filling**
- 2 blocks (8 oz each) cream cheese, softened
- 1.5 cups powdered sugar
- 2 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 8 oz Cool Whip
- 3/4 cup fresh strawberries, mashed

**Topping**
- 2 cups golden oreo
- 1 oz freeze-dried strawberry
- 1/4 cup butter

## Instructions

1. Crush 20 Golden Oreos in a bag. Mix in melted butter until combined.
2. Line an 8x8 baking dish with wax paper. Press the Oreo crust evenly into the bottom.
3. Place in the freezer to chill while you make the filling.
4. In a large bowl, beat together cream cheese, powdered sugar, lemon juice, and vanilla extract until smooth.
5. Fold in Cool Whip until fully combined.
6. Mash strawberries and stir into the filling.
7. Pull the crust from the freezer. Spread the filling evenly over the crust.
8. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours before serving.
9. Make the topping by mixing crushed Golden Oreos, freeze-dried strawberries, and a little melted butter together.
10. Sprinkle topping over the cheesecake right before serving.

## Notes

- Add the topping just before serving so it stays crunchy.
- Tastes like a strawberry shortcake popsicle.
- No oven needed — great for warm weather.
